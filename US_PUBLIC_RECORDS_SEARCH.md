# US Public Records Search — Van Duynhoven Family (Minnesota)

**Search date:** 2026-08-28
**Method:** Open web search + web fetch of public-record aggregators, obituary archives, county sites, and diocesan/parish records.
**Scope:** Blue Earth, Sibley (Green Isle / Arlington), and Nicollet counties; named individuals Morris Van Duynhoven (b. 1951) and Peter Van Duynhoven (b. 1956).

> **Reliability note:** Several primary aggregators (veripages.com, legacy.com) block automated fetching and returned HTTP 403. Their **search-index snippets are quoted below** but the full record pages could not be opened programmatically. Anything marked *[snippet only]* should be confirmed by opening the URL manually. No paywalled genealogy databases (Ancestry, GenealogyBank) were accessed — only their public landing snippets.

---

## Summary of what was found

- The surname **Van Duynhoven / Vanduynhoven does have a documented Minnesota presence** in people-search aggregators, but the confirmed hits are for **other given names** (Charles, William), not the two target individuals.
- **No record was found tying any Van Duynhoven to Green Isle / Arlington (Sibley County), St. Brendan's Catholic Church, Blue Earth County, or Nicollet County.** This is a meaningful *negative* finding — see the Kolden Funeral Home and St. Brendan sections.
- **Neither "Morris Van Duynhoven 1951" nor "Peter Van Duynhoven 1956" surfaced in Minnesota records.** The only Peter Van Duynhoven located is in **Florida**, not Minnesota (details below).

## 5. Sibley County — Green Isle / Arlington

**Negative finding, and a fairly strong one.** The funeral home that serves Green Isle and Arlington is **Kolden Funeral Home (Arlington, MN)**. Its obituary archive is publicly searchable and was queried directly.

- A search of Kolden Funeral Home obituaries returned **many Green Isle / Arlington families** (Kreger, Dressen, Alsleben, Sommers, Schwirtz, Woods, Schauer, Kube, Ryan, Duenow, Rose) — but **zero Van Duynhoven entries.**
- If a Van Duynhoven family had been long-established in Green Isle/Arlington, this is the archive one would expect them to appear in. Their absence suggests **no significant recent Van Duynhoven presence in the Green Isle/Arlington area** — or at least none captured in Kolden's records.
- Reference: https://www.koldenfuneralhome.com/obituaries

## 7. St. Brendan's Catholic Church, Green Isle

Confirmed the church exists and its status, but **no parishioner/sacramental record for Van Duynhoven was accessible** (parish sacramental registers are not published online):

- **Church of St. Brendan, 221 McGrann St., Green Isle, MN** — Diocese of New Ulm. https://www.dnu.org/greenisle
- **The parish closed:** *"Easily described as the 'crown jewel' of Green Isle, the Church of St. Brendan held its last Sunday Mass at 8 a.m. on May 30[, 2021] after 158 years..."* — Prairie Catholic (Diocese of New Ulm), June 2021. https://issuu.com/dnu.com/docs/june_2021_prairie_catholic_for_website
- Implication for records: the parish operated ~1863–2021. Historical sacramental registers (baptism/marriage/burial) would now be held by the **Diocese of New Ulm** and are **not searchable online** — they would require a direct written request to the diocesan archives. That is the correct next channel for any St. Brendan's Van Duynhoven church record.
