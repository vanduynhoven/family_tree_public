## Interpretation for Dutch-heritage / residency research
- If the Minnesota connection is a working hypothesis, current obituary/newspaper evidence does **not** support a Van Duynhoven family presence in Green Isle, Arlington, or Mankato, MN.
- The strongest documented American branches are **Warwick, NY** (immigrated 1954 from Amsterdam) and **Little Chute/Kaukauna, WI** (older Fox Valley Dutch-Catholic settlement, "Van Dyn Hoven" spelling).
- Recommended next steps if pursuing Minnesota specifically: search MN county cemetery indexes and church records directly under spelling variants (Van Duijnhoven, Van Dyn Hoven, Van Duinhoven, Duynhoven); check the Minnesota Historical Society and county historical society newspaper archives, which are not fully indexed by general web search.
