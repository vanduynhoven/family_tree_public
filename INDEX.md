## Summary

This collection documents the Van Duynhoven family history spanning four generations, from the marriage of Marianus van Duynhoven and Anna Maria van den Elzen in 1906 (Boekel, Netherlands) through the emigration of their son Johan to the United States in 1950 and his naturalization as a U.S. citizen in 1956.

The family name appears in several spellings across documents:
- Van Duynhoven
- van Duynhoven
- Van Duijnhoven
- van Duijnhoven

These are all the same family - the y/ij variation is common in Dutch names.

## For Future Research

The documents suggest several avenues for additional research:

1. **Death dates for deceased children** - The letter to Carolyn mentions offering to look up death dates
2. **Ship manifest records** - Southampton departure records for the transatlantic voyage
3. **Ellis Island / port of entry records** - U.S. arrival documentation
4. **Land/property records** - Green Isle, Minnesota settlement
5. **St. Brendan's Catholic Church records** - Additional sacramental records
6. **Netherlands civil registry** - Birth certificates, additional family records
7. **North Brabant provincial archives** - Original marriage and birth certificates
