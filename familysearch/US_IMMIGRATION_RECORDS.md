## Research target

Johan van Duynhoven (Dutch: van Duijnhoven) and family — emigrated Netherlands Nov 17–23, 1950; route Rotterdam → Harwich → Southampton → New York; naturalized June 5, 1956 in Mankato (Blue Earth County), Minnesota; certificates 7468860 (Anna) and 7468861 (Johan); resided Green Isle → Arlington → Mankato, MN.

## Headline finding — the anglicized spelling

The family's surname was anglicized from **van Duijnhoven** (Dutch `ij`) to **van Duynhoven / Van Duynhoven / Vanduynhoven** (`y`) in US records. **Searching under `van Duynhoven` — not the Dutch `Duijnhoven` — is what surfaces the US records.** The Dutch `Duijnhoven` spelling returns almost exclusively Netherlands vital/population records.

## 3. US Census (1950s–1960s)

**1950 US Census (collectionId 4464515), Minnesota:** **No van Duynhoven / van Duijnhoven found.** ✅ (expected)

Only phonetic near-misses appear (Stainhoven, Welhaven, Aphoven). **This is a corroborating negative:** the 1950 US Census was enumerated as of **April 1, 1950**, when the family was still in the Netherlands (they emigrated November 1950). Their absence from the 1950 census is exactly consistent with the emigration timeline.

- **1960 US Census:** not publicly released — the US 72-year privacy rule means the 1960 census will not open until **2032**. It cannot be searched on FamilySearch yet.
- For the 1950s–60s Minnesota residency (Green Isle → Arlington → Mankato), the usable substitute record classes on FamilySearch are the **Minnesota Death Index / County Deaths** and the **US Public Records / Social Security Death Index** entries found below, plus the naturalization card (which records residence).

## Search method notes (for reproducing / finishing)

- **Always search the anglicized `van Duynhoven` for US records**; use `van Duijnhoven` for Netherlands records.
- Pin searches to a specific collection with `&f.collectionId=<id>` in the results URL — the generic "all collections" search fuzzy-matched unrelated surnames (Van Houdt, Van Duivenbode, etc.) and a stale session filter kept reverting broad queries to Netherlands vital records.
- Useful collection IDs:
  - `1923888` — New York, New York Passenger and Crew Lists, 1909, 1925–1958 (immigration / arrivals)
  - `1927189` — Minnesota, County Naturalization Records, 1854–1957 (family NOT here)
  - `4464515` — United States, Census, 1950
  - Minnesota, Naturalization Card Index, 1930–1988 — search by name; **contains the Johan naturalization hit** (look up its collection ID from the result to bookmark).
