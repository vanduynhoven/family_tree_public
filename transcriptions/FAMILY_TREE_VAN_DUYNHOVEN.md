## Geographic Origins

### Netherlands Locations
- **Uden** - Birthplace of Marianus (1872), Johan (1915), and 5 other children
- **Boekel** - Marriage location (1906), birthplace of Anna Maria v.d. Elzen, and first 6 children
- **Bakel en Milheeze** - Johan's marriage location (1950), Anna Maria Cornelissen's birthplace
- **'s-Hertogenbosch (Den Bosch)** - Passport issued, diocese seat
- **Rotterdam** - U.S. visa issued (American Vice Consulate)
- **'s-Gravenhage (The Hague)** - Emigration exit point

### USA Locations
- **Green Isle, Minnesota** - Settlement location, church parish
- **Arlington, Minnesota** - Birthplace of all four American-born children
- **Mankato, Minnesota** - Naturalization court location
- **Chaska, Minnesota** - Death location of Morris Wilhelm Peter (2014)

## Religious Affiliation

The family maintained strong Catholic faith across generations:
- Marriage ceremonies performed in Catholic churches
- Diocese of Buscodue ('s-Hertogenbosch)
- St. Brendan's Catholic Church, Green Isle, Minnesota
- All children baptized, received First Communion
