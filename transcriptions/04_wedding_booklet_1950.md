## Summary

This wedding booklet documents the marriage of Johan van Duijnhoven (son of Marianus and Anna Maria van den Elzen) to Anna Maria Cornelissen in 1950. The civil ceremony was held on October 5, 1950, in Bakel en Milheeze, and the church wedding followed on November 28, 1950, at Heilige Willibrord church in Milheeze.

The couple emigrated to the United States shortly after their marriage and settled in Green Isle, Minnesota, where they had four children between 1951 and 1956. All children were baptized at St. Brendan's Catholic Church in Green Isle.
