## Key Discoveries from These Documents

### 1. Complete Sibling Birth Dates
All 12 children of Marianus and Anna Maria van den Elzen now have official birth records:
- Born in Boekel: 1907, 1908, 1909, 1911, 1912, 1913
- Born in Uden: 1914, 1915 (Johan), 1916, 1919, 1920, 1923

### 2. Family Migration Pattern
The family moved from **Boekel → Uden** between September 1913 and September 1914.

### 3. Anna Maria van den Elzen Death Confirmed
Died December 21, 1952 in Uden — 2 years after Johan emigrated.

### 4. Marianus's Birth Record
Born April 24, 1872 in Uden — confirms Johan's grandfather's names are on this document.

### 5. Four Sibling Marriages Documented
Shows Johan's brothers and sisters marrying between 1932-1948, all staying in the Netherlands.
