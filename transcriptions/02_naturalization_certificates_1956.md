### Note on Anna's Birth Year Discrepancy
The naturalization certificate shows Anna's birth date as September 30, 1916, but other family documents indicate Anna Maria Cornelissen was born September 30, 1916 in Bakel en Milheeze. This is Johan's wife (married October 5, 1950), not to be confused with Anna Maria van den Elzen (Johan's mother, born 1878).
