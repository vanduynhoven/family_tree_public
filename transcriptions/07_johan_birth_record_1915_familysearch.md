## Significance

This birth record confirms:

1. **Johan's exact birth date and place** — November 12, 1915, in Uden, Noord-Brabant
2. **His parents' names** — Marianus van Duijnhoven and Anna Maria van den Elzen
3. **The official spelling** — "van Duijnhoven" with the Dutch "ij" digraph (later Americanized to "van Duynhoven")

This is the same Johan who:
- Married Anna Maria Cornelissen in 1950
- Emigrated to the United States in November 1950
- Settled in Green Isle, Minnesota
- Became a U.S. citizen on June 5, 1956 (Certificate No. 7468861)
