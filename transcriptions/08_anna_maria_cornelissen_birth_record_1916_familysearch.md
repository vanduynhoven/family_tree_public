## Significance

This birth record confirms:

1. **Anna Maria's exact birth date** — September 30, 1916 (NOT 1918 as incorrectly recorded on her U.S. naturalization certificate)
2. **Her birthplace** — Bakel en Milheeze, Noord-Brabant (the same municipality where she later married Johan)
3. **Her parents' names** — Wilhelmus Cornelissen and Anna Maria Verbruggen
4. **Resolves the age discrepancy** — The U.S. naturalization certificate (No. 7468862) lists her birth year as 1918, but this official Dutch record proves she was born in **1916**

This is the same Anna Maria who:
- Married Johan van Duijnhoven on October 5, 1950 in Bakel en Milheeze (her birth municipality)
- Emigrated to the United States in November 1950
- Became a U.S. citizen on June 5, 1956 (Certificate No. 7468862)
