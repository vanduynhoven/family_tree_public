# Public Family Tree Search — Van Duynhoven Descendants

_Search date: 2026-08-28_
_Goal: locate public trees including Johan/John Van Duynhoven, his children, grandchildren, their spouses, and any photos/documents (esp. Minnesota)._

## Summary

**No public tree tying a Johan/John Van Duynhoven to Minnesota was found on any of the three target sites.** The surname exists in genealogy databases, but every hit that could be read without a paid login was a **European (Dutch/Belgian/French) historical record**, not a U.S./Minnesota descendant line. Ancestry and MyHeritage gate their public trees and record indexes behind login, so those layers could not be fully inspected anonymously.

## Notes & next steps

- **Spelling variants matter.** Geni indexes the family under **van Duijnhoven / van Duynhove / Duynhoven** as well as **van Duynhoven**. Any follow-up search should include all variants (`Duijnhoven`, `Duynhove`, `Duijnhove`).
- **Login walls are the main blocker.** Ancestry public member trees and MyHeritage SuperSearch matches are the two most likely places to find a Minnesota descendant tree, and both require an authenticated session to read. Recommend re-running these searches from a logged-in Ancestry/MyHeritage account (or via an attached, logged-in browser session).
- **The Dutch profiles on Geni are a viable ancestry anchor** — if Johan/John emigrated from the Netherlands, connecting him to one of these documented Dutch lines (e.g., the van Duijnhoven cluster) is the most promising bridge back to the origin family.
- Consider also: FamilySearch (free, but the auto-navigated 1950–1957 Minnesota query returned 0 records — worth a manual, broader-date search when logged in), and WikiTree.
