# Van Duynhoven — Other US States Branch

Descendants and relatives named **Van Duynhoven** located in US states *beyond*
the three primary clusters already documented (Minnesota, Wisconsin, New York).
States covered here: **Florida, Arizona, Ohio, New Jersey**, with negative-result
notes for **California** and **Texas**.

> Research date: 2026-08-29. Sources are public obituaries, parish directories,
> and genealogy indexes. Living-person details are limited to what is already
> publicly published.

---

## Key connecting document: Joana Van Duynhoven obituary (Warwick, NY, d. 2017)

The single most important source for this branch is the obituary of **Joana Sophia
Van Duynhoven** (née van Blokland), a long-time Warwick, NY resident who died
2017-05-20 at age 92 (b. 1925-01-15, Amsterdam, Holland; immigrated to America
1954). She is the matriarch whose descendants spread across Ohio, Arizona, New
York, and Florida. This obituary ties the multi-state network together.

- **Husband:** John Van Duynhoven (d. 1986) — the emigrant couple who settled in
  the Warwick, NY area and raised their family there.
- Source: Lazear-Smith & Vander Plaat Memorial Home,
  `https://lsvpmemorialhome.com/obituary/88edu`

The Warwick/Bellvale, NY nucleus is documented separately in
`NEW_YORK_WARWICK_BRANCH.md`; this file follows the descendants who left NY.

## OHIO — Pickerington ("Pickering, OH")

Confirmed descendants via the Joana Van Duynhoven obituary:

- **John Van Duynhoven** and wife **Paula** — of Pickering (Pickerington), OH.
  Listed as a surviving *son* of Joana.
- **John Van Duynhoven** (the younger) — of Pickering (Pickerington), OH. Listed
  as a surviving *grandson* of Joana (son of John & Paula, same household town).

Note: the obituary spells the town "Pickering, OH"; there is no incorporated
"Pickering" in Ohio — this is almost certainly **Pickerington, OH** (a Columbus
suburb in Fairfield/Franklin counties). No independent Van Duynhoven record was
found in Pickerington public sources beyond the obituary, so treat the exact
address as unconfirmed.

**Status:** CONFIRMED family branch in Ohio (two generations).
