# Van Duynhoven — New York (Warwick / Orange County) Branch

**Compiled:** 2026-08-28
**Status:** Documented from published obituaries and genealogy sources. **Relationship to Arthur van Duynhoven's Uden line: NOT established — almost certainly a SEPARATE family line** (see "Relationship to the Uden line" below).

---

## Executive Summary

A cluster of Van Duynhoven / van Duynhoven / VanDuynhoven family members settled in **Warwick, Orange County, New York** (villages of Warwick and Bellvale) beginning in the 1950s. They are Dutch Catholic immigrants, but their Netherlands origin is **Halfweg (near Amsterdam, province of Noord-Holland)** — *not* Uden/Boekel in Noord-Brabant, where Arthur's confirmed ancestral line originates. The two lines are therefore very likely **unrelated by recent descent**, despite the shared surname. The Warwick family are parishioners of **St. Stephen the Martyr RC Church, Warwick**, and buried in **St. Stephen's Cemetery**.

There appear to be **two intertwined but distinct sub-families** in Warwick:
1. **The Halfweg family** — children of John van Duynhoven & Marie Tol (Joseph, John, Peter, Jacoba, Engel, Jake). Confirmed siblings.
2. **The "John & Joana" family** — Joana Sophia van Blokland m. John Van Duynhoven (d. 1986). John's exact link to the Halfweg siblings is **not confirmed in the sources** (he may be the sibling "John" listed as predeceasing Joseph — plausible but not proven).

## Relationship to the Uden line (Arthur's ancestry)

**Conclusion: These are almost certainly two DIFFERENT Van Duynhoven families.**

| Dimension | Warwick NY branch | Arthur's line (Uden) |
|---|---|---|
| NL origin | **Halfweg / Amsterdam (Noord-Holland)** | **Uden / Boekel (Noord-Brabant)** |
| Spelling | van Duynhoven / VanDuynhoven (**y**) | van Duijnhoven (**ij**) in NL records |
| Emigration | ~1954 (Joana), post-WWII | **Nov 1950** (Johan & Anna → Green Isle, MN) |
| US settlement | Warwick, Orange County, **NY** | Green Isle / Arlington, **MN** |
| Religion | Dutch Catholic (St. Stephen, Warwick) | Dutch Catholic (St. Brendan's, Green Isle) |
| Documented common ancestor | **None found** | — |

The surname "van Duijnhoven / van Duynhoven" is toponymic (from *Duinhoven*) and is borne by multiple unrelated Dutch families. The Warwick family's roots in **Halfweg (Noord-Holland)** are geographically ~120 km from the Brabant villages (Uden, Boekel, Sambeek, Zeeland) where Arthur's ancestors and other documented "van Duijnhoven" clusters lived. Absent a shared 18th–19th-century Brabant ancestor, they should be treated as **a distinct line** until a documentary link is found.

**To disprove/confirm a connection**, the decisive record would be Joseph's father **John van Duynhoven's** own birth/baptism in the Netherlands (to see whether that John traces back to Brabant or is purely Noord-Holland). No such record was located in this search.

## Open Questions / To Verify

1. **Peter's identity & age.** Joseph's 2019 obituary lists a *predeceased* brother "Peter." But a **Peter Van Duynhoven, 83, of Welaka FL died 20 Jun 2026** (b. ~1943). A 1943 birth is 15 years after Joseph (1928), and this Peter was clearly alive in 2019 — so the 2026 Peter is likely a **different Peter** (a nephew/cousin, possibly son of one of the siblings), NOT the brother who predeceased Joseph. Confirm via the full Palatka Daily News obituary (legacy.com id 61819031 — currently 403-blocked to automated fetch).
2. **Is John (d.1986, Joana's husband) the sibling "John"?** Plausible — same surname, same Warwick community, same parish — but the sources do not state it. Needs a marriage or census record.
3. **Marie Tol's** family — the "van Tol / Tolhuijs" genealogy on genealogieonline.nl covers a different (South Holland) Tol line; no confirmed link to this Marie Tol of Halfweg.
4. **FindAGrave** memorials for St. Stephen's Cemetery, Warwick were not individually confirmed (search returned the cemetery index, not specific memorials). Worth a manual FindAGrave search for burial dates/photos.
