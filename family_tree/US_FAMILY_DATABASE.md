# Van Duynhoven — US Family Database

**Compiled:** 2026-08-28 via web search (obituaries, funeral homes, news, public records)
**Scope:** All Van Duynhoven / VanDuynhoven / Van Dyn Hoven / Vanduynhoven individuals in the United States, plus first-degree relatives (spouses, children, grandchildren, in-laws) named in the same records.

> **Surname spellings vary by branch.** The three known US branches use distinct spellings:
> - **Minnesota** (Arthur's line): `Van Duynhoven` (y)
> - **New York** (Warwick/Bellvale/Wallkill): `van Duynhoven` / `VanDuynhoven`
> - **Wisconsin** (Fox Valley — Little Chute/Kaukauna/Appleton): `Van Dyn Hoven` (three words)
>
> **⚠️ Relationship between the three branches is NOT yet confirmed.** All three trace to Noord-Brabant/Dutch Catholic origins, but no document yet links the NY or WI branches to Arthur's Uden/Boekel line. The WI "Van Dyn Hoven" spelling in particular may be an independent immigration.
