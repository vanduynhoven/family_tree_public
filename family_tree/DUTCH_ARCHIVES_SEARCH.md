# Dutch Genealogy Archives — Van Duijnhoven Collateral Branches

**Search date:** 2026-08-29
**Archives searched:** Open Archieven (openarchieven.nl), Brabants Historisch
Informatie Centrum (BHIC), Genealogie Online, DTB church registers (via Open Archieven).
**Focus:** New Van Duijnhoven records in **Uden, Boekel, Schaijk** and the surrounding
eastern Meierij, and how the **collateral branches connect to the main paternal line**.

**Main line (for reference — see `EARLY_ANCESTORS_1600s_1700s.md`):**

```
Gen −3  Petrus van Duijnhoven                (inferred, ~1700-1710)
Gen −2  Joannes Petri van Duijnhoven × Aldegundis Jansen   (Uden, children 1757-61)
Gen −1  Petrus Joannis van Duijnhoven × Elisabeth Henrici van Boxtel  (m. 21 Nov 1796 Uden)
Gen  0  Petrus van Duijnhoven (bapt. 27 Aug 1799 Uden) × Johanna van der Heijden
Gen  1  Martinus van Duijnhoven (1829-1903) × Geertruda Verwegen
        … continues to the US-emigrant generations …
```

**Spelling:** Dutch registers use **van Duijnhoven** (ij); the US branch spells it
**van Duynhoven** (y). Both were searched.

### ★ Key connective record — the 1830 Uden marriage

**Huwelijk 14 februari 1830, Uden — akte nr. 5** (BHIC deel 8033):

| Role | Person |
|---|---|
| **Groom** | **Henricus van Duijnhoven**, born **9 October 1798, Uden** |
| Groom's father | **Peter van Duijnhoven** |
| Groom's mother | **Maria Anna de Groot** |
| Bride | Petronella van der Voort, born 19 Feb 1796, Uden |
| Bride's parents | Theodorus van der Voort × Arnolda Bevers |

**Why this matters — direct link to the main-line base couple.** The main-line
patriarch **Petrus Joannis van Duijnhoven** married, on **17 February 1794 in Uden**,
**Maria Anna Francisci de Groot** (recorded in `EARLY_ANCESTORS_1600s_1700s.md`, record
bhi:fcd1e555-4423-a236-d21d-3f3550a4f9d2) — *before* his 1796 marriage to Elisabeth van
Boxtel. This 1830 marriage names the groom's parents as **"Peter van Duijnhoven × Maria
Anna de Groot"** and gives the groom's birth as **9 Oct 1798 Uden**.

→ **Inference (high confidence):** Henricus van Duijnhoven (b. 1798) is a **son of Petrus
Joannis van Duijnhoven by his de Groot marriage**, i.e. a **half-brother of the main-line
Gen-0 Petrus** (bapt. 27 Aug 1799 Uden, by the van Boxtel marriage). The two boys were
born barely a year apart in Uden to the same father by successive wives. This makes the
**Henricus/van der Voort branch a first collateral line off the main trunk at Gen −1.**
*Caveat:* confirm by pulling Henricus's 1798 baptism and the marriage bijlagen (available
via FamilySearch: "Uden, Huwelijksbijlagen 1830-1831").

## 2. BHIC Noord-Brabant — Schaijk & Boekel civil registration

### Schaijk — the "Anselmus van Duijnhoven" cluster (a distinct branch)

Genealogie Online (Van de Ven tree) and Open Archieven document a Schaijk Van Duijnhoven
family that is **separate from the Uden main line** but shares the eastern-Meierij origin:

- **Martinus van Duijnhoven** (b. Nov 1795, d. 1814) — "a child of **Anselmus van
  Duijnhoven and Theodora de Krey (Kraij)**."
  (genealogieonline.nl/stamboom-van-de-ven/I1126.php)
- A Schaijk land-sale deed names siblings **Martinus** (m. Emerentia Swaans), **Antonius**
  (m. Catharina Swaans) van Duijnhoven, plus Swaans and Schuts in-laws — all farmers
  (*landbouwers*) at Schaijk. → an **Anselmus van Duijnhoven → sons at Schaijk** branch.
- **Bevolkingsregister Schaijk 1920-1932** (BHIC archief 7626, deel 1115, wijk A folio 78)
  lists a whole 20th-c. Schaijk household — bhi:1dff7c93-50f7-c660-fe88-052c9f630af1:
  - Hendrikus Johannes van Duijnhoven (b. 1877), Anna Maria van Duijnhoven (b. 1888),
    and children **Maria Cornelia (1915), Johanna Catharina (1916), Maria Petronella
    (1918), Petronella Theodora (1921)** van Duijnhoven; in-law Johannes Marinus Verstegen.
  - Hendrikus Johannes (b. 19 Nov 1877): bhi:3bb9502a-5b2f-5f32-26ab-d7ddfa099185.
  - Johanna Maria van Duijnhoven, b. 17 Dec 1896: bhi:49c3f7e9-ff27-4777-3271-6a90f9ce6ef2.

### The Schaijk "Petrus van Duijnhoven 1757-1831" line (Van de Ven tree)

**Petrus van Duijnhoven**, bapt. **17 Jan 1757 Gemert**, *bouwman* (farmer), d. **11 May
1831 Schaijk** (genealogieonline.nl/stamboom-van-de-ven/I1133.php). Parents **Petrus
Peters van Duijnhoven (1719-1758) × Mechtildis Peters Henricks van Gemert (1723-1763)**;
grandparents **Petrus Hendrickx van Duijnhoven (±1695) × Maria Joosten (1692-1724)**.
Married (1) Anna Maria Theodorusse (Hermens), (2) Francina Smits; children incl. Leonardus
(1793), Theodorus (1798-1849), **Martin (1811-1835 Schaijk)**.

→ This is a **Gemert-origin branch that migrated to Schaijk**, *not* the Uden trunk.
The tree author's own notes flag confusion between "two Petrus van Duijnhoven" and missing
Schaijk marriage records 1782-1792 — treat the Gemert/Schaijk line as a **parallel
collateral cluster** sharing the deeper Gemert ancestry, not proven to descend from the
Uden Gen −2 couple.

### Boekel

- **Marriage 11 October 1821, Boekel** — bhi:3e46d2c6-0f3c-40ef-76bc-ee8246a798c1:
  **Henrica Andries van Duijnhoven** × Zeger Waltherus van Lieshout; the record names
  **Andries van Duijnhoven** (× Maria Lamerts Verkuijlen) as her father and Waltherus
  Zegers × Johanna van den Broek as the groom's parents. → an **"Andries van Duijnhoven"
  branch seated at Boekel**, distinct from the Uden and Schaijk clusters.

## 3. Genealogie Online trees

- **Stamboom Van de Ven - Goossens** (author: Martin van de Ven) — the richest external
  Van Duijnhoven source found. Covers the **Gemert → Schaijk** branch in depth (Petrus
  1757-1831 and descendants) and pushes that branch's ancestry to **Petrus Hendrickx van
  Duijnhoven (±1695)** and **Maria Joosten (1692-1724)** in the Gemert area.
  Person IDs used: I1124 (Martin 1811-1835), I1133 (Petrus 1757-1831), I1126 (Martinus
  1795-1814), I1150 (Godefridus b.1735), I1159 (Willibrordus b.1726).
- **Stamboom van de familie Van den Eijnde(n) uit Gemert en Haarlem** — cross-references
  the same Gemert Van Duijnhoven/van Gemert families (Aarle-Rixtel marriages ~1567 era).
- **Derks Vroomans Project** — also lists Petrus (1757-1831), confirming cross-tree
  agreement on the Gemert/Schaijk line.

These trees establish that the **Gemert cluster is the probable ancestral pool** feeding
both the Schaijk branch and, per `EARLY_ANCESTORS_1600s_1700s.md`, likely the Uden trunk
(Gen −3 Petrus, ~1700-1710) as well — a common Gemert/Aarle-Rixtel origin ~1660s-1740s.

## 5. How the branches connect — summary map

```
                     Gemert / Aarle-Rixtel Van Duijnhoven pool  (1660s-1740s)
                                       │
              ┌────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────┐
              │                        │                               │
   Gen −3 Petrus (~1700-10)   Petrus Hendrickx (±1695)          Andries van Duijnhoven
      → UDEN trunk            → Petrus Peters (1719-58)          → BOEKEL branch
              │                → Petrus (1757-1831, bapt Gemert)   (Henrica m. 1821 Boekel)
   Gen −2 Joannes Petri        → migrated to SCHAIJK
      × Aldegundis Jansen        (Van de Ven tree; sons Leonardus,
              │                   Theodorus, Martin 1811-35)
   Gen −1 Petrus Joannis  ──── m.(1794) Maria Anna de Groot ──┐
      × (2) Elisabeth van Boxtel                              │

   Gen 0 Petrus (bapt 1799)                        m. 1830 Uden Petronella van der Voort
      × Johanna van der Heijden                    → UDEN collateral half-brother line
              │
   Gen 1 Martinus (1829-1903)  … → US emigrants
```

### Confidence
- **CONFIRMED (record-backed):** the 1830 Uden marriage of Henricus (parents Peter ×
  Maria Anna de Groot); the Schaijk 1920-32 bevolkingsregister household; the Boekel 1821
  marriage; the Gemert→Schaijk Petrus (1757-1831) line and its Gemert ancestry.
- **STRONG INFERENCE:** Henricus (b.1798) is a **half-brother of the main-line Gen-0
  Petrus** via their shared father Petrus Joannis and his 1794 de Groot marriage. Verify
  with Henricus's 1798 Uden baptism + the 1830 huwelijksbijlagen (FamilySearch).
- **OPEN / PARALLEL (shared deep origin, link not yet proven):** the Schaijk/Gemert
  (Anselmus and Petrus-1757) clusters and the Boekel (Andries) cluster share the Gemert
  ancestral pool but are **not yet tied to the Uden Gen −2 couple** by a direct record.
