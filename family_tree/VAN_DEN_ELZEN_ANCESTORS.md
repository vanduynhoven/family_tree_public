# Van den Elzen Ancestor Line

Maternal line of **Anna Maria van den Elzen** (1878–1952), who married Marianus
van Duijnhoven on 18 May 1906 in Boekel. All records below were retrieved from the
**Open Archieven** public API (`api.openarchieven.nl`), sourced from the **Brabants
Historisch Informatie Centrum (BHIC)** civil registry (`bhi:` archive).

> **Key correction:** Anna Maria was born in **Boekel**, not Uden. Her family lived
> in Boekel; the Uden connection comes later through her husband Marianus van
> Duijnhoven (born Uden). Her mother's given name is recorded as **Francijna** (not
> "Francyna") van der Horst.

## What exists vs. what is missing

### Confirmed / retrieved
- ✅ 1906 marriage of Anna Maria × Marianus (bride's parents named)
- ✅ 1868 marriage of the parents Johannes × Francijna (both grandparent couples named)
- ✅ All 9 birth acts of the children (each parent-verified), including Anna Maria's
- ✅ Death of father Johannes van den Elzen (1911, Boekel)
- ✅ Death of mother Francijna van der Horst (1885, Boekel — identity confirmed via her parents)

### Missing / not yet located
- ❌ **Birth records of the parents** Johannes van den Elzen and Francijna van der Horst.
  Marrying in 1868, they were likely born ~1840s. Boekel/BHIC birth indexes for that
  window exist but were not surfaced by the API's fuzzy name search (see note). Look
  under BHIC Boekel `BS Geboorte` ~1840–1848, or derive exact ages from the 1868
  marriage act's full scan.
- ❌ **Death/marriage records of the 9 siblings** (only births captured here).
- ❌ **Grandparent-generation (Gen 2) vital records** — pre-1811 events fall under
  **DTB church registers** (Doop-, Trouw- en Begraafboeken), not civil registry.
- ❌ **FamilySearch IDs** — none of these van den Elzen individuals were matched to a
  FamilySearch ARK/tree ID in this search (Open Archieven does not expose FamilySearch
  IDs; a separate FamilySearch lookup, which requires login, would be needed).

### API note
Open Archieven's `name=` search is fuzzy and sorted alphabetically, so a bare name query
returns thousands of unrelated hits and **ignores `place=`** for ranking. The reliable
technique used here was: (1) start from a known marriage act to get exact parent names
and birthplace, then (2) query `eventtype=Geboorte&eventplace=Boekel` for the surname
and **fetch each candidate's full record** to verify both parents match. The
`eventplace` filter *does* work when combined with `eventtype` on the detail-verification
pass.
