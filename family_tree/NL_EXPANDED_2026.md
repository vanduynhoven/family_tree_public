## 1. Notable Dutch Van Du(ij/y)nhoven Individuals

### A. Rein van Duijnhoven (b. 1967) - Footballer ⚽
- **Born:** September 5, 1967, Nuenen, Noord-Brabant
- **Occupation:** Professional footballer (goalkeeper), later manager
- **Career highlights:**
  - Started at VV Nuenen (youth)
  - TSV LONGA (Tilburg)
  - Helmond Sport (professional debut at age 18)
  - MVV Maastricht (1994-1999)
  - **VfL Bochum, Germany** (1999-2006) - 119 Bundesliga appearances
- **Notable:** Scored a goal as goalkeeper at MVV Maastricht (1995/96) - voted Dutch Goal of the Year
- **Total career:** 517 professional matches
- **Source:** [nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rein_van_Duijnhoven](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rein_van_Duijnhoven)

### B. Martin van Duynhoven (b. 1942) - Jazz Musician 🥁
- **Born:** June 13, 1942, Boxmeer, Noord-Brabant
- **Occupation:** Jazz drummer and percussionist
- **Significance:** One of the "Big Four" Dutch jazz drummers of the bebop generation (1960s-70s) alongside:
  - Han Bennink
  - John Engels
  - Pierre Courbois
- **Education:** Conservatorium van Tilburg (1959-1962), Kunstakademie Den Bosch
- **Musical background:** Started in harmonieorkest (marching band) in Uden
- **Groups:** Performed with BCDE (quartet with all four Dutch jazz legends)
- **Style:** Known for philosophical, intellectual approach to jazz drumming
- **Part of:** Dutch Geïmproviseerde Muziek scene (ICP Orchestra, Willem Breuker Kollektief)
- **Source:** [nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_van_Duynhoven](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_van_Duynhoven)

### C. Serge van Duijnhoven (b. 1970) - Writer/Poet 📚
- **Born:** September 10, 1970, Oss, Noord-Brabant
- **Occupation:** Writer, poet, historian, journalist
- **Education:** Gymnasium at Titus Brandsma Lyceum, Oss
- **Achievements:**
  - Founder of literary magazine *MillenniuM*
  - Founder of *Stichting Kunstgroep Lage Landen*
  - Frontman of literary band "Dichters Dansen Niet" (Poets Don't Dance) since 1995
  - War correspondent in Sarajevo for De Morgen and Volkskrant (1995-2001)
- **Writing career:** Published in Vrij Nederland, cobra.be, International Feature Agency
- **Residencies:** Writer in residence in Armenia, USA, France
- **Current residence:** Vught, Noord-Brabant
- **Political:** Ran as candidate in 2021 Dutch elections (Tweede Kamerverkiezingen)
- **Source:** [dbnl.org/auteurs/auteur.php?id=duij004](https://www.dbnl.org/auteurs/auteur.php?id=duij004)

### D. Ton van Duinhoven (1921-2010) - Actor/Journalist 🎭
- **Born:** May 12, 1921, Schiedam
- **Died:** August 26, 2010, Amsterdam
- **Occupation:** Journalist, actor, comedy writer
- **Career highlights:**
  - Started on sports desk of Het Parool
  - Created satirical TV program "Hadimassa" with Wim de Bie and Kees van Kooten
  - Appeared in "Cassata" (1979 TV series)
  - Famous character: Feyenoord Suppoost Crooswijk
  - Film: "De vloek van Woestewolf" (1974)
- **Married to:** Ina van Faassen
- **Source:** [nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ton_van_Duinhoven](https://nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ton_van_Duinhoven)

### E. Harry Duynhoven (b. 1955) - New Zealand Politician 🇳🇿
- **Born:** June 22, 1955, New Plymouth, New Zealand
- **Dutch connection:** Father was from the Netherlands; held dual NZ-Dutch citizenship
- **Political career:**
  - Member of Parliament for New Plymouth (1987-1990, 1993-2008)
  - Mayor of New Plymouth (2010-2013)
  - Associate Minister of Transport, Minister for Transport Safety
  - Associate Minister of Energy
  - Companion of Queen's Service Order (2012)
- **Controversy:** In 2003, created constitutional crisis by applying to resume Dutch citizenship while serving as MP - Parliament retroactively amended electoral law to allow him to retain his seat
- **Currently:** District councillor for New Plymouth
- **Source:** [en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Duynhoven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Duynhoven)

## 2. Van Duijnhoven in Dutch Business

### Bovans Poultry Breeders - Founded by Van Duijnhoven Family 🐔
- **Founded:** 1954 at Stevensbeek, Noord-Brabant
- **Founders:** Four Dutch families:
  1. **Van Duijnhoven** (Harry and wife Nora)
  2. Bongers (Bo-)
  3. Van Lankveld (-vans)
  4. Van der Linden (-vans)
- **Name origin:** "Bo" + three "Vans" = BOVANS
- **Harry van Duijnhoven:**
  - Primary founder
  - Farm located at Stevensbeek
  - Brother designed the original Bovans logo (still in use)
- **Today:** Global presence - Europe, Americas, Africa, Asia, Middle East
- **Known for:** "Robust Results" - strong, healthy laying hens
- **Now part of:** Hendrix Genetics
- **Source:** [bovans.com/en/about-us/history](https://www.bovans.com/en/about-us/history-/)
