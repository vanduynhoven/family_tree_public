# Van Duynhoven / Van Duijnhoven — International Branches

Research into Van Duynhoven emigrants beyond the Netherlands and the United States.
Dutch post-war emigration (1949–1970) sent ~160,000 nationals worldwide — chiefly to
Canada, Australia, New Zealand, the US, and South Africa. This document tracks
confirmed and candidate family members in those destinations.

**Compiled:** 2026-08-29
**Name spelling note:** the family name appears as Dutch `van Duijnhoven` (ij digraph),
US `Van Duynhoven` (y), and — in New Zealand — with the `van` dropped entirely to
`Duynhoven`. Search **all** variants: `van Duijnhoven`, `Van Duynhoven`, `Duynhoven`,
`Vanduynhoven`, `Van Dyn Hoven`.

**Relationship to Arthur's line:** None of the international branches below are yet
*confirmed* to descend from Arthur's grandfather Johan van Duijnhoven (b. 1915 Uden)
or his father Marianus (b. 1872 Uden). They are documented Van Duynhoven emigrants
recorded as separate branches pending a civil-registry link. Confidence is stated per entry.

## 🇳🇿 New Zealand — CONFIRMED BRANCH (separate line)

The strongest international finding. The **Duynhoven** family of New Plymouth,
Taranaki, is a well-documented Dutch post-war immigrant family.

| **Notability** | NZ Labour Party MP for New Plymouth (1987–1990, 1993–2008); Minister outside Cabinet (Associate Minister of Transport; Minister for Transport Safety; Associate Minister of Energy) under Helen Clark; Mayor of New Plymouth 2010–2013; New Plymouth District Councillor from 2015; awarded QSO (2012) and the NZ 1990 Commemoration Medal |
| **Occupation** | Trained electrician; technical teacher (secondary + tertiary) for 15 years before Parliament |
| **Education** | Spotswood College (left at 16); later Victoria University of Wellington |

**Dutch-nationality parallel to Arthur's family (notable):**
Harry Duynhoven possessed Dutch citizenship **from birth** (via his Dutch-born father)
but **temporarily lost it due to a change of Dutch law**. In 2003 he applied to *resume*
his Dutch citizenship — which under NZ electoral law nearly cost him his parliamentary
seat (the Privileges Committee ruled the seat vacated on 11 June 2003; the government
passed a retroactive amendment to restore it). This is the **same class of
nationality-loss mechanism** (Dutch minor children losing nationality through a parent's
circumstances under the 1892 WNI / later RWN) that broke the Dutch-nationality chain in
Arthur's own family in 1956 — see `DUTCH_CITIZENSHIP_ELIGIBILITY_ANALYSIS`.

**Confidence:** Family confirmed as Dutch immigrant. Link to Arthur's Uden/Boekel line
**unconfirmed** — no shared ancestor established. Requires: father's given name, birth
town, and emigration record (NZ arrival passenger lists / Dutch emigration registers).

**Sources:** Wikipedia (en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Duynhoven); airqualityasia.org
biographical PDF; Te Ara Encyclopedia of New Zealand.

## 🇦🇺 Australia — NO DIRECT INDIVIDUAL YET; CONTEXT ESTABLISHED

No specific Van Duynhoven individual was surfaced in Australian records via open web
search. Context strongly supports that a branch may exist:

- ~160,000 Dutch nationals emigrated to Australia 1947–1970, from both the Netherlands
  and the Netherlands East Indies (NEI/Indonesia). Dutch migration to Australia was
  **overwhelmingly family-based** rather than individual.
- Primary destinations to check: Melbourne, Sydney, and regional Victoria/NSW/Queensland.

**Search leads NOT yet exhausted (require logged-in / on-site access):**
- **Trove** (trove.nla.gov.au) — digitised Australian newspapers, 218M+ articles.
  Search `Van Duynhoven`, `Duynhoven`, `van Duijnhoven` and filter by state/decade.
- **Ancestry.com.au** / **MyHeritage** — passenger arrival lists, electoral rolls
  (login required).
- **Obituaries Australia** (oa.anu.edu.au); **mytributes.com.au**;
  **deathsandfunerals.com.au**.
- **Dutch Australian Cultural Centre** (dutchaustralianculturalcentre.com.au) — migration
  history archive and family-history resources.

**Confidence:** No individual confirmed. Australian branch **plausible but unverified.**
