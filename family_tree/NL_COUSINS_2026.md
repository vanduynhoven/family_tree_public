# Netherlands-Side van Duijnhoven / van Duynhoven — Cousins & Kin (2026 research)

Research date: 2026-08-29. Focus: Netherlands (Noord-Brabant, Uden region) branches
and individuals not already documented in the main US/emigrant databases.

Spelling note: the Dutch civil records overwhelmingly use **van Duijnhoven** (ij).
The **van Duynhoven** (y) spelling is a much rarer variant of the same name.

---

## 1. Surname frequency in the Netherlands (CBG Familienamenbank)

Authoritative figures from the CBG | Centrum voor familiegeschiedenis
(Nederlandse Familienamenbank, 2007 census-based count):

| Spelling | Bearers in NL (2007) | % of population |
|----------|----------------------|-----------------|
| van Duijnhoven (ij) | **1,315 persons** | ~0.0082% (combined ij+y) |
| van Duynhoven (y)   | ~693 persons (variant cluster) | ~0.0001% (y-only) |

- In **1947** the count for "van Duijnhoven" was recorded as 0 in the sampled
  register — i.e. the name concentrates in the southern (Brabant) records that the
  1947 national sample under-covered; by 2007 it is a firmly Brabant-clustered name.
- Geographic core: **Noord-Brabant**, heaviest around Uden / Gemert-Bakel / Helmond /
  Nuenen / Veghel — the same Peelland/Meierij zone the family originates from.
- Source: https://www.cbgfamilienamen.nl/nfb/detail_naam.php?gba_naam=van+Duijnhoven

This makes it a genuinely uncommon Dutch surname (~1,300–2,000 bearers total across
both spellings), overwhelmingly rooted in the family's home region.

## 4. Notable people named van Duijnhoven / van Duynhoven (NL)

Two clearly notable public figures carry the name (relationship to the direct line
unconfirmed, but both are Noord-Brabant / same-region and therefore plausible distant
collateral kin given how rare and regionally-concentrated the surname is):

### Rein van Duijnhoven (footballer)
- Born **5 September 1967 in Veghel, Noord-Brabant** (~10 km from Uden).
- Dutch football **goalkeeper**, 193 cm. Junior club VV Nuenen.
- Career: Helmond Sport (1987–88, 1990–94), TSV LONGA (1988–90), **MVV Maastricht**
  (1994–99), **VfL Bochum** (Germany, 1999–2006; 119 Bundesliga + 61 2.Bundesliga games).
- Famous for scoring a **freekick goal from >80 m** for MVV Maastricht in 1995/96,
  voted **Goal of the Year** in the Netherlands. Cult figure ("RvD") at VfL Bochum;
  holds the club record for longest run without conceding at home (911 minutes, 2003/04).
- Later goalkeeping coach: Helmond Sport, then Roda JC Kerkrade (from 2020).
- Source: https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rein_van_Duijnhoven

### Martin van Duynhoven (jazz musician)
- Dutch **jazz drummer / percussionist**, one of the most renowned of the Dutch
  bebop generation — named alongside Han Bennink, John Engels and Pierre Courbois.
- Source: https://www.jazzhelden.nl/ (Jazzhelden portrait — "Martin van Duynhoven")
- Note: uses the rarer **van Duynhoven (y)** spelling, matching the family's own
  spelling variant.
