## 1. STRONGEST NEW CONNECTION — the "Genealogie Bernards" Uden line (COLLATERAL)

**Source tree:** *Genealogie Bernards*, author on Genealogie Online.
This is a parallel Uden van Duijnhoven household running exactly alongside our target line, almost certainly sharing a common ancestor in the early-to-mid 1700s.

### 1a. Johannes van Duijnhoven (1793–1866, Uden) — near-twin contemporary of target Petrus (1799)

- Born **4 Aug 1793, Uden**; died **12 Dec 1866, Uden**.
- Parents: **Johannes Peter van Duijnhoven (1757–1835)** × **Anna Maria Jan Hendrik Korsten (1758–1796)**.
- Grandfather: **Joannes Peters van Duijnhoven (1722–1795)** × **Allegondis Janssen van den Bout (van Haandel) (1710–?)**.
- Married **Cornelia van der Wijst (1799–1862)** on **13 Jun 1827, Uden**.
- Profile: https://www.genealogieonline.nl/genealogie-bernards/I2496.php

### 1b. Why this is collateral to our line (not the same line)

- **Locality + era match exactly:** both households are in Uden, and Johannes (b. 1793) and our target Petrus (b. 1799) are effectively the same generation.
- **Shared naming pattern:** the Bernards line's father/grandfather are **Johannes Peter** (1757–1835) and **Joannes Peters** (1722–1795); our line's earliest confirmed ancestor is **Petrus Joannis van Duijnhoven** (m. Elisabeth van Boxtel, Uden 1808). The recurring "Joannis/Peter ↔ Petrus/Joannis" alternation is the classic Brabant patronymic naming and points to a **shared ancestor around the 1720s–1750s Uden generation** — the exact junction our own research flagged as "Generation −2, pre-1811 DTB registers still needed."
- **Two separate Martinuses:** the Bernards Martinus (1828–1890) is NOT our Martinus (1829–1903, × Geertruda Verwegen). They are almost certainly first cousins.

> **Research action:** confirm the junction by locating the baptisms of **Johannes Peter van Duijnhoven (1757)** and of our **Petrus Joannis** — if they share **Joannes Peters van Duijnhoven (1722–1795)** or his siblings as a parent/grandparent, the two Uden branches are formally joined.

## 2. OTHER PUBLISHED VAN DUIJNHOVEN TREES (surname network — likely deeper-collateral)

These are distinct branches of the same Brabant/Land van Cuijk van Duijnhoven stock. None is the direct Uden line, but they establish the wider tree and are candidate merge points further back.

### 2a. "Stamboom Van de Ven – Goossens" (Martin van de Ven) — Schaijk / Land van Cuijk branch
Schaijk **borders Uden**, so this is a strong candidate for a nearby collateral branch.
- **Petrus van Duijnhoven (1757–1831)** × Anna Maria (Theodorusse) (1758–1806) — a same-name, same-era figure to keep distinct from the Bernards "Johannes Peter (1757–1835)."
- **Theodorus van Duijnhoven (1798–1849)** × Lamberdina Vissers (1804–1883).
- **Petrus van Duijnhoven (1839–1909)**, b./d. Schaijk, × Johanna Catharina van den Hoogen (1871, Schaijk).
- Also holds 1700s Duijnhovens: Adrianus "Adriaan" (1728–1809), Willibrordus (1726), Godefridus (1735), Joanna (1718), Martin (1811–1835, son of Petrus 1757–1831).
- Entry points:
  - https://www.genealogieonline.nl/stamboom-van-de-ven/I1135.php (Petrus 1839–1909)
  - https://www.genealogieonline.nl/stamboom-van-de-ven/I1124.php (Martin 1811–1835)
  - https://www.genealogieonline.nl/stamboom-van-de-ven/I1148.php (Adrianus 1728–1809)

### 2b. "Genealogie Teunissen" (F. Teunissen) — Cuijk / Wanroij branch
- **Petrus "Peter" van Duijnhoven Duinhoven (1773–1839)**, baptised Cuijk, died Wanroij; klompenmaker (clog-maker).
- Parents: **Rudolphus (van) D(u)ijnhoven (1747–1795)** × Hermina Loeffen (1743–1784); grandfather **Petrus Jacobus van Dijnhoven (1709–1773)**.
- × Maria Jacobs (1794, Wanroij); 8 children incl. Rijnerus (1798–1861).

- This branch also documents **Joannes Peter van DUYNHOVEN (1722–1795)** — note the name coincidence with the Bernards grandfather; verify whether these are the **same** 1722 individual (a potential cross-tree link pin).

## 3. CROSS-TREE CONFIRMATIONS ("Aanknopingspunten")

Genealogie Online flags that **Johannes van Duijnhoven (1793–1866, Uden)** — item 1a above — is the SAME person across multiple independent published trees (authors have mutually confirmed):

- **Stamboom familie Hebben** — confirmed same person
- **Stamboom Hebben** (two separate confirmations)
- **Stamboom van de familie Van den Eijnde(n) uit Gemert en Haarlem**

This means the collateral Uden branch is corroborated by ≥4 independent researchers and links outward into the **Hebben** and **Van den Eijnden** families. Those trees are the fastest way to harvest further descendants of the Uden collateral branch.
