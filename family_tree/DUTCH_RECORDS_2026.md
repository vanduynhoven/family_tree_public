# Dutch Archive Records — Van Duijnhoven (Uden) — 2026 Search

Search conducted August 2026 against the Dutch genealogical aggregators
**Open Archieven** (openarchieven.nl), **BHIC** (Brabants Historisch Informatie
Centrum, bhic.nl), and **WieWasWie** (wiewaswie.nl). All BHIC records surface on
Open Archieven with a stable `bhi:<uuid>` identifier; the BHIC source viewer for
each is at `https://www.bhic.nl/memorix/genealogy/search/deeds/<uuid>`.

> Source note: Open Archieven aggregates ~40 Dutch archive institutions. For Uden,
> the holding archive is BHIC (Brabants Historisch Informatie Centrum). WieWasWie
> aggregates a broadly overlapping set; the CBG (Centrum voor familiegeschiedenis,
> The Hague) owns WieWasWie. All three were queried for the surname **Duijnhoven**.
