# Extended Ancestors — Van Duijnhoven / Van der Heijden line

Research into the earliest known generation, extending the tree **earlier than 1799**.
Source: Open Archieven public JSON API (Brabants Historisch Informatie Centrum civil & church registers, Uden, Noord‑Brabant).
Compiled 2026-08-28. All records below are indexed originals; the pivotal 1821 marriage has a downloadable scan.

---

## Result summary

The tree is **confirmed and extended two full generations back from Petrus (1799)** — down to great‑great‑great‑great‑grandparents born c. 1760–1775. All links are anchored on primary records that name parents explicitly.

Previously the earliest known couple was:

- **Petrus van Duijnhoven** (1799–1882)
- **Johanna van der Heijden** (1798–1875)

Now extended to their parents (**Generation 0**) and, on the paternal side, their grandparents (**Generation −1**).

## Confirmed lineage chain (with anchor records)

### The pivot: Martinus's 1858 marriage confirms the whole line
- **Martinus van Duijnhoven** (1829–1903) married **Geertruij Verwegen** on **17 Feb 1858, Uden**.
  - His parents named in the record: **Petrus van Duijnhoven** + **Joanna van der Heijden** ✅ (matches the known tree exactly)
  - Bride's parents (bonus maternal‑side extension): **Johannes Verwegen** + **Mechelina van den Berk**
  - Record: https://www.openarchieven.nl/bhi:056b95f5-6d55-d22a-95e7-1a87ac4d04c4

### Generation 1 — Petrus & Johanna (already known, now with primary sources)
- **Petrus van Duijnhoven** — baptised **27 Aug 1799, Uden** (b. 1799, d. 1882). FamilySearch GMKY‑26P.
  - Baptism record names parents + witnesses: https://www.openarchieven.nl/bhi:48f86655-d2c0-585b-282c-7379cfa41dc3
- **Johanna (Joanna) van der Heijden** — b. 1798, d. 1875, Uden. FamilySearch GMKB‑4Y4.
- **Marriage:** **24 Feb 1821, Uden** — Huwelijksregister Uden 1821, book 8032.
  - **Names ALL FOUR parents (Generation 0).** Has a scanned original.
  - Record: https://www.openarchieven.nl/bhi:5eaf168c-2901-79a5-8ade-a6cb1f225458

### Generation 0 — the parents of Petrus and Johanna (NEW)
From the 1821 marriage + Petrus's 1799 baptism:

**Petrus's parents (paternal great‑great‑great‑grandparents):**
- **Petrus Joannis van Duijnhoven** (Petrus, son of Joannes)
- **Elisabeth Henrici van Boxtel** (Elisabeth, daughter of Hendrik; also written "Elisabeta Henricus van Boxtel")

**Johanna's parents (maternal great‑great‑great‑grandparents) — the Van der Heijden line origin:**
- **Joannes Hendriks van der Heijden** (Johannes, son of Hendrik)
- **Anna Maria Martinus Pittens** (Anna Maria, daughter of Martinus — surname **Pittens**)

> The Van der Heijden line now extends one generation earlier than 1799, to **Joannes Hendriks van der Heijden** and **Anna Maria (Martinus) Pittens** — a previously unknown surname (Pittens) entering the tree.

### Generation −1 — the grandparents (paternal side, NEW)
- **Petrus Joannis van Duijnhoven** married **Elisabeth Henrici van Boxtel** on **21 Nov 1796, Uden** (his *second* marriage).
  - Record: https://www.openarchieven.nl/bhi:d4e6d746-04ea-14a0-0f38-b695bdf431ec
- The patronymics in their names push the line back one more implied generation:
  - Petrus Joannis → his father was a **Joannes (Jan) van Duijnhoven**
  - Elisabeth Henrici van Boxtel → her father was a **Hendrik (Henricus) van Boxtel**
  - Confirmed by the 1799 baptism witnesses: **Joannes Henrici van Boxtel** (Elisabeth's brother) and **Anna Maria Joannis van Duijnhoven** (Petrus Joannis's sister).
- Note: Petrus Joannis's **first** marriage was to **Helena Jansen**, 3 Feb 1785, Uden (https://www.openarchieven.nl/bhi:f2f685e8-eb59-f67f-b0b2-79df9b928135). Petrus (b.1799) is from the *second* marriage to Elisabeth van Boxtel.

## Method note
Open Archieven's `place`/multi‑word `name` filters are unreliable (the `place` param does not restrict results, and combined‑surname queries return 0). Effective approach: search a single name + `sourcetype` + `eventplace`, then call the `records/show.json` detail endpoint per candidate to read the full parent/child relations. The detail endpoint reliably exposes all named parties with relation types (Bruidegom/Bruid/Vader/Moeder/Vader van de bruidegom, etc.) — that is how the parent links above were confirmed.
