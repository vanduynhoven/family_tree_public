### Ethnic and Geographic Notes

The **Luedtke** surname (also spelled Luedke, Ludtke, Liedtke) is of German origin, derived from a diminutive of "Ludwig." 

**Luedtke families in Minnesota:**
- **Hollywood Township, Carver County** - Charles Luedke/Noerenberg family (documented above)
- **Blue Earth County (Mankato area)** - Carl August Luedtke (born 1840 in Prussia, came to America 1858, settled Mankato 1872)
- **Hennepin County** - Various Luedtke families documented

The Carver County Luedke families were Lutheran, primarily German immigrants who settled in the western portion of Carver County (Hollywood Township, New Germany, Waconia area) in the late 19th/early 20th century.
