## Summary
- @F141@ (Theodorus × Maria Werts): 4 → **7 children** (3 new: @I445@ @I446@ @I447@;
  @I160@ Peter enriched with birth 23 Aug 1843).
- @F144@ (Cornelis × Ida van de Weijer): 1 → **9 children** (8 new: @I448@–@I455@).

All births cited to BHIC Bakel en Milheeze records; where both a civil (BS Geboorte)
and church (DTB Dopen) record exist, both were confirmed to name the same parents
(evidence strength: strong / corroborated). DTB-only entries (1808–1810, 1813) rest on
a single church source (moderate) but the parent naming is explicit and consistent.

Records evaluated in the 1808–1855 window; the following Bakel Cornelissen births in
that range were checked and **excluded** as belonging to other couples: Johannes 1816
(Dirk Cornelissen × Johanna Maria van Leunen), Theodora Maria 1848 / Helena 1850 /
Theodorus 1852 (Johannes Cornelissen × Johanna Rooyakkers), Noldina 1854 / Josephus
1855 (Christiaan Cornelissen × Antonet Verspaget).
