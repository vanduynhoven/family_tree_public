# Van Ham Family Research

**Focus:** Ancestors of Joanna Lamberti van Ham (mother of Elisabeth van Boxtel)

## Target Person

## Possible Second Marriage of Lambertus van Ham

**Marriage:** 14 May 1741, Breugel  
**Spouse:** Catharina Jan Scheutijens  
**Archive:** BHIC DTB Trouwen

Note: This could be:
- A second marriage for the same Lambertus Adriani (after death of Joanna Arnoldi)
- A son named Lambertus (Lambertus Lamberti van Ham)

The 1712 baptism record shows "Catharina Joannes Smits" as mother, suggesting the original wife Joanna Arnoldi may have died and Lambertus remarried by then.
