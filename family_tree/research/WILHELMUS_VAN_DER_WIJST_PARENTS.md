# Parents of Wilhelmus van der Wijst (b. 10 Apr 1805, Zeeland)

**Research question:** Who were the parents of Wilhelmus van der Wijst
(~1800–1810, Zeeland/Uden), the father of Arnoldus van der Wijst
(b. 8 Oct 1837, Uden)?

**Verdict:** RESOLVED — **strong evidence** (two independent primary sources).

## Conclusion

Wilhelmus van der Wijst was **baptised 10 April 1805 in Zeeland (Noord-Brabant)**,
son of:

- **Father:** Joannes (Joannis / Joannes Willems) van der Wijst
- **Mother:** Petronella de Klein (= Petronella Klein / de Klijn)

This confirms the "1805 candidate" line from the prior research and **rules out**
the 1794 Hubertus baptism (different family).

## Evidence chain

### 1. Arnoldus's 1837 birth names his parents (starting point)
- **Record:** BS Geboorte, Uden, akte 106, 8 Oct 1837 — Geboorteregister Uden 1837.
- **Child:** Arnoldus van der Wijst (b. 8 Oct 1837, Uden).
- **Father:** Wilhelmus van der Wijst — **Mother:** Helena van der Sanden.
- Source: BHIC `bhi:a26d80fe-a501-d8f5-a244-6a294571cd31`
  (https://www.openarchieven.nl/bhi:a26d80fe-a501-d8f5-a244-6a294571cd31)
- **Note on date:** the task brief cited "5 Oct 1837"; the source register records
  **8 Oct 1837**. The existing GEDCOM already carries 8 Oct 1837, so no change needed.
- Dutch 1837 birth records do **not** name grandparents, so this record alone
  cannot answer the question — it only fixes the father as "Wilhelmus van der Wijst".

### 2. Wilhelmus's own marriage record names HIS parents (primary source A)
- **Record:** BS Huwelijk, Zeeland (NB), 25 Apr 1847 — Wilhelmus van der Wijst
  (widower) marries Johanna Catharina van Wiechen.
- **Groom:** Wilhelmus van der Wijst — **birth stated: 10 April 1805, Zeeland.**
- **Father of groom:** Joannes Willems van der Wijst.
- **Mother of groom:** Petronella Klein.
- Source: BHIC `bhi:299d8385-dde1-8ff9-c91d-b62089716ebb`
  (https://www.openarchieven.nl/bhi:299d8385-dde1-8ff9-c91d-b62089716ebb)
- This is a **second marriage** of the same Wilhelmus (Helena van der Sanden having
  died); the stated birth 10 Apr 1805 / Zeeland ties it to the 1805 baptism below.

### 3. The 1805 Zeeland baptism (primary source B — independent corroboration)
- **Record:** DTB Dopen, Zeeland (Zeeland doopboek 1752–1811), 10 Apr 1805.
- **Child (Kind):** Wilhelmus van der Wijst.
- **Father (Vader):** Joannis van der Wijst.
- **Mother (Moeder):** Petronella de Klein.
- Witnesses (Getuigen): Petrus van der Wijst, Hermana van der Wijst.
- Source: BHIC `bhi:0c1e40be-4629-11e3-a747-d206bceb4d38`
  (https://www.openarchieven.nl/bhi:0c1e40be-4629-11e3-a747-d206bceb4d38)

### Cross-check
The 1847 marriage's stated birth (**10 Apr 1805, Zeeland**) and named parents
(**Joannes Willems van der Wijst × Petronella Klein**) match the 1805 baptism
(**10 Apr 1805, Zeeland; Joannis van der Wijst × Petronella de Klein**) exactly.
Two independent record types (marriage + baptism) agree on date, place, and both
parents → **strong evidence**.

## Name-spelling notes
- Father: **Joannes / Joannis Willems van der Wijst** ("Willems" = patronymic,
  i.e. son of a Wilhelmus — consistent with him naming a son Wilhelmus).
- Mother: **Petronella de Klein** — also rendered *Klein*, *de Klijn*.
- Wife (Arnoldus's mother): **Helena van der Sanden** = *van der Zanden*.
