# Open Gaps Research — Collateral Lines (2026)

Source throughout: OpenArchieven / BHIC (Brabants Historisch Informatie Centrum),
DTB and Burgerlijke Stand (BS) indexed registers. All records cited by `bhi:` id
resolve at `https://www.openarchieven.nl/bhi:<id>`.

Selected the 3 most tractable, **fully-indexed** gaps (no reliance on unscanned
schepenbank / parish books): Gap A (Cornelissen×Werts children), Gap B (Jaspers×
Verwegen children + Adriana's death), Gap C (Joost van de Weyer parents + Ida's
siblings). Gaps requiring unindexed scans (Joannes Cornelissen ~1750, Willemijn van
de Meulenhof, Cornelis van den Elsen Boekel, Henricus Peters van Boxtel, Willem
Werts parents) were deliberately skipped this cycle.

## GAP B — Arnoldus Jaspers × Adriana Verwegen: children + Adriana's death  (GEDCOM @F102@)

**Correction to the brief:** the couple married 5 May 1849 Uden is Arnoldus Jaspers
× **Adriana** Verwegen (@I260@, b.29 Apr 1820, sister of Geertruda) — NOT "Antonia
Verwegen" (@I337@ is a different person, a niece b.1872). Research was directed at
the correct couple.

### Children
- Only **Maria Jaspers** (b. **15 Dec 1850** Uden, bhi:5e19509a-c515-91db-b2bb-a1274e01b3fa,
  already @I324@) matches Arnoldus Jaspers × Adriana Verwegen in the indexed BS
  Geboorte Uden.
- A parent-matched sweep of **all** indexed "Jaspers, relation=Kind, Geboorte"
  births — Uden and any-place, window 1850–1872 — surfaced **no further child**
  with these two parents. Evidence for "Maria is the only surviving child in the
  index" = moderate (absence in a complete index, but the index has gaps).

### Adriana's death — factual correction (evidence: strong)
The GEDCOM previously stated Adriana "died ~1865" and Arnoldus "remarried ~1868
Allegonda Janssens." The Uden death index disproves this for **this** Adriana:

- **Adriana Verwegen died 28 Jan 1896, Uden** — BS Overlijden
  bhi:7b87cafe-8e31-a39a-c124-f51458ac9d5b. The record names her parents
  **Johannes Verwegen × Mechelina van den Berk** and her husband **Arnoldus
  Jaspers** — an exact match to @I260@ (same birth parents, same spouse).

So Adriana outlived 1865 by three decades; the "remarried 1868" claim belonged to a
different Adriana/family and has been **withdrawn** from the @F102@ note. (If
Arnoldus did remarry, it was after 1896, not 1868 — not investigated this cycle.)

**Added to GEDCOM:** DEAT 28 Jan 1896 Uden on @I260@ with sourced NOTE; @F102@ note
corrected.

## GAP C — Joost (Judocus) van de Weyer × Josina (Justina) Biemans  (GEDCOM @F147@)

The single richest result. Joost's own parents were unknown; his family is now
reconstructed from the Bakel en Milheeze DTB registers (evidence: strong —
patronymics consistent across 6+ independent records).

### The marriage
- **Judocus van de Weyer × Justina Biemans, married 5 Feb 1769, Bakel en Milheeze**
  — DTB Trouwen bhi:2a97eecb-8227-8cf9-51de-a4e40bcfbceb.

### Joost's and Josina's parents (from patronymics)
Across the marriage and every child's baptism, Joost is recorded as **"Judocus
Mathys / Judocus Tysse van de Weyer"** and Josina as **"Justina Judoci Biemans"**:
- Joost's father = **Mathias (Matthijs) van de Weyer** — patronymic Mathys/Tysse.
- Josina's father = **Judocus Biemans** — patronymic Judoci.

**Added to GEDCOM:** @I437@ Mathias van de Weyer (Joost's father, new @F152@ →
@I424@ FAMC); @I438@ Judocus Biemans (Josina's father, new @F153@ → @I425@ FAMC).
This pushes both the van de Weyer and Biemans lines back one generation.
