## Overview

**Gen 2 Martinus van Duijnhoven (1829–1903)** married **Geertruda Verwegen (1833–1900)** and had 11 children. Two of their children who stayed in the Netherlands and had descendants are documented here:

1. **Petrus van Duijnhoven** (b. 3 May 1867, Uden) — FamilySearch GW7F-2BW
2. **Petronella van Duijnhoven** (b. 1 Jun 1869, Uden) — FamilySearch GWWD-96D

Their brother **Marianus van Duijnhoven** (b. 24 Apr 1872) is the direct ancestor who also stayed in Netherlands.
