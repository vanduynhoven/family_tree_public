# Geertruda Verwegen — 1939 Death Akte OCR Verification

## Question
Does 'wijlen' (or an equivalent "deceased" marker) appear before Geertruij Verwegen
as mother in the 22 Oct 1939 death akte of her son Antonius van Duijnhoven?
If so, she was already dead by 22 Oct 1939.

## Finding
- The word **"wijlen" does NOT appear** before "Verwegen, Geertruij".
- The akte instead uses the equivalent trailing marker **"beiden overleden"**
  ("both deceased") applied to BOTH parents (Martinus van Duijnhoven AND
  Geertruij Verwegen).
- **Conclusion: Geertruij (Geertruda) Verwegen was ALREADY DECEASED by 22 Oct 1939.**
  The genealogical result is identical to a "wijlen" marker — the akte simply
  uses "beiden overleden" as the convention.

## Evidence strength
- **moderate→strong**: single primary source (the death akte), but the "beiden
  overleden" clause is explicit and unambiguous.

## GEDCOM implication
- Death of Geertruda Verwegen can be recorded as **BEF 22 OCT 1939**.
