# Van Duijnhoven Ancestral Chain: Gen -3 to Gen -8 (1539-1679)

## Geographic Context

All documented locations are in **Noord-Brabant, Netherlands**:
- **Aarle-Rixtel** (now part of Laarbeek municipality since 1997) — birth of Petrus Jansen (1679), death of Jan Jansen (1696)
- **Gemert** — marriage of Jan Jansen & Maria Joordens (1676)
- **Uden** — marriage of Petrus Jansen & Maria Jan Jacobs (1718)

These are neighboring villages in the eastern Brabant region, near Helmond.
