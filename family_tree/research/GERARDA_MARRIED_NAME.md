# Research: Married name & husband of Gerarda Maria van Duijnhoven (@I016@)

**Subject:** Gerarda Maria van Duijnhoven
- Born 25 Sep 1919, Uden / Volkel, Noord-Brabant
- Daughter of Marianus van Duijnhoven & Anna Maria van den Elzen (family @F003@)
- Died 30 Apr 2002 at Velp (Gelderland), aged 82

## Question
Find her **married surname** and the **name of her husband**.

## Result: NOT FOUND in digitized public sources (as of 2026-08-31)

Her married name and husband could **not be established** from any digitized,
publicly-searchable record. This is a documented negative result, not a gap left
unsearched. No husband has been added to the GEDCOM, because inventing one would
be fabrication.

## Sources checked

### 1. Prayer card (bidprentje) — the record that resolved her death
- Open Archieven index: https://www.openarchieven.nl/hwh:c905fc6f-069c-286a-e104-8a69c1d44f43
- Digital original (Heemkundekring Weerderheem, record 038658):
  https://weerderheemcollecties.nl/cgi-bin/bidprent.pl?ident=038658
- **Full transcription lists ONLY:** "Overlijden op 30 april 2002 te Velp —
  Overledene (vrouw): **Grada van Duijnhoven**, geboren op 25 september 1919 te
  Volkel." (maiden name)
- **No spouse field.** For comparison, every other bidprentje retrieved for a
  married woman in this collection carries an explicit `Echtgenoot -` (husband) or
  `Weduwnaar -` (widower) line (e.g. Tonny van Duijnhoven & Frans van Haren;
  Mechtilda van Duijnhoven & Petrus Wolters). This record has none — the card as
  indexed simply does not record a husband.

### 2. Birth / civil-registration records (BHIC, archive code `bhi`) — CONFIRM identity
- Birth (BS Geboorte), Uden 25-09-1919:
  https://www.openarchieven.nl/bhi:d4551aed-951c-61cc-d4fd-407457628ee5
  - Child: Gerarda Maria van Duijnhoven (Vrouw), b. 25-09-1919
  - Vader: Marianus van Duijnhoven — Moeder: Anna Maria van den Elzen
  - This exactly matches @I016@ and her parents @F003@.
- Bevolkingsregister (gezinskaart) registrations, Uden, same date: identifiers
  `080be3fb-...`, `2af86375-...`, `de860d54-...` — all under her **maiden** name.

### 3. Marriage records — searched, none exist online under her name
- OpenArchieven records API, `eventtype=Huwelijk`, name variants
  "Gerarda van Duijnhoven", "Gerarda van Duynhoven", "Grada van Duijnhoven",
  "Gerarda Duijnhoven": **0 hits** for each.
- WieWasWie marriage search: no matching Gerarda van Duijnhoven marriage.
- Likely cause: Dutch civil-registration **privacy windows**. Marriage acts are
  generally released only after ~75-100 years; a marriage after 1938 is therefore
  not yet public. Her death record (<50 years) is likewise not in the civil index —
  which is exactly why the death is known only via the (privately-donated) prayer
  card.

### 4. Death under a married surname at Velp 2002 — searched, none found
- OpenArchieven + web searches for a Velp / Rheden 2002 death record under any
  married surname (b. 1919 Volkel): **no match**. The Gelders Archief BS Overlijden
  index for Rheden/Velp does not surface a 2002 death for her under a married name
  (2002 deaths are inside the 50-year privacy window and largely unpublished).

## Why she was still "van Duijnhoven" on the prayer card
In the Netherlands a married woman legally keeps her birth surname; prayer cards
and death indexes are frequently filed under the **maiden** name, especially when
the donating collection (here Weerderheem, Valkenswaard) catalogued the card that
way. So "Grada van Duijnhoven" on the card does **not** imply she never married —
it is simply how the card is indexed. The move from Volkel (Noord-Brabant) to Velp
(Gelderland), ~90 km north, is itself consistent with a marriage that relocated her,
but provides no husband name.
