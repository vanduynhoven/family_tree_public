# Cornelissen — Great-Grandparents Research

Research into the parents of **Theodorus Cornelissen** (@I412@) and **Maria Werts**
(@I413@), i.e. the paternal grandparents of **Peter Cornelissen** (b. ~1848–1852 Bakel,
m. Johanna Verhoeven 11 Feb 1874 Bakel).

Source: OpenArchieven / Brabants Historisch Informatie Centrum (BHIC) civil-registration
(Burgerlijke Stand) and church (DTB) records.

## Result summary

| Person | Event | Date / Place | Father | Mother |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Theodorus Cornelissen | Baptism (Doop) | **30-01-1808, Bakel en Milheeze** | **Cornelis (Cornelius) Cornelissen** | **Ida van de Weijer (van de Weyer)** |
| Maria Werts | Birth (Geboorte) | **10-09-1814, Gemert** | **Willem Werts** | **Johanna van der Elzen (Joanna van den Elsen)** |

Both parent sets are independently **corroborated** by the couple's own 1833 marriage
record AND by each individual's baptism/birth record → **evidence: strong**.

## Maria Werts — birth

- Identifier: `3d201711-c4ac-0834-43f4-59de01bd377e`
- URL: https://www.openarchieven.nl/bhi:3d201711-c4ac-0834-43f4-59de01bd377e
- Kind: **Maria Werts**
- Vader: **Willem Werts**
- Moeder: **Joanna van den Elsen** (= Johanna van der Elzen in the marriage record)
- Parents match the 1833 marriage record. Age at marriage ≈ 18 — consistent.
- NOTE / disambiguation: a *different* Maria Werts was born 28-11-1815 in Gemert to
  **Cornelus** Werts (record shows "Joanna Maria Werts", father Cornelus Werts,
  mother Joanna Maria Smits) — NOT our person. The correct match is the 10-09-1814
  child of **Willem** Werts, because the father's name matches the marriage record.
