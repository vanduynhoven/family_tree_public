# VERWEGEN Family - 5 Generations Deeper Research

## Research Date: 2026-08-29
## Source: OpenArchieven.nl API + BHIC (Brabants Historisch Informatie Centrum)

## SUMMARY OF CONFIRMED LINEAGE

### Generation 1: Geertruda Verwegen (b. ~1835)
- **Marriage**: 17-2-1858, Uden
- **Spouse**: Martinus van Duijnhoven
- **Parents**: Johannes Verwegen & Mechelina van den Berk

### Generation 2: Johannes Verwegen (b. ~1793)
- **Marriage**: 13-2-1820, Uden
- **Spouse**: Mechelina van den Berk (daughter of unknown parents)
- **Parents**: Geurt (Godefridus) Verwegen & Helena (Joannis) Gielens
- **Source**: BS Huwelijk 13-2-1820 Uden - explicitly names parents as "Geurt Verwegen" and "Helena Gielens"
- **Record ID**: 49cdc0ff-908c-454b-9079-2d6a6ad99619

### Generation 3: Geurt Peter Verwegen (b. ~1745)
- **Marriage**: 31-1-1773, Boekel
- **Spouse**: Leen Jan Gielens (bride's name - she was daughter of Jan Gielens)
- **Witnesses**: Gerardus van de Burgt, Joannes van der Wist
- **Parents**: Petrus (Peter) Verwegen & Unknown mother
- **Source**: DTB Trouwen 31-1-1773 Boekel
- **Record ID**: 673e6578-4629-11e3-a747-d206bceb4d38

**IMPORTANT NOTE**: There appear to be TWO different Geurt/Godefridus Verwegen individuals:
1. **Geurt Peter Verwegen** (married 1773 Boekel) - son of Peter Verwegen
2. **Godefridus Petri Verwegen** (baptized 18-12-1773 Uden) - son of Petrus Verwegen & Helena Joannis Gielens

The 1820 marriage record shows Johannes Verwegen's father as "Geurt Verwegen" with mother "Helena Gielens", which matches Godefridus Petri Verwegen (the second person).

### REVISED Generation 3: Godefridus (Geurt) Petri Verwegen
- **Baptism**: 18-12-1773, Uden
- **Parents**: Petrus Verwegen & Helena Joannis Gielens
- **Godparents**: Joannes Verwegen, Joannes Henrici van der Wijst, Henrica Joannis Gielens
- **Source**: DTB Dopen Uden
- **Record ID**: f803089d-ba08-8f53-344f-ce8d4f72912c

- Based on marriage ~1770 and children from 1773: born approximately 1740-1745
