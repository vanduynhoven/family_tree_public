## GEOGRAPHICAL CONTEXT

### Key Locations:
1. **Uden** - Primary residence of Joannes Peter(s) and family
2. **Volkel** - Part of Uden municipality; birthplace listed as "Uden-Volkel"

### Regional Note:
All locations are in Noord-Brabant, Netherlands. The van Duijnhoven family was established in this region throughout the 17th-18th centuries, with branches in Uden, Aarle-Rixtel, and the separate Stiphout line.
