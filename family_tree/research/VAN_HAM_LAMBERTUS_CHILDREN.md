## Research Notes

### Second Wife - Catharina Joannes Smits
The 1712 Best baptism record clearly shows "Catharina Joannes Smits" as the mother of the twins Thomas and Anna. This indicates:
1. First wife Joanna Arnoldi vant Haenuelt likely died between 1696-1710
2. Lambertus remarried, probably around 1705-1710
3. The "Joannes Smits" in Catharina's name is her patronymic (father's name)
4. Nicolaus Joannes Smits (godparent) was likely her brother

### Possible Additional Marriage Record
A 1741 Breugel marriage shows "Lambertus van Ham × Catharina Jan Scheutijens" - this could be:
- A third marriage for the same Lambertus (unlikely at ~85 years old)
- A son named Lambertus (possibly Lambertus Adrianus P. from 1696) marrying
- An unrelated Lambertus van Ham

### Children Not Found
Despite thorough searching:
- No baptism record found for Joanna Lamberti van Ham (our direct ancestor)
- Possible gap years 1691-1695, 1697-1711 may have additional children
