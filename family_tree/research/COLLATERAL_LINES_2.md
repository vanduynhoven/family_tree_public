## 2. VAN RIJBROEK LINE (Veghel / Uden)

**Anchor:** Petrus Johannes van Rijbroek (@I039@, b. 5 Sep 1902 Veghel) married
Johanna Maria van Duijnhoven (@I012@) on 29 Mar 1932 in Uden.

**Already in tree:** his parents — Johannes van Rijbroek (@I404@) × Ida Verbruggen
(@I405@), family `@F136@`. (NOTE: an earlier note misremembered the mother as
"Anna van Berlo" — the correct mother is **Ida Verbruggen**, confirmed below. Anna/
Joanna van Berlo actually belongs to the *Wassenberg* line, §3.)

**NEW this pass — the grandparents + the parents' marriage record**, from:

- **Marriage akte `bhi:46b701f5-3dc6-e413-a16a-97c061f6ee87`** (BHIC BS Huwelijk
  **Uden, 18 May 1900**): Johannes van Rijbroek × Ida Verbruggen. Named persons:
  Ida Verbruggen (bride), Johannes van Rijbroek (groom + his father, same name),
  **Petrus Verbruggen**, **Helena van den Broek**, **Johanna Maria van Dijk**.
  Resolving the roles:
  - van Rijbroek side: **Johannes van Rijbroek (sr) × Johanna Maria van Dijk** →
    `@I480@` × `@I481@` (family `@F166@`)
  - Verbruggen side: **Petrus Verbruggen × Helena van den Broek** →
    `@I482@` × `@I483@` (family `@F167@`)
- Son Petrus's 1902 birth akte `bhi:53bb063d-152a-63a2-6dde-6ca831471a8a`
  independently names the parents "Ida Verbruggen, Johannes van Rijbroek."
- Existing family `@F136@` was **enriched** with the marriage date/place (18 May 1900,
  Uden) + akte citation, and Ida (@I405@) was linked FAMC→`@F167@`.

**Reconciliation note:** a duplicate Ida Verbruggen (`@I479@`) and duplicate parents'
family (`@F165@`) were created mid-pass, then merged into the pre-existing `@I405@` /
`@F136@` and deleted. Final tree has no duplicates and no dangling pointers.

**Evidence strength: STRONG** — parents corroborated by the 1900 marriage akte AND
the 1902 birth akte (two independent sources); grandparents from the 1900 akte.

## 3. WASSENBERG LINE (Uden) — reconnects to the VERWEGEN surname

**Anchor:** Hendricus Johannes Wassenberg (@I041@) married Hendrica Maria "Riek"
van Duijnhoven (@I018@) on 4 Jan 1946 in Uden. (Previously CONFIRMED as an endogamous
**second-cousin** marriage — see `WASSENBERG_VD_CONNECTION.md`.)

**Already in tree:** groom's parents — Hendricus Wassenberg **sr.** (@I409@) ×
Johanna van Duijnhoven (@I410@, first cousin of Marianus, daughter of Hendricus vD 1837
@I053@ × Anna Maria van der Wijst @I411@).

**NEW this pass — Hendricus sr.'s birth, his parents, and his grandparents:**

- **Birth akte `bhi:582daa6d-0cdd-d5fe-bbb4-71e5c18ce4ce`** (BHIC BS Geboorte
  **Uden, 5 Oct 1873**): Hendricus Wassenberg, child of **Joannes Wassenberg × Joanna
  Maria Verwegen**. → Hendricus sr. (@I409@) now has birth date 5 Oct 1873, Uden.
- **Parents' marriage akte `bhi:093f9a8d-0e04-97db-788b-fa69f50f7d23`** (BHIC BS
  Huwelijk **Uden, 21 Feb 1868**): Joannes Wassenberg × Joanna Maria Verwegen →
  `@I484@` × `@I485@` (family `@F168@`). Names both sets of parents (Hendricus sr.'s
  grandparents):
  - Wassenberg side: **Albertus Wassenberg × Joanna van Berlo** → `@I486@` × `@I487@` (family `@F169@`)
  - Verwegen side: **Cornelis Verwegen × Hester van Wijhoven** → `@I488@` × `@I489@` (family `@F170@`)

**Triple corroboration of Hendricus sr.'s parents:** the 1873 birth akte, the 1868
parents' marriage akte, and the groom-parent fields of the 1909 marriage akte
(`bhi:9060aa0f-0679-1911-2631-a2b4be0120bc`) all name Joannes Wassenberg × Joanna
Maria Verwegen.

**⚠ Potential additional endogamous tie — VERWEGEN:** Hendricus sr.'s mother is a
**Verwegen**, and the Verwegen surname is heavily represented in the van Duijnhoven
maternal lines (see `VERWEGEN_3GEN.md`, `VERWEGEN_5GEN_DEEPER.md`,
`VERWEGEN_SIBLINGS_CHILDREN_*.md`, `MEGCHELINA_VERWEGEN_CHILDREN.md`). Joanna Maria
Verwegen (b. ~1840s, m. 1868 Uden, parents Cornelis Verwegen × Hester van Wijhoven)
should be cross-checked against the existing Verwegen tree — this Wassenberg family
may connect to the van Duijnhoven line through the Verwegen surname as well as through
the already-confirmed van Duijnhoven cousin tie. **This is an open lead for a future pass.**

**Evidence strength: STRONG** — parents triple-corroborated; grandparents from the
1868 marriage akte.
