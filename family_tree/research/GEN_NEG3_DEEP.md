**Conclusion:** The Gemert branch descends from **Petrus Cornelius van Duijnhoven (1633)**, which is a DIFFERENT progenitor than our line's **Johannes van Duynhoven (1539)**. The two lines diverged before 1600. They are DISTANT COUSINS, not close relatives.
