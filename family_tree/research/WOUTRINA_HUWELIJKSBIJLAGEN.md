# Woutrina van Duijnhoven — 1859 huwelijksbijlagen investigation (browser)

**Task:** Access the 1859 huwelijksbijlagen (marriage supplements) for the
Peter van der Voort × Petronella Verwegen marriage (Uden, 21 May 1859,
`bhi:70470602-c8b9-2b5a-e042-f39ff1d68936`) to identify **Woutrina van
Duijnhoven's parentage** — the supplement should contain a certified extract of
Peter's birth record and, if required, statements naming the grandparents.

**Verdict:** **PARENTAGE NOT OBTAINED.** The huwelijksbijlagen for Uden 1859 are
**not digitized / not available online**, and the records that *are* online do
not name Woutrina's parents. This browser pass CONFIRMS and extends the earlier
`WOUTRINA_PARENTAGE.md` finding with new direct evidence: I viewed the actual
marriage-akte scan image (not just the A2A index) and confirmed the akte body
records Woutrina only as **"wijle" (the late)** — deceased before the 1859
wedding — with no grandparents named. Evidence strength on parentage:
**cannot be determined (no accessible source).**

## What the marriage akte scan actually says (direct read of aktenummer 27)

Handwritten register body, Uden No. 27, "den een-en-twintigsten dag der maand Mei
1859":

- **Bruidegom:** Peter van der Voort, *jongman*, oud **drie en dertig** jaren, van
  beroep **bouwman**, wonende te Uden. "**Meerderjarige zoon van Joannes van der
  Voort, bouwman mede te Uden woonachtig, en van wijle [= the late] Woutrina van
  Duijnhoven.**" → **Woutrina was already deceased by 21 May 1859.**
- **Bruid:** Petronella Verwegen, *jongedochter*, oud (illegible ~35) jaren, van
  beroep landbouwster, wonende te Uden. "Meerderjarige dochter van Joannes Verwegen
  … en van wijle **Mechelina van den Berk**."
- Overgelegde bewijzen (the documents submitted = what the *bijlagen* contained):
  1° nationale-militie bewijs (groom); 2° **akte van geboorte van den bruidegom** —
  geboren **den dertienden September 1826 te Uden**; 3° akte van geboorte van de
  bruid — geboren **den eersten April 1824 te Uden**; 4° de afkondigingen
  (Zondag 8 en 15 Mei 1859).

The akte body names the parents by name only. It does **not** state Woutrina's
own parents, her birth place, or (since she was dead and the groom was of age =
meerderjarig) any parental-consent clause that would have named grandparents.

## Why the bijlagen can't resolve it here

- **Woutrina was deceased before the wedding**, and the groom was *meerderjarig*
  (of age), so no living-parent consent act was needed → the supplement would
  carry a **certified extract of Peter's 1826 birth akte** (which itself names
  Woutrina without her parents) and possibly a **death extract for Woutrina** —
  but even a death extract of this era typically gives her age/residence, not
  necessarily her own parents.
- The bijlagen are **image-only archival files that are not online.** Reaching
  them requires either an on-site visit / scan-on-request at the BHIC, or reading
  the original **Uden DTB doopregister ~1793–1806** page-by-page (her name is
  unindexed).
