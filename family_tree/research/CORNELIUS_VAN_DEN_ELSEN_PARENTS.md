# Parents of Cornelius van den Elsen (~1770 Boekel)

## Subject
**Cornelius van den Elsen** (c. 1770 – 17 Oct 1811 Boekel)
- Married: Wilhelmina Henrici Bevers, 2 Feb 1798 Boekel
- Son: Gerardus Cornelus van den Elsen (bap. 4 Dec 1798 Boekel) → @I384@

## Confirmed Parents

### Father: Hendrick Jan van den Elsen
**Source:** Death record of Cornelius van den Elsen, 17 Oct 1811, Boekel
- OpenArchieven ID: `f14bf471-e252-898e-5137-171b2ccdfd8f`
- URL: https://www.openarchieven.nl/bhi:f14bf471-e252-898e-5137-171b2ccdfd8f
- Record text: "Overlijden, 17-10-1811, Boekel, N.N., Cornelius van den Elsen, Hendrick Jan van den Elsen"
- Relationships: Vader (Father) = Hendrick Jan van den Elsen; Moeder (Mother) = N.N. (unknown)

### Mother: Unknown (N.N.)
The death record does not name Cornelius's mother.
