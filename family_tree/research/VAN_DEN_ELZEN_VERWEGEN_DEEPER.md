# Van den Elzen & Verwegen Deeper Ancestors Research

## Summary

This research extends the known ancestry one generation back (4th generation from Anna Maria van den Elzen / Geertruda Verwegen).

---

## VAN DEN ELZEN LINE

### Known (3rd generation from Anna Maria):
- **Johannes van den Elzen** (b. 8 Jun 1838 Boekel) x Francijna van der Horst
  - Parents: **Gerardus Cornelus van den Elzen** x **Johanna Tonie Jan Lamers**

### Source Found:
- Marriage record 21-2-1868 Boekel: Johannes van den Elzen x Francijna van der Horst
  - Lists parents: Gerardus Cornelus van den Elzen (father of groom) x Johanna Tonie Jan Lamers (mother of groom)
  - Source: [OpenArchieven bhi:11a0d43a-b90e-0ff3-751b-fa3ebe4f222b](https://www.openarchieven.nl/bhi:11a0d43a-b90e-0ff3-751b-fa3ebe4f222b)

### 4th Generation - NOT FOUND
- Gerardus Cornelus van den Elzen's parents: **UNKNOWN**
- No baptism record found for Gerardus Cornelus (~1800-1815 Boekel)
- No marriage record found for Gerardus Cornelus to Johanna Tonie Jan Lamers
- Evidence strength: **weak** - no primary source located

  - Marriage: 21 Apr 1792 (or 8 May 1792) Boekel
  - Source: [OpenArchieven bhi:68e53276-4629-11e3-a747-d206bceb4d38](https://www.openarchieven.nl/bhi:68e53276-4629-11e3-a747-d206bceb4d38)

    - Father: Franciscus van der Horst, Mother: Catharina Huberti van den Elsen
    - Source: [OpenArchieven bhi:b1eea8dc-739d-3f48-c83b-d08d5f8042ab](https://www.openarchieven.nl/bhi:b1eea8dc-739d-3f48-c83b-d08d5f8042ab)

- Evidence strength: **moderate** - patronymic naming convention is reliable evidence

### 4th Generation - Catharina van den Elsen's father:

## VERWEGEN LINE

### Known (3rd generation from Geertruda):
- **Geertruda Verwegen** (wife of Martinus van Duijnhoven)
  - Parents: **Johannes Verwegen** x **Mechelina van den Berk** (m. 13 Feb 1820)
  - Grandparents: **Geurt Peter Verwegen** x **Leen Gielens** (m. 31 Jan 1773 Boekel)

### Found - Marriage of Geurt Peter Verwegen:
- **Geurt Peter Verwegen** x **Leen Jan Gielens**
  - Marriage: 31 January 1773 Boekel
  - Witnesses: Gerardus van de Burgt, Joannes van der Wist
  - Source: [OpenArchieven bhi:673e6578-4629-11e3-a747-d206bceb4d38](https://www.openarchieven.nl/bhi:673e6578-4629-11e3-a747-d206bceb4d38)

### 4th Generation - Geurt Peter Verwegen's father:
- **Peter Verwegen** (father of Geurt)
  - Deduced from patronymic: "Geurt Peter Verwegen" = Geurt/Gerardus, son of Peter/Petrus
  - Mother: **UNKNOWN**
  - No baptism record found for Geurt Peter (~1740-1755)
- Evidence strength: **moderate** - patronymic naming convention

### 4th Generation - Leen Gielens's father:
- **Jan Gielens** (father of Leen/Helena)
  - Deduced from: "Leen Jan Gielens" = Helena, daughter of Jan
  - Mother: **UNKNOWN**
- Evidence strength: **moderate** - patronymic naming convention

### Confirmation - Johannes Verwegen's parents:
- Marriage record 13-2-1820 Uden confirms:
  - Johannes Verwegen married Anna Maria van der Putten (NOT Mechelina van den Berk)
  - His parents: Geurt Verwegen x Helena Gielens
  - Source: [OpenArchieven bhi:49cdc0ff-908c-454b-9079-2d6a6ad99619](https://www.openarchieven.nl/bhi:49cdc0ff-908c-454b-9079-2d6a6ad99619)

### Note on Mechelina van den Berk:
- The marriage to Mechelina van den Berk (13 Feb 1820) was NOT found in OpenArchieven
- The Johannes Verwegen who married Anna Maria van der Putten (13-2-1820) has parents Geurt Verwegen x Helena Gielens
- This may be a different Johannes Verwegen, or the wife's name was recorded differently

## RESEARCH NOTES

### Patronymic naming system:
In 18th-century Brabant, names followed the pattern: [Given name] [Father's name] [Family name]
- "Franciscus Wilhelmi van der Horst" = Franciscus, son of Wilhelmus, of the van der Horst family
- "Geurt Peter Verwegen" = Geurt (Gerardus), son of Peter (Petrus), of the Verwegen family
- "Leen Jan Gielens" = Leen (Helena), daughter of Jan, of the Gielens family

### Limitations:
1. Pre-1811 records (DTB - Dopen/Trouwen/Begraven) are less consistently indexed
2. Some Boekel parish records appear to have gaps in OpenArchieven
3. No baptism records found for several key individuals (~1740-1780 period)
4. Mothers' names rarely recorded in marriage records of this era

### Next steps for deeper research:
1. Search original parish registers of Boekel (not all may be in OpenArchieven)
2. Search Uden DTB for Verwegen baptisms
3. Look for death/burial records that may list parents
4. Search notarial records for family relationships
5. Clarify the Johannes Verwegen x Mechelina van den Berk marriage vs Anna Maria van der Putten
