## 6. SHANNA VAN DYN HOVEN - Murder Victim (2000)

### Extensive News Coverage - High Profile Case

**Wisconsin Watch / WPR "Open and Shut" Podcast Series (2022)**
"Two decades after high-profile murder trial, questions remain in the prosecution of Ken Hudson"

**Case Details:**
- **Victim:** Shanna Van Dyn Hoven, age 19
- **Date:** June 25, 2000
- **Location:** Plank Road, Kaukauna, WI (while jogging)
- **Circumstances:** Stabbed to death near woods and a quarry
- UW-Madison student (just finished freshman year)
- Loved the color orange, nicknamed "Shanna Banana"
- Memorial with orange lilies erected on Plank Road

**Conviction:**
- **Ken Hudson** convicted of first-degree intentional homicide
- Sentenced: Life in prison plus 73 years
- Sentenced April 3, 2001 by Judge Harold Froehlich
- Additional charges: attempted kidnapping, attempted homicide of witness David Carnot, reckless endangerment (16-mile chase)
- Prosecutor: Outagamie County DA Vince Biskupic

**Ongoing Appeals:**
- Hudson has maintained innocence
- Multiple appeals filed raising questions about prosecution
- Won new round of DNA testing
- Wisconsin Innocence Project involved
- Latest appeal filed August 2023 (Fox 11)

**Legal Records:**
- VAN DYN HOVEN v. PEKIN INSURANCE COMPANY (2002) - Wisconsin Court of Appeals case
- Multiple appeal decisions documented through Wisconsin Courts

**News Sources:**
- Wisconsin Watch: https://wisconsinwatch.org/2022/05/openandshut-episode-5-the-circus/
- WPR: https://www.wpr.org/shows/openandshut/
- Fox 11: https://fox11online.com/news/local/
- Urban Milwaukee, Wausau Pilot & Review, WNA News (syndicated coverage)
