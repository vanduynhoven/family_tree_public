## Research Notes

### Key Discovery: Location is UDEN, not Schijndel
Previous research assumed Schijndel area. The baptism and burial records clearly place this family in **Uden**, Noord-Brabant.

### Patronymic Naming Convention
In 18th century Noord-Brabant, middle names indicated parentage:
- **Henricus Petri/Peters** = Henricus, son of Petrus
- **Joanna Lamberti/Lambertus** = Joanna, daughter of Lambertus
