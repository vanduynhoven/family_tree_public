## Research Notes

### Patronymic Naming Convention
In 18th century Noord-Brabant, middle names often indicated parentage:
- **Henricus Peters** = Henricus, son of Peter
- **Joanna Lambertus** = Joanna, daughter of Lambertus

This provides circumstantial evidence for the names of Elisabeth's grandparents.
