## Why This is the WRONG Record

### 1. Different Parents
- **Record says:** Father = Antoon van der Heijden, Mother = Anna van der Rijt
- **Our Johanna:** Father = Joannes Henrici van der Heijden, Mother = Anna Maria Pittens
- These are completely different families!

### 2. Different Husband
- **Record says:** Echtgenoot (spouse) = Johannes van der Pol
- **Our Johanna:** Husband = Petrus van Duijnhoven
- Petrus van Duijnhoven was alive until 13 Mar 1882 - if our Johanna died in 1872, she would be listed as wife of Petrus van Duijnhoven, NOT as widow of Johannes van der Pol.

### 3. Corroborating Evidence: Johannes van der Pol Death Record

Found in OpenArchieven (bhi:b059b4cd-df37-989a-6ae3-fcd931534ec6):

| Field | Value |

This proves Johannes van der Pol died in 1856, leaving his wife Johanna van der Heijden (daughter of Antoon) a widow. That same Johanna then died in 1872, listed as widow of Johannes van der Pol.

### 4. The OTHER Johanna's Later History

The WieWasWie research found that the OTHER Johanna van der Heijden (widow of Johannes van der Pol) likely remarried:
- Marriage (19-05-1877 in Uden) - probably to Franciscus Goossens
- But wait - she died in 1872? This needs investigation.

Actually, this suggests there may be MULTIPLE "Johanna van der Heijden" in Uden. The one who died in 1872 (akte 56) could be a different person from one who married Franciscus Goossens.
