# Gen2 Uncle/Aunt Research: Antonius van Duijnhoven (b.1865)

## Summary

Antonius van Duijnhoven (b. 16 Dec 1865, Uden) was the brother of Marianus van Duijnhoven (Gen3 ancestor). He is the **uncle** of Johan/John van Duijnhoven (Arthur's grandfather).

Antonius married **twice** and had at least **13 children** between the two marriages.

---

## Cousin Relationships

The children of Antonius are **first cousins** to:
- Johan/John van Duijnhoven (Arthur's grandfather)
- All other children of Marianus van Duijnhoven

This makes their descendants Arthur's **first cousins once removed** or **second cousins**.
