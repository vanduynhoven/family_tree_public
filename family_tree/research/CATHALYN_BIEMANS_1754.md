## Summary

Cathalyn Janse Biemans (of **Bakel**) married Jan Peeters van Duynhoven (of
**Stiphout**) on **23 June 1754** at the Bakel *schepenbank* (civil bench). The
couple then settled in **Stiphout**, where at least **three children** were
baptized (1757–1764). The groom is **not** from the Bakel van Duijnhoven line —
he originates in Stiphout — so this branch links the Biemans family into a
**Stiphout** van Duynhoven family, distinct from our main Bakel line.

## 3. Connection to our GEDCOM lines

- **NOT the Bakel van Duijnhoven main line.** Our main line is Bakel-based
  (Petrus 1799 ← Petrus Johannes 1748 ← Joannes Peter 1722 ← Petrus Jansen 1679).
  This groom, **Joannes Petri van Duijnhoven**, was born and lived in **Stiphout**
  — a separate van Duynhoven family. The 1754 marriage therefore documents a
  **Biemans → Stiphout-van-Duynhoven** union, not a Bakel-van-Duijnhoven event.
- **Biemans side (the real link to our tree):** Cathalyn was likely a sister of
  **Judocus Biemans** (b. ~1735–1750, Bakel), who married Josina Biemans —
  ancestors of the **Joost van de Weyer** line in our GEDCOM. The 1757 godparent
  "Wilhelma Biemans" and the recurring Bakel Biemans patronym "Joannis/Janse"
  support Cathalyn belonging to that Bakel Biemans sibling group (children of a
  Jan/Joannes Biemans of Bakel). Confirming Cathalyn ↔ Judocus as siblings
  requires the Biemans **baptism** records (a shared father Jan/Joannes Biemans).
