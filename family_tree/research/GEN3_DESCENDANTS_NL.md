## IMPORTANT: The Two Johanna van Duijnhovens

This research uncovered a remarkable coincidence that requires clarification:

### The OTHER Johanna van Duijnhoven (mother of groom Hendricus Johannes Wassenberg)

**Marriage:** 12 February 1909, Uden  
Johanna van Duijnhoven x Hendricus Wassenberg

**Parents of this Johanna:**
- Hendricus van Duijnhoven (father)
- Anna Maria van der Wijst (mother)

**Children of Hendricus Wassenberg x Johanna van Duijnhoven (Uden):**
1. Hendricus Johannes Wassenberg (b. 7 Mar 1911, Uden) — married Hendrica Maria van Duijnhoven
2. Anna Maria Wassenberg (b. 28 Jan 1913, Uden)
3. Martina Geerdina Wassenberg (b. 12 Jan 1915, Uden)

**Relationship to Our Family:**
The Johanna van Duijnhoven who is the mother-in-law of Hendrica Maria belongs to a DIFFERENT van Duijnhoven branch:
- Her father was Hendricus van Duijnhoven (married to Anna Maria van der Wijst)
- Our family's patriarch was Marianus van Duijnhoven (married to Anna Maria van den Elzen)

These may be distant cousins (common in a small region like Uden/Boekel), but they are NOT the same person as our family's Johanna van Duijnhoven (b. 1914).
