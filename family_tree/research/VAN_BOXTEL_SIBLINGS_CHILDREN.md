# Van Boxtel Siblings of Uden - Marriages and Children

## Background

**Parents:** Henricus Peters van Boxtel (d. 21 Feb 1789, Uden) × Joanna Lamberti van Ham

**Known siblings of Elisabeth Henricus van Boxtel (~1767-1856):**

## 1. LAMBERDINA (HENRICI) VAN BOXTEL

**Baptism:** 7 October 1768, Uden

### Marriage
- **Date:** 6 June 1797, Uden (RC)
- **Spouse:** Joannes Leonardi (wonende te Uden)
- **Witnesses:** Wilhelmus van der Meir (reverendus dominus), Arnoldus van Beckkum
- **Source:** [BHIC DTB Trouwen, archief 1456, inv. 16, folio 90v02](https://www.openarchieven.nl/bhi:2899cff4-c04f-1ac1-37dd-4b1ebe5ba57a)

### Known Children

| Child | Baptism Date | Place | Notes |
|-------|--------------|-------|-------|
| **Wilhelmus Leonardi** | 5 Nov 1800 | Uden | Father noted as "pater extraneus" (father from elsewhere). Godparents: Gerardus van Wijck, Jacomina Henrici van Boxtel |

**Source:** [BHIC DTB Dopen, archief 1456, inv. 12, folio 153r02](https://www.openarchieven.nl/bhi:62654ed5-0d96-4f3a-1e14-db91f05dec9c)

### Research Notes
- Jacomina Henrici van Boxtel appears as godmother - possibly another sibling not previously identified
- "Pater extraneus" suggests Joannes Leonardi may have come from outside Uden parish

## 3. PETRONILLA (HENRICI) VAN BOXTEL

**Baptism:** 27 September 1774, Uden

### Status: DIED YOUNG - MULTIPLE BURIALS FOUND

| Event | Date | Notes |
|-------|------|-------|
| Burial | 5 Jul 1779 | Petronilla Henrici van Boxtel |
| Burial | 31 Aug 1781 | Petronilla Henrici van Boxtel |
| Baptism | 30 Nov 1782 | Petronilla Henrici van Boxtel (new child with same name) |
| Burial | 13 Jun 1789 | Petronilla Henrici van Boxtel |

### Research Notes
- The family had at least two daughters named Petronilla (common practice when first child died)
- First Petronilla (bap. 1774) likely died by Jul 1779
- Second Petronilla (bap. 30 Nov 1782) died 13 Jun 1789
- **No surviving Petronilla van Boxtel appears to have married**

**Note:** A "Petronilla Joannis van Boxtel" married 10 May 1799 - this is a different family (Joannis patronymic, not Henrici)

## 4. GERTRUDIS (HENRICI) VAN BOXTEL [@I229@]

**Baptism:** 26 September 1776, Uden

### Marriage
- **Date:** 15 July 1805, Uden (RC)
- **Spouse:** Anthonius Arnoldi de Groot (wonende te "wedn" - widower)
- **Witnesses:** Petro and Arnoldo van Beckkum
- **Source:** [BHIC DTB Trouwen, archief 1456, inv. 17, folio 4r](https://www.openarchieven.nl/bhi:4cf7bf80-ab62-9e1b-8f27-bfbbc10afe3e)

### Notes on Spouse
Anthonius Arnoldi de Groot was previously married. Records show:
- Marriage 1: 3 Jun 1800 (DTB Trouwen)
- First wife died; burial 11 Aug 1801
- Children from first marriage: baptisms 15 Oct 1798, 30 Jun 1801, 21 Aug 1802

### Known Children (with Gertrudis)
**Research needed** - Anthonius de Groot appears as godfather in 4 Jan 1807 baptism of nephew Theodorus Verhoeven (child of Gerardus Verhoeven × Maria Arnoldi de Groot - his sister's child).

Children baptisms after 1805 likely include those of Gertrudis van Boxtel.

**Need further research:** Search for de Groot children with mother "Gertrudis" after July 1805.

## REFERENCE: ELISABETH (HENRICI) VAN BOXTEL (THE KNOWN SIBLING)

**Baptism:** ~1767, Uden

### Marriage
- **Date:** 21 November 1796, Uden (RC)
- **Spouse:** Petrus Joannis van Duijnhoven (geboren te Uden)
- **Witnesses:** Arnoldus van Beckkum, Joannes van der Velden
- **Source:** [BHIC DTB Trouwen, archief 1456, inv. 16, folio 86r04](https://www.openarchieven.nl/bhi:d4e6d746-04ea-14a0-0f38-b695bdf431ec)

### Known Children

| Child | Baptism Date | Place | Godparents | Notes |
|-------|--------------|-------|------------|-------|
| **Joannes van Duijnhoven** | 17 Dec 1797 | Uden | Joannes Leonardi, Anthonetta Franscisci van Scheijndel | Firstborn son |
| **Petrus van Duijnhoven** | 27 Aug 1799 | Uden | Joannes Henrici van Boxtel, Anna Maria Joannis van Duijnhoven | Father noted as "al overleden" (already deceased!) |

**Sources:** 
- [BHIC DTB Dopen, inv. 12, folio 125v04](https://www.openarchieven.nl/bhi:cad419ab-1fc3-ec69-16a5-30e381f80cf0)
- [BHIC DTB Dopen, inv. 12, folio 141r07](https://www.openarchieven.nl/bhi:48f86655-d2c0-585b-282c-7379cfa41dc3)

### Important Discovery
**Petrus Joannis van Duijnhoven died between December 1797 and August 1799** - Elisabeth was a widow by the time her second son was born.

Elisabeth appears as godmother in later records:
- 30 Oct 1804: godmother to Anthonius van den Broek
- 20 Apr 1810: godmother to Theodorus van den Broek
