## Other Verwegen-van Duijnhoven Connections

The research confirms THREE separate Verwegen women married into the van Duijnhoven family:

1. **Ardina Verwegen** - Married Joannes van Duijnhoven, 26 January 1856, Uden
   - Sister of Geertruda
   - Parents: Joannes Verwegen & Mechelina van den Berk
   - Source: BHIC 645958b1-7cc4-bb66-bafa-35c46257bf42

2. **Geertruda Verwegen** - Married Martinus van Duijnhoven, 17 February 1858, Uden
   - Subject of this research
   - Parents: Johannes Verwegen & Mechelina van den Berk
   - Source: BHIC 056b95f5-6d55-d22a-95e7-1a87ac4d04c4

3. **Megchelina Verwegen** - Married Petrus (Cornelus) van Duijnhoven
   - Born: 25 July 1871, Uden
   - Parents: Adrianus Verwegen & Everdina van der Wijst
   - Source: BHIC BS Geboorte 72f2a1fb-899d-47d0-63f5-460bfe4f47ce
   - **Note:** Different Verwegen family line (Adrianus, not Johannes)

Also noted: **Antonius Verwegen** (brother of Geertruda) married **Petronella van Duijnhoven** in 1860.

## Research Gaps & Further Investigation Needed

1. **Johannes Verwegen's Parents:** The marriage of Johannes Verwegen to Mechelina van den Berk (~1821) predates most digitized civil records. Church (DTB) records from 1795-1821 need examination.

2. **Mechelina van den Berk's Parents:** No birth or baptism record found. Church records from ~1795-1800 in Uden or surrounding parishes needed.

3. **Generation 3 Confirmation:** The connection to Geurt Peter Verwegen & Helena Gielens is POSSIBLE but UNCONFIRMED for Geertruda's line.

4. **Children of siblings:** Full documentation of Geertruda's nieces and nephews through her 9+ siblings.
