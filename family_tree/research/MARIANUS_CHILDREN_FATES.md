## Why the gaps persist & recommended next steps

1. **Dutch privacy windows.** These four were born 1909–1916. Marriages are typically indexed only after 75 years and deaths after ~50 years; many post-1940 events are simply not yet in OpenArchieven/WieWasWie.
2. **Name frequency.** "van Duijnhoven" + common given names (Johanna Maria, Gerardus, Antonius, Hubertus) produce hundreds of NL-wide false positives; only parent-verified detail records are trustworthy, and few survive that filter.
3. **Recommended manual follow-ups:**
   - Consult the **Boekel Bevolkingsregister 1920–1938 household folio** for the Marianus van Duijnhoven family — it should record each child's departure (marriage/emigration) or death and destination municipality, which then points to the exact marriage/death akte.
   - Query **WieWasWie** logged-in with an explicit parent filter (father=Marianus, mother=Anna Maria van den Elzen).
   - Check **BHIC** archive 50 (Boekel BS Huwelijk/Overlijden) directly for 1930s–1980s.
   - Family **bidprentjes** (memorial cards) collections (vhg/nba/idm) for exact death dates once a municipality is known.
