# VERBRUGGEN and VAN GROOTEL Family Research

## Summary

Research into the VERBRUGGEN and VAN GROOTEL families, ancestors of Anna Maria Verbruggen (b. 14 October 1877, Bakel), going back 3 generations from her parents.

- All locations are in the former municipality of **Bakel en Milheeze** (now part of Gemert-Bakel), Noord-Brabant, Netherlands
