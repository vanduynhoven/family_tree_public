# Shanna Van Dyn Hoven Research

## Summary

**Shanna Marie Van Dyn Hoven** (b. ~1981, d. June 25, 2000) was murdered in Kaukauna, WI at age 19. Her parents are **Steven and Nancy Van Dyn Hoven** of Kaukauna.

### KEY FINDING: Likely from TONY (Anton) Line

Based on the evidence, Steven Van Dyn Hoven is the son of **Al (Albert) and Ceil (Cecilia) Van Dyn Hoven** of Kaukauna. The Rosella E. Brandt obituary (2002) lists "Al and Ceil Van Dyn Hoven of Kaukauna and their children: Kevin (Deb), Lawrence (Deb), Dennis (Nancy), **Stephen (Nancy)**, Joe (Beth), Kris (Ann) Sanders, and Don (Amy) Hurlbutt."

Since "Al" is distinct from Albert Jr. (who married Rosella Vanden Heuvel and had children including Clyde), Al must descend from one of the two undocumented brothers: **Anton (Tony) or Gus**. Given the naming pattern (Al could be Albert/Alfred, a common name passed down), the most likely scenario is:

**Anton (Tony) Van Dyn Hoven x Agnes** → **Al (Albert/Alfred) Van Dyn Hoven x Ceil** → **Stephen Van Dyn Hoven x Nancy** → **Shanna Marie Van Dyn Hoven**

## Family Tree Position Analysis

### The Seven Brothers of Albert Sr.
From Wilbert Van Dyn Hoven's obituary (2007):
> "He was preceded in death by his parents, brothers Anton (Agnes), Albert (Rosella), Hank (Florence), Frank (Ruth), Gus (Bernice), John (Lillian)"

The seven sons of Albert Van Dyn Hoven Sr. x Adriana Vande Wettering:
1. **Anton "Tony" (x Agnes)** - UNDOCUMENTED descendants
2. **Albert Jr. (x Rosella Vanden Heuvel)** - Clyde's line documented
3. **Hank (x Florence Romenesko)** - Raymond "Wimpy" line documented  
4. **Frank (x Ruth, later Helena Spierings?)** - Lyle line documented
5. **Gus (x Bernice)** - UNDOCUMENTED descendants
6. **John (x Lillian Romenesko)** - Diane/Nancy line documented
7. **Wilbert (x Grace Hietpas)** - Jerry/Gerald/Joyce line documented

### Why Tony is Most Likely

1. **Al is NOT from Albert Jr.'s line**: Albert Jr. x Rosella Vanden Heuvel had children including Clyde, LaVerne, Virginia, Alice, Audrey, and Carol - NO child named Al/Albert.

2. **Al could be from Tony or Gus**: These are the two undocumented branches. A son named "Al" (Albert or Alfred) would be consistent with family naming patterns.

3. **Tony (Anton) is the eldest brother**: More likely to name a son after the patriarch Albert Sr.

4. **Location match**: The "Al and Ceil Van Dyn Hoven of Kaukauna" - Kaukauna is where Tony and the other brothers lived.

## Murder Case Summary

- **Perpetrator**: Kenneth Hudson (convicted 2001)
- **Sentence**: Life in prison plus 73 years
- **Presiding Judge**: Outagamie County Circuit Judge Harold Froehlich
- **Prosecutor**: Outagamie County District Attorney Vince Biskupic
- **Status**: Hudson has maintained innocence and has filed multiple appeals; granted new DNA testing; case featured in Wisconsin Watch "Open and Shut" podcast series

### Case Details
- Shanna was jogging on Plank Road near a quarry
- Neighbor David Carnot heard screaming and found her bleeding
- Hudson fled in his truck, hitting Carnot and a fence
- Police pursued Hudson in a 16-mile chase
- Hudson had a bloody knife in truck; blood on his body
- Hudson represented himself at trial after multiple attorneys withdrew/were fired
- Significant controversy over police conduct and evidence handling
