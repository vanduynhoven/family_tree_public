## Geographic Context

### Bakel en Milheeze

- A former municipality in Noord-Brabant, Netherlands
- Merged into Gemeente Gemert-Bakel in 1997
- Roman Catholic region with strong agricultural traditions
- The Cornelissen and Verbruggen families were prominent local surnames

## Notes

- The Cornelissen surname derives from the patronymic "son of Cornelis" and is very common in Noord-Brabant
- The Verbruggen surname means "from the bridges" and is also common in this region
- Both families were likely Roman Catholic farmers in the Bakel area
- Marriage occurred late (Anna Maria was 34, Johan was 34) - possibly delayed by WWII or other circumstances
