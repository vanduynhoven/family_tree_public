## Arts & Culture

### Martin van Duynhoven - Jazz Drummer & Artist
**Born:** June 13, 1942, Boxmeer, Netherlands  
**Professions:** Drummer, graphic designer, drawer, etcher, lithographer, musician, photographer

**Musical Career:**
- One of the four most renowned Dutch jazz drummers alongside Han Bennink, John Engels, and Pierre Courbois
- Part of the bebop generation of the 1960s-70s
- Member of the quartet BCDE with Bennink, Courbois, and Engels
- Studied 1959-1962 at Tilburg Conservatory and Den Bosch Art Academy
- First musical experience in a marching band (Harmonieorchester) in Uden

**Art Collections:**
- Works held at International Institute for Social History (IISG)
- Works held at Stedelijk Museum Amsterdam

## Business & Industry

### Harry van Duijnhoven - Poultry Genetics Pioneer
**Location:** Stevensbeek, Netherlands  
**Wife:** Nora van Duijnhoven

**Achievement:**
- **Co-founder of Bovans Poultry Breeders** (1954)
- The breeding center was based at his farm in Stevensbeek
- Founded with four Dutch families: Bongers, Van Duijnhoven, Van Lankveld, and Van der Linden
- Company name "Bovans" = one "Bo" + three "Vans"
- Bovans is now part of **Hendrix Genetics**, a leading global multi-species animal genetics company
- Bovans laying hen breeds (Bovans Brown, Bovans White, Bovans Black) are recognized globally for egg production quality

**Legacy:**
- Bovans breeds are among the world's top-performing laying hens
- Used globally from barn/aviary systems to backyard farming
