# Van Boxtel & Van Ham - Extended Ancestry Research

## Summary

Research into 5 additional generations for the Van Boxtel and Van Ham families connected to Elisabeth Henricus van Boxtel (~1767-1856), wife of Petrus Johannes van Duijnhoven.

---

## VAN BOXTEL LINE

### Generation -2: Henricus Petri van Boxtel
- **Death:** 21 February 1789, Uden (confirmed)
- **Patronymic:** "Petri" confirms his father was named Petrus/Peter
- **Spouse:** Joanna Lamberti van Ham
- **Source:** BHIC DTB Begraven, identifier `049afe85-54f7-f711-581a-8fac7d8acf18`

### Generation -3: Petrus van Boxtel (father of Henricus)
- **Estimated birth:** ~1700-1710
- **Location:** Likely Uden or Oirschot area
- **Children with patronymic "Petri" found in Uden/Oirschot:**
  - Multiple "Petri van Boxtel" children recorded 1720s-1760s in Oirschot
  - Adrianus Petrus van Boxtel married 26-1-1721 in Oirschot to Catharina Adrianus van de Loo
  - Children baptized in Oirschot 1723-1736

### Generation -4: (Petrus's father) - UNCERTAIN
- The patronymic system indicates Petrus's father could be identified from his own patronymic
- Petrus Adrianus van Boxtel married 16-10-1684 in Oirschot to Peternilla Dielis van de Veen
  - This could be the right generation (children ~1685-1720)
- If this is correct: **Adrianus van Boxtel** would be Gen -4

### Earlier Van Boxtel Records (potential ancestors):
- **Henricus Arnoldi van Boxtel** married 5-5-1670 in Vught to Wilhelma Joannis van Cromvoort
- **Henricus Adriani van Boxtel** married 3-11-1633 (patronymic = son of Adrianus)
- **Joannes Petrus van Boxtel** married 20-8-1641 in Oirschot to Maria Petrus van Collenberg
- **Adam Lambertus van Boxtel** baptized 21-9-1653 in Breda
  - Father: Wilhelmus Bartholomei van Boxtel
  - Mother: Maria Rolandi
- **Adam Michaelis van Boxtel** baptized 9-2-1648 in Breda
  - Father: Franciscus Michiels van Boxtel

## VAN HAM LINE

### Generation -2: Joanna Lamberti van Ham (mother of Elisabeth)
- **Father:** Lambertus van Ham (confirmed by patronymic "Lamberti")
- **Active in Uden:** 1774-1782 (appears as godmother/witness)
- **Siblings confirmed (children of Lambertus van Ham):**
  - Daniel Lamberti van Ham (multiple records 1774-1782)
  - Agnes Lamberti van Ham (1773, 1775, 1782)
  - Henricus Lamberti van Ham (1777, 1782)
  - Jacobus Lamberti van Ham (1774-1788 multiple)
  - Gertrudis Lamberti van Ham (1774)

### Generation -3: Lambertus van Ham
- **Marriage:** 21-1-1686 in Veghel
- **Full name:** Lambertus Adriani van Ham
- **Spouse:** Joanna Arnoldi vant Haenuelt
- **Patronymic:** "Adriani" confirms his father was Adrianus
- **Children found in Best/Rijsbergen (1688-1712):**
  - Adrianus Lambertus P. van Ham - bapt. 17-2-1688
  - Anna M. Lambertus P. van Ham - bapt. 27-7-1690
  - Thomas Lambertus van Ham - bapt. 30-7-1712 in Best
  - Anna Lambertus van Ham - bapt. 30-7-1712 in Best
- **Source:** BHIC DTB Trouwen, identifier `8cb1858d-a66a-42c9-772a-a7f86a6f58b0`

### Generation -4: Adrianus van Ham (father of Lambertus)
- **Estimated birth:** ~1630-1640
- **Active in:** Rijsbergen/Veghel area
- **Marriage found:** 20-1-1619 in Rijsbergen
  - **Adrianus Adrianus van Ham** x Andrea Boudewinus
  - This indicates his father was ALSO named Adrianus
- **Source:** BHIC DTB Trouwen, identifier `174c3058-9c07-d459-28be-94d1f838dd20`

### Generation -5: Adrianus van Ham (grandfather of Lambertus)
- **Estimated birth:** ~1590-1600
- **Location:** Rijsbergen area (West-Brabant)
- Son married in 1619 as "Adrianus Adrianus van Ham"
- This represents the EARLIEST confirmed Van Ham ancestor

### Earlier Van Ham Records:

## RESEARCH NOTES

### Limitations:
- DTB records before ~1620 are sparse or non-existent for these areas
- The patronymic system (father's name as middle name) helps trace lineage but requires interpretation
- Multiple individuals with similar names in the same time period require careful cross-referencing

### Further Research Needed:
1. **Van Boxtel:** Confirm the connection between Petrus van Boxtel (Gen -3) and specific marriage/baptism records
2. **Van Boxtel:** Find Gen -5 and -6 through Schepenbank (aldermen's court) records or notarial acts
3. **Van Ham:** Search for records before 1619 in Rijsbergen municipal archives
4. **Elisabeth's birth:** Still need to locate Elisabeth van Boxtel's baptism record (~1767)

### Geographic Migration:
- **Van Ham:** Originated in Rijsbergen (West-Brabant), migrated to Veghel, then Uden
- **Van Boxtel:** Active in Oirschot/Vught area, with branch in Uden
