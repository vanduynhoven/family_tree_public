# Wassenberg × van Duijnhoven Connection — CONFIRMED ENDOGAMOUS

**Question:** Which "Johanna van Duijnhoven" was the mother of Hendricus Johannes
Wassenberg, who married Hendrica Maria "Riek" van Duijnhoven (@I018@) on 4 Jan 1946
in Uden? Is she from the same family — i.e. is this a cousin marriage?

**Answer:** She is **NOT** either of Marianus's daughters (I012/I013). She is
**Johanna van Duijnhoven, daughter of Hendricus van Duijnhoven (1837–1910, @I053@)
and Anna Maria van der Wijst** — and Hendricus was the **brother of Martinus**
(Gen 2), making Johanna a **first cousin of Marianus (@I005@)**. The 1946 marriage
was therefore **endogamous: the groom and bride are SECOND COUSINS**, both
great-grandchildren of Gen 1 patriarch Petrus van Duijnhoven (1799–1882) ×
Johanna van der Heijden (1798–~1875).
