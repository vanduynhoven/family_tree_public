# Gen 3: Petrus Cornelus van Duijnhoven Research

## Important Clarification

Research reveals **TWO different individuals named Petrus** in this generation:

1. **Petrus Cornelus van Duijnhoven** (b. 4 May 1863) - son of Martinus x Elisabeth van Schijndel
2. **Petrus van Duijnhoven** (b. ~1875?) - who married Megchelina Verwegen

The task mentioned "Petrus Cornelus (b.3 May 1867)" but BHIC records show Petrus Cornelus was actually born **4 May 1863** (not 1867), and he married **Petronella Donkers** (not Megchelina Verwegen).

## Verwegen Connection Notes

This family has **three Verwegen connections**:
1. **Geertrui Verwegen** - second wife of Martinus van Duijnhoven (1829), mother of Marianus (Gen3 ancestor)
2. **Megchelina Verwegen** (b. 1871) - wife of Petrus van Duijnhoven (Erp line)
3. **Wilhelmina Verwegen** - married Hendricus van Duijnhoven on 27 May 1904 in Uden (bhi:02b84969-c3fa-605f-0c02-dffc9108fca9)

The Verwegen families are from Boekel/Uden area - likely related.
