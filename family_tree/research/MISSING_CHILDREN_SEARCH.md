## OBSERVATION: NAME PATTERNS

The family shows typical Dutch Catholic naming conventions:
- **Martinus** (1908) - Named after paternal grandfather or saint
- **Johanna Maria** (1913) followed by **Johanna** (1914) - Very close births, possibly first Johanna Maria died young (no death record found) and name reused
- **Gerardus** (1909) and **Gerarda Maria** (1919) - Male/female variants
- **Jan** (1912) and **Johan** (1915) - Variants of Johannes
