# Cornelis Verwegen (@I497@) — Parentage Investigation #2

**Date:** 2026-08-31
**Question:** Is Cornelis Verwegen (@I497@, b.ABT 1827 Uden, husband of Hester van Wijhoven,
father of Joanna Maria Verwegen) a child of Johannes Verwegen × Mechelina van den Berk (@F026@,
married 31 Jan 1819 Uden)?

## Conclusion

**NO LINK CAN BE MADE.** No primary source names Cornelis Verwegen (@I497@)'s own parents, and
**no birth or baptism record for any Cornelis Verwegen born in the 1820s exists in the
OpenArchieven dataset** (BHIC / Uden and all archives). The @F026@ hypothesis is **unsupported**
by any record, and a new **contradiction in @I497@'s own record** surfaced during this
investigation that should be resolved first. `@F026@ CHIL @I497@` was **NOT** written.

### 4. The only ~1827-era Cornelis Verwegen is an OLDER man (b.1775)
Uden marriage 2 Mar 1827 (bhi:47786d8b-9e37-1770-1f80-2f1682331d2e):
- Groom **Cornelis Verwegen, b. 18 Sep 1775**, son of **Gerardus Verwegen × Helena Gielens**
  (the "Geurt Peter × Helena Gielens" line), married **Joanna van Berlo** (b. 20 Jun 1797).

This Cornelis (b.1775) is a different person from @F026@'s children and is far too old to be a
child of the 1819 marriage. He is the likely father of a Joanna Maria Verwegen — see §5.

### 5. ⚠️ NEW CONTRADICTION in @I497@'s record (mother mismatch)
The 1833 **birth** akte of Joanna Maria Verwegen conflicts with the 1868 **marriage** akte on the
mother's identity:
- **Birth akte** — BHIC BS Geboorte, Geboorteregister Uden 1833, **akte 151**, 11 Nov 1833
  (bhi:87dad4ee-9afb-9204-f594-2b3ea4c91cac):
  Child **Joanna Maria Verwegen, b. 11 Nov 1833**; Father **Cornelis Verwegen**;
  Mother **Joanna van Berlo** — *NOT* Hester van Wijhoven.
- **Marriage akte (1868)** — same bride, same birth date (11 Nov 1833), but mother recorded as
  **Hester van Wijhoven**.

Same name + same birth date (11 Nov 1833) but two different mothers named across two akten. Two
readings are possible and CANNOT be distinguished from the indexed data alone:
  (a) One woman, one of the two akten mis-records the mother (the 1868 marriage index may have
      confused Hester van Wijhoven — who appears in that same akte's neighbourhood — with the true
      mother Joanna van Berlo); OR
  (b) Two different Joanna Maria Verwegens sharing the 11 Nov 1833 birth date, i.e. @I497@'s
      Cornelis is a *different* Cornelis from the 1775 one who married Joanna van Berlo.
The 1833 birth akte (a primary source at the event) favours **Joanna van Berlo** as the true
mother, which would tie @I497@ to the **Cornelis b.1775** (Gerardus × Helena Gielens line), NOT
to @F026@.

## Why the link was NOT written
1. **No positive evidence** ties Cornelis (@I497@) to @F026@ — no birth akte, no baptism, no
   record naming Johannes Verwegen / Mechelina van den Berk as his parents.
2. **No 1820s Cornelis Verwegen child record exists at all** in the dataset.
3. The **only concrete lead points elsewhere**: @I497@ married Hester van Wijhoven per the 1868
   akte, yet his daughter's 1833 birth akte names the mother as **Joanna van Berlo**, associating
   him with the **Cornelis b.1775 (Gerardus Verwegen × Helena Gielens)** line rather than with
   Johannes × Mechelina.

Recording `@F026@ CHIL @I497@` now would assert an unsupported and probably wrong parentage.
