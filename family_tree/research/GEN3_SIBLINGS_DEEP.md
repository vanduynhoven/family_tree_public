## Louis van Duijnhoven Investigation

The newspaper article mentioned "Louis van Duijnhoven" receiving Tonsuur (first step to priesthood) on 18 December 1948 in Den Bosch from Bishop Mutsaerts. He was a Frater of the Oblates of St. Francis de Sales.

### Search Results:
- **No birth record found** for "Louis van Duijnhoven" in BS Geboorte
- **No marriage records** (expected if he became a priest)
- **Bidprentje (memorial card) found:**
  - **Death:** 27 July 1990, Goirle
  - **Records:**
    - [hwh:616aa2a2-6872-4532-c29d-bd618cf91a30](https://www.openarchieven.nl/hwh:616aa2a2-6872-4532-c29d-bd618cf91a30) (Heemkundekring Weerderheem)
    - [rat:cd6c75f8-3865-11e0-bcd1-8edf61960649](https://www.openarchieven.nl/rat:cd6c75f8-3865-11e0-bcd1-8edf61960649) (Regionaal Archief Tilburg)
    - [vhg:69cd380e-0b81-11ea-9178-d89d6717b464](https://www.openarchieven.nl/vhg:69cd380e-0b81-11ea-9178-d89d6717b464) (Stichting Erfgoed Goirle)

### Analysis:
- "Louis" may have been a religious name taken when entering the order
- The Oblates of St. Francis de Sales had a house in Goirle (where he died)
- He could be one of the 12 siblings using a different name
- Possible candidates: Hubertus, Jan, or Marinus (all males without found marriage records)
- Birth would likely be 1920s-1930s based on 1948 Tonsuur date
- **Marinus (b. 1920)** would be 28 in 1948 - reasonable age for ordination
