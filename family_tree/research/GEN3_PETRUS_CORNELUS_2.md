# Petrus (Cornelus) van Duijnhoven (b. 3 May 1867) - Gen 3

## Summary

**Petrus van Duijnhoven** (recorded as "Petrus" without middle name in most records, but possibly "Petrus Cornelus" - needs verification) was born 3 May 1867 in Uden. He is the son of **Martinus van Duijnhoven** (Gen 2, b.1829) and **Geertruda Verwegen**.

He married **Megchelina Verwegen** (b. 25 Jul 1871, Uden - daughter of Adrianus Verwegen x Everdina van der Wijst). They had at least 8 children, with 3 dying in infancy.

**Note:** There are TWO Petrus Cornelus van Duijnhovens in this generation:
1. **Petrus Cornelus (b. 4 May 1863)** - son of Martinus x **Elisabeth van Schijndel**, married **Petronella Donkers**
2. **Petrus (b. 3 May 1867)** - son of Martinus x **Geertruda Verwegen**, married **Megchelina Verwegen** ← THIS FILE

## Death Record

**NOT FOUND** in OpenArchieven/BHIC. Petrus was still alive in July 1947 (listed as father at children's marriages). Death likely occurred after 1947, possibly beyond the digitized record range or in a different municipality.

## Research Notes

1. **Marriage record of Petrus x Megchelina:** NOT FOUND in OpenArchieven. First child born Aug 1904, so marriage likely 1903-1904.

2. **Petrus death record:** NOT FOUND. He was alive in July 1947 (age ~80). Death may be:
   - In a different municipality archive
   - Beyond digitized date range
   - Recorded under variation of name

3. **Name variation:** In children's birth/marriage records, father consistently recorded as "Petrus van Duijnhoven" without "Cornelus" middle name. The middle name "Cornelus" from earlier research may be incorrect, or used only in formal documents.

4. **Verwegen connection:** Both Petrus's mother (Geertruda Verwegen) and wife (Megchelina Verwegen) are from the Verwegen family. Megchelina is daughter of Adrianus Verwegen x Everdina van der Wijst.
