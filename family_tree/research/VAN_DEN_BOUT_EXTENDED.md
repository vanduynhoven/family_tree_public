## Dual Surname Interpretation

The "van den Bout" / "van Haandel" dual naming suggests several possibilities:

1. **Farm Name Alias**: "Van Haandel" from a hamlet near Erp; "van den Bout" from a specific farm/dike feature
2. **Maternal Name**: Allegondis may have used her mother's surname (van den Bout) alongside paternal (van Haandel)
3. **Migration**: Family may have moved from "den Bout" (Zuid-Holland origin per CBG) to the Haandel area in Brabant
4. **Interchangeable Usage**: Common in 18th century to use multiple toponymic surnames

The strong Van Haandel presence in Erp DTB records vs. absence of Van den Bout suggests **Van Haandel was the primary family name** in church records.

## Conclusion

The Van Haandel family was established in Erp with documented presence from at least 1691 (Ansem van Haandel burial). The patronymic naming pattern strongly suggests Allegondis "Janssen" van den Bout was actually a **daughter of a Jan van Haandel** from the Erp area.

The "van den Bout" name appears exclusively in compiled genealogies and may represent:
- An alias/farm name used alongside the primary van Haandel surname
- A name used in specific contexts (possibly legal/notarial documents not yet searched)
- A transcription from now-lost records
