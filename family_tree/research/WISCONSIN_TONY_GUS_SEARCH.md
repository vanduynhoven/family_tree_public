# Wisconsin Van Dyn Hoven: Tony (Anton) & Gus Branch Search

**Research Date:** August 29, 2026
**Focus:** The last two undocumented sons of Albert Sr. & Adriana (Vande Wettering) Van Dyn Hoven

---

## Executive Summary

Despite extensive obituary and web searches, **no direct obituaries were found for Anton "Tony" Van Dyn Hoven, his wife Agnes, Gus Van Dyn Hoven, or his wife Bernice**. However, significant indirect evidence strongly suggests that **Shanna Van Dyn Hoven's line (via her grandparents Al & Ceil) descends from Tony's branch**.

### Key Finding

**Al (Albert/Alfred) Van Dyn Hoven x Ceil (Cecilia) of Kaukauna** are confirmed to be the grandparents of Shanna Marie Van Dyn Hoven (murdered 2000). Their son **Stephen Van Dyn Hoven x Nancy** are Shanna's parents. Since Al is NOT among the documented children of any of the five known branches, he almost certainly descends from either **Tony (Anton) x Agnes** or **Gus x Bernice**.

> "She is survived by her dear friends **Al and Ceil Van Dyn Hoven of Kaukauna** and their children: Kevin (Deb) Van Dyn Hoven, Lawrence (Deb) Van Dyn Hoven, Dennis (Nancy) Van Dyn Hoven, **Stephen (Nancy) Van Dyn Hoven**, Joe (Beth) Van Dyn Hoven, Kris (Ann) Sanders, and Don (Amy) Hurlbutt."

### Children of Al & Ceil Van Dyn Hoven

2. **Lawrence** (Deb) Van Dyn Hoven

4. **Stephen** (Nancy) Van Dyn Hoven — **SHANNA'S FATHER**
5. **Joe** (Beth) Van Dyn Hoven
6. **Kris** (Ann) Sanders — different surname, possibly stepchild or married name
7. **Don** (Amy) Hurlbutt — different surname, possibly stepchild

### Why Al Must Be from Tony or Gus's Line

**Al is NOT a known child of Albert Jr.**: Albert Jr. x Rosella Vanden Heuvel had documented children: Clyde, LaVerne, Virginia "Skippy", Alice, Audrey, and Carol. **No "Al" or "Albert"** among them.

**Al is NOT from any other documented branch**: All other brothers' children are documented in obituaries, and none include an "Al Van Dyn Hoven."

**Conclusion**: Al Van Dyn Hoven must be a son of either **Anton (Tony) x Agnes** or **Gus x Bernice** — the only two undocumented branches.

## Shanna Marie Van Dyn Hoven Connection

### Victim Profile
- **Full Name**: Shanna Marie Van Dyn Hoven
- **Birth**: ~1981
- **Death**: June 25, 2000 (age 19), Kaukauna, Wisconsin
- **Parents**: **Steven Van Dyn Hoven** and **Nancy Van Dyn Hoven**
- **Education**: Completed first year at UW-Madison
- **Nickname**: "Shanna Banana"

### Legal Documentation

**Wisconsin Court of Appeals, 2002** (*Van Dyn Hoven v. Pekin Insurance Company*):
> "SHANNA MARIE VAN DYN HOVEN, DECEASED, BY HER SPECIAL ADMINISTRATOR, **STEVEN VAN DYN HOVEN**, AND **NANCY VAN DYN HOVEN**, PLAINTIFFS-APPELLANTS"

Steven Van Dyn Hoven served as Special Administrator of Shanna's estate in the civil case against Pekin Insurance Company following her murder.

### Nancy Van Dyn Hoven COVID Story (2020)
Nancy Van Dyn Hoven (age 59 in December 2020, b. ~1961) was released from Ascension St. Elizabeth Hospital in Kaukauna after an 84-day COVID battle. This matches the same Nancy who is Stephen's wife.
