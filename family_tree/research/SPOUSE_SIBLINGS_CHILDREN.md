### Important Correction

The original GEDCOM shows Johanna born 13 Oct 1798 with parents:
- Father: Joannes Henrici van der Heijden (b. ~1712)
- Mother: Anna Maria Martinus Pittens

The baptism records **confirm** this family, but the children were baptized in **UDEN**, not Veghel as previously thought. The father's birth year of ~1712 appears to be incorrect - based on children born 1795-1803, the father was likely born ~1765-1770.

## Key Findings

### VERWEGEN Family
1. **Large family** - 15 children between 1820-1842
2. **High infant mortality** - Two Cornelias, suggesting first died young
3. **Strong family networks** - Multiple marriages to van der Wijst family (two siblings married two siblings)
4. **Van Duijnhoven connection** - Petronella married Peter van der Voort, whose mother was a van Duijnhoven

### VAN DER HEIJDEN Family
1. **DTB records found** - 7 baptisms in Uden 1795-1803
2. **Naming patterns** - Multiple Joannes and Wilhelma suggests high infant mortality
3. **Pittens connections** - Grandfather Martinus Pittens was frequent godparent

### CORNELISSEN Family
1. **Research gap** - Peter × Johanna marriage record needed
2. **Common surname** - Over 19,000 Cornelissen records in area
3. **Agriculture background** - Both parents were farmers
