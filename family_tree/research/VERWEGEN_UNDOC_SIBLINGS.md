## 3. JOHANNA MARIA VERWEGEN (b. 9 Aug 1837, Uden) - @I268@

### Birth Record
- **Date:** 9 Aug 1837
- **Place:** Uden
- **Parents:** Johannes Verwegen, Mechelina van den Berk
- **Source:** BHIC BS Geboorte bhi:1ff4f2cc-c6c8-9776-f996-f56217ed7151

### Marriage Evidence
**Indirect evidence strongly suggests marriage to Sijme (Simon) Smits:**

1. **1873 child marriage record** shows:
   - Johannes Smits married Antonetta van den Bogaard on 6 Sep 1873 Uden
   - **Parents of groom:** Sijme Smits, **Johanna Maria Verwegen**
   - Source: BHIC BS Huwelijk bhi:3097e223-b8b1-a79c-26c5-10bdc2d1c9cb

2. **1906 death record** shows:
   - Antonius Smits died 1 Sep 1906 Uden
   - **Parents:** **Johanna Maria Verwegen**, Simon Smits
   - Source: BHIC BS Overlijden bhi:0a81393a-48bf-9d09-3bbb-36f423628385

**Direct marriage record NOT FOUND** - searched 1855-1870, Sijme/Simon/Siem variants. The marriage may have occurred outside Uden or the record may be indexed differently.

### Known Children (via Smits)
Based on the 1873 and 1906 records:
- Johannes Smits (married 1873)
- Antonius Smits (died 1906)
- Possibly more children

**Conclusion:** Johanna Maria married Sijme (Simon) Smits, likely ~1858-1862. Direct marriage record not found. Death record not confirmed.

## Research Notes

1. **Name confusion:** The 1867 Schaijk marriage of "Martinus Verwegen × Johanna van der Zanden" is a DIFFERENT person - that Martinus had father Theodorus Verwegen, not Johannes. Our Martinus married much later (1882) at age 40.

2. **Antonetta confusion:** An older Antonetta Verwegen married Johannes de Groot and had children from 1840. This is NOT the Antonetta b.1839 who died 1843. The older Antonetta may be from a different Verwegen branch.

3. **Johanna Maria's marriage:** Despite strong indirect evidence (children's records naming her as mother), no direct marriage record was found for Johanna Maria Verwegen × Sijme Smits. The marriage may have occurred in a neighboring municipality.

4. **Carlina mystery:** No records found after birth. Possible infant death (unindexed), emigration, or religious life.
