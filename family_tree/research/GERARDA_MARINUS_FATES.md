# The Fates of Gerarda Maria & Marinus van Duijnhoven

Research resolving the two previously-unknown fates among the children of
**Marianus van Duijnhoven (@I005@) × Anna Maria van den Elzen (@I006@)**
(married Boekel, 18 May 1906; family moved to Uden/Volkel c. 1914; 12 children).

Both fates are now **RESOLVED** and written to the GEDCOM
(`vanduynhoven_family.ged`).

---

## SUBJECT 1 — Gerarda Maria van Duijnhoven (@I016@)

| | |
|---|---|
| Born | **25 September 1919**, Uden/Volkel, Noord-Brabant |
| Died | **30 April 2002**, Velp (aged 82) |
| Position | 10th child, 4th daughter |
| Evidence strength | **strong** (exact birth-date + birthplace match on a dedicated death source, corroborated by household records) |

### What was found
- **Prayer card (bidprentje)** indexed by *Heemkundekring Weerderheem*
  (Valkenswaard), record **038658**: *"Grada van Duijnhoven, geboren 25-09-1919
  te Volkel, overleden 30-04-2002 te Velp."* "Grada" is the everyday diminutive
  of Gerarda. The birth date (25-09-1919) and birthplace (Volkel — the Uden
  hamlet where the family's *gezinskaart* was kept) match her exactly.
  → https://www.openarchieven.nl/hwh:c905fc6f-069c-286a-e104-8a69c1d44f43
  (digital original: https://weerderheemcollecties.nl/cgi-bin/bidprent.pl?ident=038658)
- **Family household cards (Bevolkingsregister / gezinskaarten), Uden/Volkel:**
  she is listed as a child of the household for **1920–1929** (deel 1576, folio 142,
  "Gezinskaarten Volkel") and **1930–1938** (deel 1583, folio 457) → she was still
  living in the parental home through at least 1938 (age ~19).
  → https://www.openarchieven.nl/bhi:2af86375-84dd-153d-e9be-d94f11f0c2de
  → https://www.openarchieven.nl/bhi:080be3fb-8fcc-903c-a8f4-877f13ac333b
- **Civil birth registration**, Uden, 25 Sep 1919 (confirms identity).

### Interpretation
No death or marriage record surfaces under her **maiden** name in the civil
registration index. Combined with the prayer card recording her death only in
2002, this is consistent with her having **married** (so her civil death was
indexed under a married surname) and lived a normal-length life, dying at 82.
Her married surname was not recovered from the open indexes; the prayer-card scan
(JS-gated on the Weerderheem viewer) likely names her spouse but could not be
machine-read in this pass — that is the single open follow-up.

### Verdict
Did **not** die young and did **not** emigrate. Lived to 82, died 2002 in Velp.

## SUBJECT 2 — Marinus van Duijnhoven (@I017@)

| | |
|---|---|
| Born | **10 November 1920**, Uden/Volkel (Raamseweg), Noord-Brabant |
| Died | **27 May 2014**, Zeeland, Noord-Brabant (aged 93) |
| Spouse | **Anna Vervoort** (married c. 1953; she died 2000) |
| Buried | Cemetery (kerkhof) at Volkel |
| Position | 11th child, 7th son |
| Evidence strength | **strong** (exact birth-date match on a prayer card whose full text was read, plus civil birth registration) |

### What was found
- **Prayer card (bidprentje)** indexed by *Heemkundekring Zeeland*: *"Marinus van
  Duijnhoven, echtgenoot van Anna Vervoort †, geboren te Volkel 10 november 1920,
  overleden te Zeeland 27 mei 2014."* Birth date matches exactly.
  → https://www.openarchieven.nl/hkz:d46efb24-6deb-9301-8381-a2f3e75ce94b
- The **full prayer-card text was read** and gives his life story:
  - Grew up on the family farm on the **Raamseweg in Volkel** (one of the 12 children).
  - **Married in 1953**; the couple stayed working the farm.
  - **1978:** moved off the farm to the Bovenstraat; later (aged 71) to the Heuvelstraat.
  - His wife **Anna Vervoort died in 2000**.
  - An active man — many hobbies, cycling and bicycle repair; made a pilgrimage to
    **Santiago de Compostela**.
  - Survived by **children, grandchildren and great-grandchildren.**
  - Buried at the **Volkel cemetery.**
- **Civil birth registration**, Uden, 10 Nov 1920 (confirms identity).
  → https://www.openarchieven.nl/bhi:2b8f58ad-e573-ed2e-3cff-f665f6286e3b

### Records ruled OUT (different men named Marinus van Duijnhoven)
- d. 1 Jul 1969, Helmond — mother Johanna Maria van Roij, father Jan (not our line).
- d. 15 Jul 1991, "Brackwede"/Gemert klooster — born **1910** Erp.
- Various Aarle-Rixtel / Mook / Erp registrations — wrong birth years/places.

### Verdict
Survived the WWII occupation, married Anna Vervoort, farmed at Volkel, and lived a
long life — died at **93** in 2014. Did **not** die young or emigrate.
