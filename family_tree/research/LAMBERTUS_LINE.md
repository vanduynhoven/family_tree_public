## Summary

Lambertus van Duijnhoven (1861-1932) was Arthur's great-great-uncle — a brother of Marianus (1872-1949, Arthur's great-grandfather). Of his 11 children, **7 survived to adulthood** and at least 3 are confirmed to have had descendants. Their descendants are Arthur's **2nd cousins (1-3x removed)** depending on generation.
