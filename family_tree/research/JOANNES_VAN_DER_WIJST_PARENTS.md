**Evidence strength: strong** — marriage record naming the couple + multiple
corroborating baptisms of their children in the same parish, plus consistent
patronymics. Joannes's *individual* baptism record was not isolated in the index
(the API's place filter is unreliable), so the child-of-Willem link is established
by patronymic + parish + chronology rather than by a single baptism line naming
