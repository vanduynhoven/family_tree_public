## Candidate Marriage (PROVISIONAL)

### Marriage: 5 February 1708, Roosendaal
- **Groom:** Adrianus van Ham
- **Bride:** Anna Janssen Strijbos
- **Source:** bhi:39ffcd6e-2129-b1c6-ee08-01a42f527d0f (BHIC DTB Trouwen)
- **Full Record:** "Trouwen, 5-2-1708, Roosendaal, Anna Janssen Strijbos, Adrianus van Ham"

**Analysis:**
- Geographic fit: Roosendaal is ~15km from Rijsbergen (reasonable marriage distance)
- Timing fit: Adrianus born 1688 would be 20 years old in 1708 (typical marriage age)
- **COMPLICATION:** Subsequent children's baptism records list father as "Adrianus Geerse/Geeraerse van Ham" (patronymic suggesting "son of Geerard/Gerard"). This does NOT match our Adrianus Lambertus P. whose patronymic should be "Lamberti"

**Possible Explanations:**
1. Clerical error or variation in recording patronymics
2. "Geerard" was an alternate name or middle name for Lambertus Adriani
3. This is a DIFFERENT Adrianus van Ham (son of a Gerard van Ham), not our subject
4. Mixed family naming conventions in the region

**Evidence Strength:** WEAK - patronymic mismatch is significant

## Research Conclusions

### CRITICAL FINDING: Patronymic Mismatch

The 1708 Roosendaal marriage between "Adrianus van Ham" and "Anna Janssen Strijbos" is a CANDIDATE match for our Adrianus Lambertus P. van Ham (@I347@), but subsequent baptism records show the father's patronymic as "Geerse/Geeraerse" (son of Geerard/Gerard), NOT "Lamberti" (son of Lambertus).

**Implications:**
1. If this is our Adrianus Lambertus, the patronymic was recorded incorrectly
2. If this is a different Adrianus (son of Gerard), then NO descendants are confirmed for @I347@
3. The naming of child "Gerardus" (1724) supports the father being son of Gerard

### Recommended Action
- DO NOT add these children to GEDCOM under @I347@ at this time
- Mark as "Possible descendants - unconfirmed"
- Further research needed on Gerard/Geerard van Ham family in Roosendaal area
