## Genealogical Notes

### Patronymic Analysis:
- **Dirck Cornelis van den Elsen** = Dirck, son of Cornelis van den Elsen
- Cornelius's grandfather was therefore **Cornelis van den Elsen** (generation before Dirck)

### Witnesses at Cornelius's Baptism:
- **Jan Peter van Leuken** - likely maternal uncle (brother of Joanna Maria)
- **Lucia Cornelis van den Elsen** - paternal aunt (sister of Dirck)

### Death Record Discrepancy:
The death record (1811) names "Hendrick Jan van den Elsen" as father, but this conflicts with the baptism record which clearly shows "Dirck Cornelis van den Elsen". Possible explanations:
1. Transcription error in the civil register
2. Informant provided incorrect information
3. Different Cornelius van den Elsen (unlikely given matching dates and location)

**The baptism record is the primary source for parentage and should be considered authoritative.**
