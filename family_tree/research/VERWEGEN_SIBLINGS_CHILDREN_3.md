# Children of Cornelia Verwegen x Arnoldus van der Wijst

**Marriage:** 18 February 1865, Uden
**Connection:** Cornelia is a sibling of Geertruda Verwegen (Gen 2 Van Duijnhoven wife)

**Double Family Connection:** Cornelia's brother Adrianus Verwegen married Arnoldus's SISTER Everdina van der Wijst

## Parents

### Arnoldus van der Wijst
- **Birth:** ~1836 (based on children's births)
- **Death:** 27 July 1907, Uden
- **Death Record:** "Overlijden, 27-7-1907, Uden, Helena van der Sanden, Arnoldus van der Wijst, Wilhelmus van der Wijst, Cornelia Verwegen"
- **Source:** BHIC BS Overlijden, ID: 467694b2-9739-ad70-abda-c44e78ffb99f

## Summary

**Total Children:** 11 confirmed
- **Survived to Adulthood:** 6-7 (Helena I, Johannes, Johanna, Antonius, Petrus, Albertus; possibly Wilhelmina counts as adult death at 23)
- **Died in Infancy:** 4 (Magdalena I, Maria, Magdalena II, Helena II)
- **Status Unknown:** Helena I (first born; no death record found but also no marriage record)

**Naming Pattern:**
- Used family names repeatedly after infant deaths (two Magdalenas, two Helenas)
- Names suggest family connections: Magdalena (possibly after grandmother), Johannes (after Cornelia's father), Wilhelmina/Helena (possibly van der Wijst family names)

**Birth Interval:** 1865-1882 (17 years of childbearing)

## Research Notes

1. The marriage record for 18 February 1865 in Uden was not found in OpenArchieven search, but the children's birth and death records consistently confirm Arnoldus van der Wijst and Cornelia Verwegen as parents.

2. Arnoldus's death record (1907) confirms his parents as Wilhelmus van der Wijst and Helena van der Sanden.

3. The 1920 marriage of Albertus shows interesting details - "Antonius van der Wijst" listed as father with "Maria van Goor" - this appears to be Albertus's brother Antonius and his wife acting as parents/witnesses since father Arnoldus was deceased.

4. High infant mortality rate (4 of 11 children died in infancy) was typical for this era in Noord-Brabant.

5. Double family connection: Cornelia's brother Adrianus Verwegen married Everdina van der Wijst (sister of Arnoldus), creating intermarried families.
