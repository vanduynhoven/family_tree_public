## Summary of Lineage Findings

### Van Duijnhoven Line (Father's side)
```
Martinus van Duijnhoven + Geertrui Verweegen
    └── Marianus van Duijnhoven (b. 1872, Uden)
         + Anna Maria van den Elzen (b. 1878, Boekel)
            └── Johan van Duijnhoven (b. 1915, Uden)
```

### Verweegen Connection via Marianus's mother
Geertrui Verweegen (mother of Marianus) was a Verweegen -- the same family as the groom in the 1819 marriage record:
- Joannes Verweegen married Michaelina van den Berk in 1819
- Joannes's parents: Arnoldus Verweegen + Adriana Klaassen

### Van den Elzen Line (Mother's side)
```
Johannes van den Elzen + Francijna van der Horst
    └── Anna Maria van den Elzen (b. 30 Sep 1878, Boekel)
         + Marianus van Duijnhoven
            └── Johan van Duijnhoven (b. 1915)
```

### Van den Berk Connection
The Michaelina van den Berk marriage record shows:
- Her parents: Arnoldus van den Berk + Petronella van de Voort
- She married Joannes Verweegen (ancestor of Geertrui Verweegen?)
