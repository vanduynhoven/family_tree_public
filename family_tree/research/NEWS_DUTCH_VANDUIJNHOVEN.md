## 6. Notable Van Duijnhoven Individuals

### Rein van Duijnhoven - Professional Goalkeeper
*Wikipedia (NL & DE) + Dossier 1902 (MVV Historical Archive)*

**Personal:**
- Born: 5 September 1967, Veghel, Netherlands
- Height: 193 cm
- Position: Goalkeeper

**Career:**
- **VV Nuenen** - Youth
- **TSV LONGA Tilburg** - Early career
- **Helmond Sport** - First professional club, age 18
- **MVV Maastricht** (1994-1999)
  - Eredivisie debut: Season 1994-95
  - Relegation and First Division championship (1996-97)
- **VfL Bochum** (1999-2007) - Bundesliga, Germany
  - Initially backup to Thomas Ernst
  - Became starting goalkeeper
  - Voted best Second Division goalkeeper 2001-02
  - Played 119 Bundesliga matches
  - Became a "Kultfigur" (cult figure/fan favorite)
  - Total: 517 professional matches

**Famous Goalkeeper Goal - 3 May 1997:**
- Match: MVV Maastricht vs FC Den Bosch at De Geusselt stadium
- Score at time: MVV trailing 0-1 (Ruud van Nistelrooy goal for Den Bosch)
- What happened: Van Duijnhoven took a free kick from just outside his own 16-yard box
  - NOT a goal kick as often reported - it was a free kick from over 80 meters
  - Ball bounced on opponent's 16-yard line
  - Den Bosch keeper Jan van Grinsven (Van Duijnhoven's former MVV teammate) came out, misjudged
  - Ball bounced again on 5-meter line and went in
- Result: Equalizer; MVV won 2-1 (Rik Platvoet penalty)
- Recognition: **Voted Goal of the Year in the Netherlands**
- Appeared on Studio Sport national TV the next day
- MVV became First Division champions that season, 3 points ahead of Cambuur

**Press Coverage (VI.nl - Voetbal International, 20 January 2001):**
"Keeper Rein van Duijnhoven heeft zich tijdens een oefenwedstrijd van zijn Duitse club VfL Bochum aan zijn hoofd geblesseerd. De wond van de oud-doelman van MVV moest worden gehecht. Het is onzeker of Van Duijnhoven volgende week zaterdag het competitieduel tegen Bayern München kan spelen."

### Martin van Duynhoven - Jazz Drummer
*Wikipedia (NL & DE) + Various Music Sources*

**Personal:**
- Born: 13 June 1942, Boxmeer, Netherlands

**Musical Background:**
- Started in harmonieorchester (marching band with drummers and wind instruments) in **Uden**
- Studied 1959-1962 at Conservatory of Tilburg and Art Academy Den Bosch

**Career:**
- One of the most renowned Dutch jazz drummers alongside Han Bennink, John Engels, and Pierre Courbois
- Part of the bebop generation of the 1960s-70s
- The four formed a quartet called **BCDE**
- Associated with Dutch Creative Jazz / Geïmproviseerde Muziek Nederland
- Worked with ICP Orchestra, Willem Breuker Kollektief
- Multi-talented: also graphic designer, drawer, etcher, lithographer, photographer

**Collections:**
- Works in Internationaal Instituut voor Sociale Geschiedenis
- Works in Stedelijk Museum Amsterdam

**Press (Slagwerkkrant, 18 November 2016):**
"Van Duynhoven is van die vier degene die de meest filosofische, intellectuele benadering van het jazzdrummen heeft. Hij is een exponent van de zo gevierde Geïmproviseerde Muziek Nederland, die internationaal furore maakte met Mengelberg-Bennink, ICP Orchestra en het Willem Breuker Kollektief."

**Sources:**

### Serge van Duijnhoven - Poet, Writer, War Correspondent
*Wikipedia + DBNL + Literatuurmuseum*

- Born: 10 September 1970, Oss, Netherlands
- Education: Gymnasium at Titus Brandsma Lyceum, Oss

**Career:**
- Poet, writer, historian
- Co-founder of literary magazine MillenniuM
- Founder of Stichting Kunstgroep Lage Landen
- Frontman of musical collective "Dichters Dansen Niet" (Poets Don't Dance)
- Freelance contributor to Vrij Nederland, cobra.be, International Feature Agency

**War Correspondent Work:**
- **1995-2001: Correspondent in Sarajevo** for De Morgen and de Volkskrant
- Won the **Nova Malekodonia Award**
- Passenger on "Literature Express Europe 2000"

**Published Works Relating to War:**
- *Wij noemen het rozen. Balkan* (1999, Podium) - ISBN 90-5759-123-5
- *Oorlogsfotografen* (2002, Ludion Beaux Arts)
- "Uit het kookboek van de Bosnische oorlog" - Published in Passionate, 1998

**Film Coverage:**
- Since 2008: "Onze Man In Cannes" - Cannes Film Festival correspondent
- Reports for media in Netherlands, Flanders, USA (San Francisco, Seattle), Asia (Beijing, Shanghai, Guangzhou)

**Political:**
- Candidate for Partij voor de Republiek in 2021 Dutch elections

**Press (Literatuurmuseum):**
