# Van Duijnhoven Endogamy Patterns

Research into repeated intermarriage ("endogamy") within and around the Van
Duijnhoven family of Uden, Noord-Brabant. Endogamy here means a Van Duijnhoven
marrying a person who is already tied to the family — most often through the
**Verwegen** branch (which married into the Van Duijnhovens repeatedly across
generations) and the **Wassenberg** branch.

**Method.** Systematic sweep of every `BS Huwelijk` (civil marriage) record in
OpenArchieven / BHIC where a `van Duijnhoven` appears, restricted to `eventplace
= Uden`, 1815–1950 (221 person-mentions → 49 unique marriage aktes). Each akte's
full person list (bride, groom, and all four parents) was pulled via the
OpenArchieven `records/show` API and cross-checked for a second family tie.
A parallel sweep of `Verwegen` marriages was run to catch direct VD×Verwegen
unions. All findings cite the BHIC akte identifier (`bhi:<id>`).

## Already-confirmed patterns (from prior research — restated for context)

1. **1904** — Megchelina Verwegen (niece of Geertruda Verwegen) married Petrus
   van Duijnhoven (Geertruda's son). *Niece marries into aunt's married family.*
2. **1860** — Antonius Verwegen (Geertruda's brother) married Petronella van
   Duijnhoven.
3. **1859** — Petronella Verwegen married Peter van der Voort, whose mother was
   Woutrina van Duijnhoven.
4. **1946** — Hendrica Maria "Riek" van Duijnhoven married Hendricus Wassenberg,
   whose mother was Johanna van Duijnhoven (`bhi:3132f6e3...`, akte confirmed in
   this sweep: bride Hendrica Maria vD / Marianus vD, groom Hendricus Johannes
   Wassenberg / mother **Johanna van Duijnhoven**).

## NEW endogamous patterns found this sweep

### A. The Verwegen-sisters / Van Duijnhoven-brothers double marriage (STRONG)

Already noted narratively in the GEDCOM; this sweep supplies the **primary akte**
for the second half of the pair.

- **26 Jan 1856, Uden** — **Joannes van Duijnhoven × Ardina Verwegen**
  (`bhi:645958b1-7cc4-bb66-bafa-35c46257bf42`).
  - Groom: Joannes van Duijnhoven, s/o Hendricus van Duijnhoven & Maria Janssen.
  - Bride: Ardina Verwegen, d/o **Joannes Verwegen** & Mechelina van den Berk.
- Pairs with **17 Feb 1858, Uden** — Martinus van Duijnhoven × **Geertruda
  Verwegen** (Ardina's sister, same parents Joannes Verwegen & Mechelina van den
  Berk). Martinus and Joannes van Duijnhoven were brothers (both s/o Petrus van
  Duijnhoven 1799–1882 × Johanna van der Heijden).
- **Two Verwegen sisters married two Van Duijnhoven brothers, two years apart.**
- **Evidence: strong** (marriage akte 1856 + existing GEDCOM narrative + shared
  parent names Joannes Verwegen / Mechelina van den Berk across both unions).

### B. Adrianus van Duijnhoven × Wilhelmina Verwegen (STRONG — direct VD×Verwegen)

- **27 May 1904, Uden** (`bhi:49162654-b111-069b-0ee8-4984a552c6b7`).
  - Groom: **Adrianus van Duijnhoven**, s/o Hendricus van Duijnhoven & Anna Maria
    van der Wijst.
  - Bride: **Wilhelmina Verwegen**, d/o Adrianus Verwegen & Everdina van der Wijst.
- Corroborated by two of their children's marriage aktes, which both name mother
  **Wilhelmina Verwegen**:
  - 29 May 1936, Uden — Anna Maria van Duijnhoven × Adrianus van der Aa
    (`bhi:aea34a07-b1b7-4351-5522-9a09a22cb9cd`).
  - 9 May 1941, Uden — Everarda Josephina van Duijnhoven × Jan Maria van den Oever
    (`bhi:3892f91f-ee7a-4670-20c5-0e0ffed6af67`).
- Note both spouses' mothers are **van der Wijst** — a further intermarrying
  surname in this cluster (see E).
- **Evidence: strong** (marriage akte + 2 children's aktes).

### C. Adriana van Duijnhoven × Johannes Verwegen (STRONG — direct VD×Verwegen)

- **3 Feb 1922, Uden** (`bhi:fb36a951-a596-e5d7-3cd1-c6197b4d578c`).
  - Bride: **Adriana van Duijnhoven**, d/o Antonius van Duijnhoven & Maria van
    Schijndel.
  - Groom: **Johannes Verwegen**, s/o **Antonius Verwegen** & Johanna Maria de Groot.
- The groom's father Antonius Verwegen ties this union into the same Verwegen
  network as confirmed pattern #2 (Antonius Verwegen × Petronella van Duijnhoven).
- **Evidence: strong** (single akte, full parentage on both sides). A reverse
  direction of the usual pattern — here the VD is the *bride*, marrying a Verwegen.

### D. Van Duijnhoven with a Verwegen mother marrying out (MODERATE — descent-side endogamy)

Grooms/brides who are Van Duijnhoven *and* half-Verwegen by descent:

- **31 Jul 1891, Uden** — Antonius van Duijnhoven × Maria van Schijndel
  (`bhi:fc3bdecc-b304-67f9-3a64-95c80aebdfef`). Groom s/o Martinus van
  Duijnhoven & **Geertruij Verwegen** — i.e. a son of pattern-A/Geertruda's own
  line (Geertruij/Geertruda Verwegen). This couple are the parents of the
  Adriana of pattern C (mother Maria van Schijndel matches).
- **28 Jan 1938, Uden** — Gerardus van Duijnhoven (s/o Petrus vD & **Megchelina
  Verwegen**) × Antonia Scheepers (`bhi:758e96ab-4e9d-d0f8-1639-504c30cf63b2`).
- **23 Jun 1947, Uden** — Ardina van Duijnhoven (d/o Petrus vD & **Megchelina
  Verwegen**) × Peter Joannes Antonius Geraerts
  (`bhi:4f638360-88e8-9a5d-1b81-04f9b91f084f`).
  (Gerardus 1938 and Ardina 1947 are children of confirmed pattern #1: Petrus van
  Duijnhoven × Megchelina Verwegen.)
- **Evidence: moderate** (each a single akte; they document the *offspring* of the
  known Verwegen unions, not new spousal endogamy).

### E. Wassenberg × Van Duijnhoven — earlier generation (STRONG — cluster continuity)

Confirmed pattern #4 (1946) is not isolated; an **earlier** Wassenberg–VD marriage
exists, and both Wassenberg grooms have a Verwegen mother:

- **12 Feb 1909, Uden** — **Johanna van Duijnhoven × Hendricus Wassenberg**
  (`bhi:9060aa0f-0679-1911-2631-a2b4be0120bc`).
  - Bride: Johanna van Duijnhoven, d/o Hendricus van Duijnhoven & Anna Maria van
    der Wijst.
  - Groom: Hendricus Wassenberg, s/o Joannes Wassenberg & **Joanna Maria Verwegen**.
- So the Wassenberg family carried Verwegen blood *and* married Van Duijnhoven
  women in **both** 1909 and 1946 — a repeated three-family (VD × Wassenberg ×
  Verwegen) intermarriage across two generations.
- **Evidence: strong** (this akte + confirmed 1946 akte = 2 independent Wassenberg–VD
  unions, both with a Verwegen mother).

### F. van der Wijst as a secondary intermarrying surname (MODERATE — lead)

The van der Wijst surname recurs as a Van Duijnhoven spouse/mother, sometimes
alongside a Verwegen:

- **26 May 1911, Uden** — Hendrica van Duijnhoven × Johannes van der Wijst
  (`bhi:85c1c0ea-995a-7fcb-17f2-a09fc3d9603b`); groom's mother **Cornelia
  Verwegen**. (VD bride × van der Wijst groom who is himself half-Verwegen.)
- **16 Jun 1866, Uden** — Hendricus van Duijnhoven × Anna Maria van der Wijst
  (`bhi:6e97dead-495c-43d2-bafe-8b172cd57263`).
- Adrianus vD × Wilhelmina Verwegen (pattern B) both had van der Wijst mothers
  (Anna Maria van der Wijst / Everdina van der Wijst).
- **Evidence: moderate/lead** — van der Wijst clearly interlocks with the
  VD–Verwegen cluster, but no single VD×VD or VD×known-relative union is yet
  proven here. **Open lead for a future cycle.**

### G. Neighbouring-parish sweep — Petrus vD × Megchelina Verwegen children marrying out (MODERATE — descent-side, closes the "widen the place filter" lead)

Widening the sweep beyond Uden (Erp, Boekel, Zeeland, Veghel) surfaced **three
more children** of confirmed pattern #1 (Petrus van Duijnhoven @I352@ × Megchelina
Verwegen @I336@) marrying out — the union's descendants scattered to neighbouring
parishes rather than all marrying in Uden. All three name mother **Megchelina
Verwegen** + father **Petrus van Duijnhoven** in the akte:

- **17 Jan 1930, Boekel** — Gerardus van Duijnhoven × Geertruda Hijasintha
  Scheepers (`bhi:f66402da-52e8-35e6-fa16-440bd4579713`). *(3rd child.)*
- **9 Jun 1939, Erp** — Gerardus Petrus van Duijnhoven × Johanna Maria van den
  Biggelaar (`bhi:11dd560d-1768-f102-ce1e-2294ad0ec885`). *(5th child.)*
- **22 Jul 1947, Zeeland** — **Johannes van Duijnhoven (@I360@, 8th child)** ×
  Wilhelmina Bongers (d/o Johannes Bongers & Waltera van den Elzen)
  (`bhi:993e97de-3164-c1c4-5bfd-10261f62dd73`). **NEW to the GEDCOM** — @I360@
  previously had no marriage recorded; note updated this cycle.
- These join the already-documented Uden children Gerardus (1938) and Ardina
  (1947) under pattern D. The 1930/1939 aktes were already cited on the children's
  own GEDCOM records; the **1947 Zeeland marriage of @I360@ is the genuinely new
  find**.
- **Evidence: moderate** (descent-side — these document the *offspring* of the
  known Verwegen union marrying non-relatives, not new spousal endogamy). Their
  value is negative-space: confirms the Verwegen alliance did **not** loop back a
  further generation through these five surviving children — they all married
  *out* (Scheepers, van den Biggelaar, Bongers, van Eck, Geraerts), into
  non-cluster families.

### H. Direct VD × van der Wijst union (LEAD — advances pattern F)

- **22 Apr 1932, Boekel** — **Francisca van Duijnhoven × Cornelis van der Wijst**
  (`bhi:1ace3557-dd92-3032-074e-7ca299390070`).
  - Bride: Francisca van Duijnhoven, d/o **Lambertus van Duijnhoven** & Maria van
    Deursen.
  - Groom: Cornelis van der Wijst, s/o Johannes van der Wijst & Francina van den
    Biggelaar.
- This is the first *direct* VD × van der Wijst marriage found (pattern F only had
  van der Wijst as a mother/secondary surname). **However**, van der Wijst is an
  extremely common Uden surname (543 marriage-mentions), and no shared-ancestor
  loop-back to the Verwegen-linked van der Wijst branch is yet proven. The groom's
  parents (Johannes vd Wijst × Francina van den Biggelaar) do not obviously tie to
  the Everdina/Anna Maria van der Wijst of pattern B.
- **Evidence: lead** — a real VD × van der Wijst union, but not yet demonstrated
  endogamous. Needs parentage tracing of Cornelis van der Wijst's line.

## Key structural finding

The Van Duijnhoven–Verwegen bond was **not a one-off** but a sustained,
multi-generational alliance in Uden:

- **Gen A (1850s):** two Verwegen sisters (Ardina, Geertruda) married two Van
  Duijnhoven brothers (Joannes, Martinus) — 1856 & 1858.
- **Gen A' (1860):** Antonius Verwegen × Petronella van Duijnhoven (confirmed #2).
- **Gen B (1904):** Adrianus vD × Wilhelmina Verwegen; and Megchelina Verwegen
  (niece) × Petrus vD (confirmed #1).
- **Gen B (1922):** Adriana vD × Johannes Verwegen (Antonius Verwegen's son).
- The **Wassenberg** family, itself carrying Verwegen descent, married Van
  Duijnhoven women in **1909 and 1946** — extending the cluster to three surnames.

This is textbook rural-Catholic-Brabant endogamy: repeated alliances between a
small set of landholding families (Van Duijnhoven, Verwegen, van der Wijst,
Wassenberg, van Schijndel) concentrated in Uden.
