# VERWEGEN Spouse Name Discrepancy - RESOLVED

## The Discrepancy

Two conflicting records were identified:
1. **Earlier research (VERWEGEN_3GEN.md):** Johannes Verwegen married Mechelina van den Berk, ca. 1821
2. **New search result:** Johannes Verwegen marriage on 13-2-1820 shows Anna Maria van der Putten

## Verdict: TWO DIFFERENT MEN NAMED JOHANNES VERWEGEN

**There is NO error.** These are two separate individuals who both happened to be named Johannes (Joannes) Verwegen living in Uden at the same time.

---

## Person 1: Johannes Verwegen (son of Geurt & Helena Gielens)

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Parents** | Geurt Verwegen & Helena Gielens |
| **Married** | Anna Maria van der Putten (also spelled "Verputten"), 13 February 1820, Uden |
| **Died** | 29 June 1826, Uden |
| **Death Record** | BHIC `87d4856e-6ae6-eb41-0f10-4cd681515e20` |
| **Death Record Text** | "Overlijden, 29-6-1826, Uden, Helena Gielens, Joannes Verwegen, Geurt Verwegen, Anna Maria Verputten" |

This Johannes died in 1826 and **cannot be Geertruda Verwegen's father**.

---

## Person 2: Johannes Verwegen (husband of Mechelina van den Berk)

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Parents** | Unknown (not yet established) |
| **Married** | Mechelina van den Berk, ca. 1821 (pre-dates most digitized civil records) |
| **Children** | 10+ children born 1822-1833, all in Uden |
| **Status** | Alive at daughter Geertruda's wedding (17 Feb 1858) |

This Johannes **is** Geertruda Verwegen's father.

---

## Proof: Children Born AFTER June 1826

The following children were born to "Joannes Verwegen & Mechelina van den Berk" AFTER the death of Johannes (son of Geurt) on 29 June 1826:

| Child | Birth Date | BHIC Record ID |
|-------|-----------|----------------|
| Cornelia Verwegen | 1 January 1828 | `b18993e1-8c0a-3d17-7905-e6e8fedf1af5` |

Since Johannes (son of Geurt, husband of Anna Maria van der Putten) died 29 June 1826, he could not have fathered these children. Therefore, **there were two different men named Johannes Verwegen**.

---

## Conclusion

The VERWEGEN_3GEN.md document is **CORRECT**:
- Geertruda Verwegen's father was Johannes Verwegen who married Mechelina van den Berk
- The 13 February 1820 marriage to Anna Maria van der Putten belongs to a DIFFERENT Johannes Verwegen (son of Geurt Verwegen & Helena Gielens), who died in 1826

No correction to VERWEGEN_3GEN.md is needed. The document already correctly notes this distinction in the "Generation 2" section.
