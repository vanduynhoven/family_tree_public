# Van den Elzen Grandparents Research

## Research Goal
Find the parents of Gerardus Cornelus van den Elzen (b. ~1798, Boekel) who is the great-great-grandfather of Anna Maria van den Elzen (b. 1878, Boekel).

## Family Chain
- **Anna Maria van den Elzen** (b. 1878 Boekel) married Marianus van Duijnhoven
- **Johannes van den Elzen** (b. 8 Jun 1838 Boekel) - father of Anna Maria, married Francijna van der Horst
- **Gerardus Cornelus van den Elzen** (b. 4 Dec 1798 Boekel) - father of Johannes
- **Cornelius van den Elsen** (senior) - father of Gerardus - **FOUND**
- **Wilhelmina (Henrici) Bevers** (b. 19 Oct 1775 Boekel) - mother of Gerardus - **FOUND**

## CONFIRMED: Parents of Gerardus Cornelus van den Elzen

### Father: Cornelius van den Elsen
- **Birth:** Unknown (~1765-1775), likely Boekel/Erp area
- **Death:** 17 October 1811, Boekel (BS Overlijden)
- **Marriage:** 2 February 1798, Boekel (RC), to Wilhelmina Henrici Bevers

## Research Notes

1. **Name variations:** The surname appears as "van den Elsen" (older records, Latin style) and "van den Elzen" (later BS records, Dutch style) - these are the same family.

2. **Patronymic evidence:** The brother Henricus Cornelii van den Elsen's patronymic "Cornelii" (genitive of Cornelius) confirms their father was named Cornelius.

3. **Mother of Cornelius Sr:** Not yet identified. The death record of Cornelius (1811) shows "N.N." as spouse, suggesting Wilhelmina may have died before 1811, or the clerk did not record her name.

4. **Outstanding research:** 
   - Birth/baptism of Cornelius van den Elsen (senior) ~1765-1775
   - Identity of Cornelius senior's mother (wife of grandfather Cornelius)
   - Death record of Wilhelmina Bevers
