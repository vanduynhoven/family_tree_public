# Jan van Duijnhoven (@I011@, b.1912 Boekel) — Oblate friar lead: DISCONFIRMED

## Question
Did Jan van Duijnhoven (@I011@, b. 1912 Boekel, son of Marianus van Duijnhoven
× Anna Maria van den Elzen) become an Oblate (OMI) religious brother named
"Louis (Ludovicus)" who died in 1990 at Goirle, Noord-Brabant?

## Verdict: NOT CONFIRMED — the lead is disproven as stated.
The record that seeded this hypothesis is real, but it belongs to a **different
man**. The birthplace and birth year do not match.

## Key evidence

### The single Goirle death record for a "van Duijnhoven" ~1990
Full record fetched from Open Archieven / Heemkundekring Weerderheem:

> **Overlijden op 27 juli 1990 te Goirle**
> Overledene (man): **Louis van Duijnhoven, geboren op 28 februari 1926 te Groesbeek**
> Bron: Heemkundekring Weerderheem, Bidprentjesverzameling Valkenswaard
> (original record identifier 038675)
> - https://www.openarchieven.nl/hwh:616aa2a2-6872-4532-c29d-bd618cf91a30
> - https://weerderheemcollecties.nl/cgi-bin/bidprent.pl?ident=038675

Mismatch table:

| Field       | Our Jan (@I011@)      | Goirle 1990 "Louis"        | Match? |
|-------------|-----------------------|----------------------------|--------|
| Birth year  | 1912                  | **1926**                   | ✗ (14 yr off) |
| Birthplace  | Boekel                | **Groesbeek** (~50 km away)| ✗ |
| Given name  | Jan (Johannes)        | Louis                      | — (plausible religious name, but see below) |
| Death place | (hypothesised) Goirle | Goirle                     | ✓ |
| Death year  | (hypothesised) 1990   | 1990                       | ✓ |

Only the death place and year — the two facts that were *assumed* in the
hypothesis, not independently known — line up. Everything independently
verifiable (birth year, birthplace) contradicts it. The "78-year-old dying in
1990 → born 1912" reasoning in the brief fails: this man was 64, not 78.

Note also: the bidprentje gives the deceased's name simply as "Louis van
Duijnhoven" with no indication of a religious order, brother title, or Oblate
affiliation. There is no evidence in the record that this Louis was a religieus
at all.

### Exhaustive Open Archieven check
Open Archieven records API (`records/search.json`), searched by name + place:

- `name="van Duijnhoven", eventplace="Goirle"` → **3 hits**, ALL the same
  27-Jul-1990 event (1× Overlijden, 1× Bidprentje, 1× Overlijden duplicate) —
  i.e. the single person above. No other van Duijnhoven death is recorded at
  Goirle.
- `name="Louis van Duijnhoven"` → 4 hits: the 3 Goirle-1990 duplicates above +
  one unrelated 2023 death in "Nederland".
- `name="van Duijnhoven", eventplace="Boekel"` → 216 hits (the ancestral
  cluster), but none is a ~1990 Goirle death and none identifies an Oblate.
- Web searches for `Ludovicus/Louis van Duijnhoven Goirle Oblaat broeder 1990`
  surface only the same Weerderheem bidprentje — no independent Oblate/OMI
  necrology entry for a van Duijnhoven at Goirle.

### Oblate-house-at-Goirle premise not corroborated
Searches for an OMI ("Oblaten van Maria Onbevlekt") community specifically in
**Goirle** returned no confirming source. Documented Dutch Oblate houses appear
elsewhere (e.g. Valkenburg/Limburg — "Klooster van de Paters Oblaten"; a German
Oblate group at Heer near Maastricht). The BHIC has an OMI congregation
overview (https://bhic.nl/ontdekken/verhalen/de-missionarissen-oblaten-van-de-onbevlekte-maagd-maria-omi)
but nothing ties an OMI house to Goirle, nor this individual to the order.
The "major OMI house in Goirle" claim in the brief is unsupported by the sources
checked and should be treated as unverified.

## Conclusion
- The "Louis van Duijnhoven died 1990 Goirle" record is a **name/place/year
  coincidence** with a man born 1926 in Groesbeek — not our Jan (@I011@),
  b. 1912 Boekel.
- No source found places Jan van Duijnhoven (@I011@) in the Oblates, gives him a
  religious name, or connects him to Goirle.
- **GEDCOM NOT modified.** @I011@'s fate remains unknown. Do NOT record the
  Oblate/Goirle death for @I011@ — it would introduce a false identity.
