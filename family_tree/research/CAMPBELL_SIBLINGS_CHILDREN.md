# Children of the Campbell Siblings

## Research Date: 2026-08-29

---

## Overview

This document tracks the children of Mary Cecilia Campbell's 15 surviving siblings (16 total, minus infant Jerome who died in infancy). These are the nieces and nephews of Mary Cecilia Campbell Van Duynhoven (1958-2019).

**Parents:** Fairman Campbell (1912-1990) & Mary Ellen Ortscheid (1919-2012)
- Married: October 2, 1940 in Pecatonica, Illinois
- Resided: Red Wing, Minnesota; later Yuma, Arizona

**Per Mary Ellen's 2012 obituary:**
> "Mary is also survived by 65 grandchildren, 60 great grandchildren, and one great, great grandchild."
