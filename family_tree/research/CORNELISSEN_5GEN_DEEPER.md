## NOTES ON NAMING CONVENTIONS

- **Patronymic system:** Before ~1811, surnames often included father's first name
  - Example: "Gerardus Joannis Verbruggen" = Gerardus, son of Joannis Verbruggen
  - Example: "Petrus Tonies van Grootel" = Petrus, son of Tonies van Grootel

- **Latin forms in DTB:** Joannes = Jan, Johannes; Gerardus = Gerit; Petrus = Peter
- **Regional spelling variations:** Bukkems/Buckems, Van Grootel/Grootel
