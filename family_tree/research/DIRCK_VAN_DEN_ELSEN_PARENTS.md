## Evidence

### 1. Patronymic Analysis (Strong)
The name "Dirck **Cornelis** van den Elsen" follows Dutch naming convention where the middle name is the father's given name:
- **Dirck** = given name (Theodorus in Latin)
- **Cornelis** = patronymic = father's name
- **van den Elsen** = family surname

This is consistent across multiple records:
- Marriage 1768: "Dirck Cornelis van den Elsen"
- Baptism witness 1775: "Theodorus Cornelis van den Elsen" (Latin form)
- Baptism witness 1783: "Theodorus Cornelis van den Elsen"
