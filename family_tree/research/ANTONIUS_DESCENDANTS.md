# Antonius van Duijnhoven - Descendants Research

## Important Note: Identity Clarification

**Two different Antonius van Duijnhoven individuals exist in this time period:**

1. **Antonius van Duijnhoven (b. 1864 Helmond, d. 1947 Helmond)** - Married Antonia Vermulst (1880-1948) on 5 Feb 1903 in Helmond. Had 11 children. Occupation: Landbouwer (farmer). This is the individual well-documented on genealogieonline.nl.

2. **Antonius van Duijnhoven (b. 1865 Uden)** - The individual referenced in the task. Less documentation found online.

The research below documents the **Helmond-born Antonius (1864-1947)** branch, which appears to be a separate family line from the Uden branch. Further research needed to determine if these lines connect to a common ancestor.
