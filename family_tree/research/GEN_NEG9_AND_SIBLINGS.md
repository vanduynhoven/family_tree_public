## Key Insights

### 1. Patronymic Confirmation
The nickname "Jan **Dirkse**" in the Van Elk source is fully confirmed by the Bernards genealogy showing Johannes was a son of **Dirck** Maeszn van Duijnhoven.

### 2. Wife's Family Connection
Jenneken Willems van Duijnhoven (wife of Johannes Gen -8) was **also a Van Duijnhoven** herself! She was the daughter of:
- **Wilhelmus Jansen van Duijnhoven** (~1515-?)
- **Theodora** (surname unknown)

This means Johannes and Jenneken were likely **distant cousins** - a common occurrence in Dutch genealogy of this period.

### 3. Clerical Profession
**Goossen Janssen van Duijnhoven** (sibling of Dirck Gen -7) was a **pastoor** (parish priest) in Asten or Aarle. This indicates the family had some social standing and education.

### 4. Geographic Concentration
The family was concentrated in these Noord-Brabant towns:
- Aarle-Rixtel (most births/deaths)
- Lieshout
- Gemert (marriages)
- Stiphout (earliest origins)
- Son, Mierlo, Lierop, Helmond (property holdings)

### Potential Confusion
According to Cor Wildenberg (cited in Bernards), there may be **two different persons named Thomas van Duijnhoven**:
1. Thomas (~1485), father of Dirck Maeszn - **our line**
