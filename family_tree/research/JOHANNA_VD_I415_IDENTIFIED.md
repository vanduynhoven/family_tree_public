# Johanna van Duijnhoven (@I415@) — Line Identified

**Question:** Which Van Duijnhoven family line does Johanna van Duijnhoven (@I415@) belong to?

**Answer (evidence: STRONG):** She belongs to a **separate Stiphout / Peelland (Helmond-area) Van Duijnhoven line — NOT the main Uden/Boekel line** of the family tree. Her surname coincidence with the tree's principal surname is exactly that: a coincidence on the maternal (Verhoeven) side of the American matriarch Anna Maria Cornelissen.

---

## Who she is

- **@I415@ Johanna (Joanna) van Duijnhoven** — wife of **@I414@ Wilhelmus (Willem) Verhoeven**, family **@F142@**.
- **Born in Stiphout** (a village adjoining Helmond, west of Bakel), c. 1798–1802.
- **Parents: Joannis (Johannes) van Duijnhoven × Catharina Verbakel.**
- Married Wilhelmus Verhoeven **26 May 1822 in Bakel en Milheeze**; she came to Bakel through this marriage.
- Mother of the Verhoeven children of Bakel en Milheeze, including **Johanna Verhoeven (@I161@, b. 13 Sep 1839)**, who married Peter Cornelissen 11 Feb 1874 — the line that leads to Anna Maria Cornelissen (b. 1916), wife of Johan van Duijnhoven of the American branch.

## Position in the tree

She sits on the **maternal Verhoeven/Cornelissen side**. She is *not* a blood ancestor of the Uden Van Duijnhoven patriarch **Petrus van Duijnhoven (b. 1799 Uden)**; the two Van Duijnhoven surnames meet only at the 1950 marriage of Johan van Duijnhoven (Uden line) × Anna Maria Cornelissen (whose maternal great-great-grandmother is this Johanna). Uden lies ~30 km north of the Bakel/Stiphout Peelland cluster, and no shared ancestry has been found; her line is the Stiphout Van Duijnhoven family.

## Why "separate line," not the Uden line

- **Geography:** Johanna was born in **Stiphout** and her father Joannis van Duijnhoven lived in the Stiphout/Helmond corner of the Peel; the tree's main line is rooted in **Uden** (Petrus b. 1799), ~30 km north, tracing back to Aarle-Rixtel.
- **No church cluster in Bakel:** OpenArchieven has **zero** `DTB Dopen` van Duijnhoven baptisms in Bakel parish, i.e. the family did not originate in Bakel; Johanna married in.
- **Surname coincidence, not kinship:** the connection to the tree's Van Duijnhoven surname is only through the 1950 Cornelissen×van Duijnhoven marriage, on the maternal side. No genealogical link between Joannis van Duijnhoven (Stiphout) and Petrus van Duijnhoven (Uden) has been found.
