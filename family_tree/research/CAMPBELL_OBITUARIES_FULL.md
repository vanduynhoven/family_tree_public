## PRIMARY SOURCE: Mary Ellen (Ortscheid) Campbell Obituary (2012)

**Source:** https://www.mahnfamilyfuneralhome.com/obituaries/mary-campbell
**Died:** November 8, 2012
**Born:** July 4, 1919, Cuba City, Wisconsin
**Parents:** Joseph and Teresa (McDonald) Ortscheid

### Full Text:
Mary Ellen (Ortscheid) Campbell of Yuma, Arizona, formerly of Red Wing, died on November 8, 2012 at The Lighthouse at Waconia. She was born July 4, 1919 in Cuba City, Wisconsin, to Joseph and Teresa (McDonald) Ortscheid. She was raised and attended school in Winnebago, Illinois. In May 1940, Mary graduated from Northern Illinois University in Dekalb, Illinois, with a teaching degree. She married Fairman Campbell October 2, 1940, in Pecatonica, Illinois. Mary taught in various states including: Illinois, Arizona, and Minnesota. Mary completed her professional career and retired from the Red Wing Correctional Facility in 1974 after ten years of being a reading tutor and librarian.

Mary was a woman who had a deep faith in God and a devout devotion to the Blessed Virgin Mary. She loved and was intensely proud of her entire family. After Mary and her husband Fairman retired in 1976, they traveled (via their RV) throughout the 49 states, flew to Hawaii and drove the entire length of Mexico. She loved to talk, laugh, work hard, played numerous musical instruments and was a self taught painter. In the summer of 2007 Mary moved back to Minnesota to live with her family until October 2010 when, after two hospitalizations, she moved to the Lighthouse in Waconia, Minnesota.

### Full Text:
George Edward Willis, 91, of Red Wing, died Wednesday, July 20, 2022 at the Mayo Clinic Health System in Red Wing. He was born September 12, 1930 in Red Wing, to Christ and Helen (Wolpers) Willis. He grew up in Red Wing and attended St. Joseph School and graduated from Red Wing Central High School in 1948. He married Laura Barringer in 1956 and they later divorced in 1972. George worked at the Conoco Station from 1946-1998 and bought the station in 1960. In 1970, he bought a second station at Main and Potter and a bulk oil business. **In 1979 he married Marion Campbell.**

**Died:** March 16, 2024
**Born:** August 23, 1943, Leaf River, IL
**Wife of:** James Campbell (son of Fairman & Mary Ellen Campbell)

### Full Text:
Cheryl Lee Campbell was born 8/23/43 and lived her entire life in Leaf River. She met Jim Campbell, the love of her life, at 23, married and together they raised 3 children. Cheryl was a very active member of their community. She also served as the town librarian for several years as well as taking a large part in the church nursery. Cheryl, along with her close friends, loved to laugh. She truly loved people and was generous with her time and resources. Whether she was hitting the garage sales or getting ready for bible study, Cheryl carried her sharp wit with her wherever she went and kept it with her until her last breath. On Saturday, March 16th, Cheryl Lee Campbell passed peacefully with her husband and family by her side on to the presence of her Savior.

## SECONDARY SOURCE: Dale Czapiewski Obituary (2021)

**Source:** https://www.watkowski-mulyck.com/obituaries/dale-czapiewski
**Died:** October 17, 2021
**Born:** April 13, 1943, Winona, MN
**Husband of:** Marie Campbell (daughter of Fairman & Mary Ellen Campbell)

### Full Text:
Dale William Czapiewski passed away at home on October 17, 2021, surrounded by his family. Born on April 13, 1943, in Winona, MN, to Clarence (Cal) and Marion (Schneider) Czapiewski, Dale spent his childhood along the banks of the Mississippi River, happily hunting and fishing with his father and brother. He graduated from Winona Senior High in 1961. Shortly after, Dale proudly stepped up to answer the call to serve his country during the Vietnam War, stationed at the DMZ in South Korea. He was also a lifelong member of the VFW. Dale had a 38-year career in the Heat Treat department at Peerless Chain.

**Dale married Marie Campbell on September 19, 1992**, and the couple shared the next 29 years together. He often pointed to his wedding ring and referred to their marriage as "more than just a band of gold." Their marriage was full of love.
