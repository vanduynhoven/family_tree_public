# Van der Wijst Endogamy Sweep

**Date:** 30 Aug 2026
**Method:** OpenArchieven API (`records/search.json` + `records/show.json?archive=bhi`)
— full-akte person/relation parsing across Uden, Boekel, Zeeland, Veghel,
Nistelrode, Schaijk, Erp, Sint-Oedenrode, 1850–1950. Two-sided sweep: `name=van
der Wijst` and `name=van Duijnhoven`, each filtered on `eventplace`, then every
akte's full record fetched and scanned for the other surname (+ Verwegen).
Cross-referenced against `vanduynhoven_family.ged`.

**Result:** **22 unique marriage aktes** link the van der Wijst and Van
Duijnhoven surnames. This closes the "van der Wijst" open lead in
`ENDOGAMY_PATTERNS.md` §F: van der Wijst is **not a peripheral coincidence** but a
structural intermarrying surname that enters the Van Duijnhoven tree through **two
independent doorways**, each already anchored in the GEDCOM.

## Executive summary — the two doorways

**Doorway 1 — Cornelia van der Wijst (1799–1862) → the collateral "Jean" branch.**
Cornelia married Johannes van Duijnhoven (b.1793, son of Johannes Peter "Jean"
@I083@) on 13 Jun 1827 Uden. She is already in the GEDCOM (family @ line 1671).
Her daughters' marriages appear in this sweep with "Cornelia van der Wijst" as
*Moeder van de bruid* (see B1, B2).

**Doorway 2 — Anna Maria van der Wijst → the direct sibling line (@I053@).**
A **different** van der Wijst woman, Anna Maria, married **Hendricus van
Duijnhoven (1837–1910, @I053@)** on 16 Jun 1866 Uden. Hendricus was the **brother
of the direct ancestor Martinus (1829, @I003@)** — so this van der Wijst married
straight into the core line, one step off the direct ancestor. Her descendants
(the 1904 Adrianus marriage, the 1909 + resulting 1946 Wassenberg marriages, the
1911 Johannes van der Wijst marriage) all trace back through @I053@.

**Doorway 3 (reinforcing) — the Verwegen × van der Wijst double sibling-swap.**
Already documented in GEDCOM: **Cornelia Verwegen × Arnoldus van der Wijst**
(18 Feb 1865) and her brother **Adrianus Verwegen × Everdina van der Wijst**
(7 Feb 1863) — Arnoldus and Everdina were siblings. This feeds VD blood into the
van der Wijst line one generation later (C-group below), because both Verwegen
women were sisters of Geertruda Verwegen (Gen 2 VD wife @I136@... i.e. the
Martinus × Geertruda couple).

**Highest-value single find:** the **1904** marriage (A3) where **both spouses'
mothers are van der Wijst sisters** (Anna Maria + Everdina) — a van der
Wijst-saturated union sitting one step off the direct line on the groom's side.

## Correction to the task brief

The lead described "Petrus van der Wijst (@I360@) married Antonia Cornelissen." In
the current GEDCOM this individual is **@I345@**, not @I360@ — Petrus van der Wijst
(b.5 Sep 1878, d.1 Feb 1963), son of **Cornelia Verwegen × Arnoldus van der Wijst**
(family @F106@), married Antonia Cornelissen. His marriage akte did not surface in

open sub-lead. He is a Verwegen-blooded van der Wijst (C-group type), nephew of
Geertruda Verwegen — not a VD×VD union.

## Bottom line

van der Wijst is a **confirmed structural intermarrying surname**, entering the
tree via (1) Cornelia van der Wijst → Jean collateral branch, and (2) Anna Maria
van der Wijst → the direct sibling line @I053@, and reinforced by (3) the
Verwegen×van der Wijst double sibling-swap. The 1904 union (A3) — both mothers van
der Wijst sisters — and the 1932/1940 van der Wijst-brothers × van
Duijnhoven-sisters swap (A4/A5) are the two strongest previously-undocumented
patterns. Recommend upgrading `ENDOGAMY_PATTERNS.md` §F from "MODERATE — lead" to a
**resolved** pattern and adding A3/A5 as named sub-patterns.
