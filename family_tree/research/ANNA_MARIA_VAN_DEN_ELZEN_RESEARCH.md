# Anna Maria van den Elzen Family Research

Research on the maternal grandmother of Johan van Duijnhoven.

## Summary

Anna Maria van den Elzen (1878-1952) married Marianus van Duijnhoven on 18 May 1906 in Boekel. Through archival research, we have now identified her complete parental lineage including her mother's maiden name (previously unknown as "Francyna, surname unknown").

## Zuster Cleopha Investigation

### Known Facts (from Delpher article, 1953)
- **Secular name:** Anna Maria van den Elzen
- **Born:** 3 November 1861, Oss
- **Congregation:** Dochters van Maria en Joseph (Daughters of Mary and Joseph)
  - Also known as: "Zusters van de Choorstraat"
  - Moederhuis: Choorstraat, 's-Hertogenbosch
  - Founded: 7 July 1820 by Pastoor Heeren
- **Religious life:** Entered 1878, celebrated 75 years in 1953
- **Locations:** Served ~60 years at Doesburg, retired at Nuland
- **Honors:** Papal distinction at 50-year jubilee, Royal distinction from Prince Bernhard at 70-year jubilee
- **Status in 1953:** Still alive at age 92

### Congregation Details
**Congregatie Dochters van Maria en Joseph** (official name)
- Founded: 7 July 1820 in 's-Hertogenbosch
- Founder: Pastoor Jacobus Heeren with Catharina van Hees
- Location: Choorstraat, in shadow of Sint Janskathedraal
- Original purpose: Care for the poor, sick, orphans
- Later activities: Education (including deaf-mute children), care for elderly
- Notable locations in Noord-Brabant included:
  - **Nuland:** Sint Jozefoord, Sint Martinusgesticht (where Zuster Cleopha retired)
  - **Oss:** H. Hartklooster
  - **Sint-Michielsgestel:** Doofstommeninstituut
  - And many more across Brabant and beyond

Source: [BHIC - De Dochters van Maria en Joseph](https://www.bhic.nl/ontdekken/verhalen/de-dochters-van-maria-en-joseph)

### Relationship Analysis

| Person | Birth Year | Birthplace | Father |
|--------|------------|------------|--------|
| Zuster Cleopha (Anna Maria van den Elzen) | 1861 | **Oss** | Unknown |
| Anna Maria van den Elzen (Johan's grandmother) | ~1878 | **Boekel** | Johannes van den Elzen |

**Observations:**
1. Both share the name "Anna Maria van den Elzen"
2. **Different birthplaces:** Oss vs Boekel (neighboring municipalities, ~12km apart)
3. **17 year age difference:** Born 1861 vs ~1878
4. Our Anna Maria's father, Johannes van den Elzen, was born 8 June 1838 in Boekel

**Possible Relationship:**
- The van den Elzen families of Oss and Boekel were likely related
- Zuster Cleopha could be a **cousin** or more distant relative
- Both areas are in the Land van Cuijk / Maasland region of Noord-Brabant
- Further research needed to establish the exact connection through shared ancestors
