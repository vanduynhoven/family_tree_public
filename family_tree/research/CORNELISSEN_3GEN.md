### Bakel en Milheeze

- Former municipality in Noord-Brabant, Netherlands
- Merged into Gemeente Gemert-Bakel in 1997
- Roman Catholic region with strong agricultural traditions
- Both Cornelissen and Verbruggen were prominent local surnames
