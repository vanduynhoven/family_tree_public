### 3. Surname Origin Analysis
The surname "Van Duynhoven" (and variants Van Dyn Hoven, Van Duijnhoven) is:
- **Toponymic**: Meaning "from Duynhoven" - a location in North Brabant, Netherlands
- **Extremely rare**: Very few families with this name immigrated to the US
- **Geographic clustering**: All US Van Duynhovens appear connected to either the Minnesota/Wisconsin Dutch settlements or historic Dutch New York

The probability of an **independent** Van Duynhoven line appearing in Greenwood, Arkansas in 1953 is extremely low.
