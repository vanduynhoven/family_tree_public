## Relationship to Arthur

Johannes Peter "Jean" van Duijnhoven is Arthur's great-great-great-great-great-granduncle:
- Jean (1757-1835) is the younger brother of **Petrus Johannes van Duijnhoven** (1748-1819)
- Petrus Johannes is Arthur's direct ancestor (GEN -2)
- Both are sons of **Joannes Peters van Duijnhoven** (1722-1795) and Allegondis Janssen van den Bout (van Haandel)
- Jean's descendants are **collateral cousins** to Arthur's direct line
