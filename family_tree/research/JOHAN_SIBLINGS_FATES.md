d.20 May 1949 Uden)** × **Anna Maria van den Elzen** (married 18 May 1906, Boekel).
Focus: the four less-documented older siblings of Johan (@I014@, b.12 Nov 1915,

Primary source: OpenArchieven / OpenArch API (`api.openarch.nl/1.0/records`),

## Why three of four fates stay open

Dutch civil-registration privacy windows (huwelijk 75 yr, overlijden ~50 yr) plus
incomplete post-1930 digitization mean the *own-marriage* and *death* records for
siblings who married in the 1930s–50s or died recently are frequently absent from
the open index. The pattern here is consistent: **birth aktes (1900s–1910s) are all
online and confirm parentage; later life events are online only where they fall
before the embargo and have been indexed** (Johanna Maria 1932, Martinus 1949).
