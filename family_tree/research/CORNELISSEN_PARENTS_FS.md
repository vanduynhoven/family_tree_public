This is a **daughter's birth record** mentioning the mother, NOT Anna Maria's own birth record from the Netherlands.

1. **FamilySearch's Dutch civil records** coverage for Noord-Brabant varies by municipality
2. **Bakel en Milheeze** civil registration records may not be fully indexed
3. **Anna Maria Cornelissen** (1916) is relatively recent - some Dutch privacy restrictions apply to records less than ~100 years old
