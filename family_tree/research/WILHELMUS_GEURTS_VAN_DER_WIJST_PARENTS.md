# Parents of Wilhelmus Geurts van der Wijst (@I490@) — one generation back

**Target:** Wilhelmus (Willem) **Geurts** van der Wijst — GEDCOM `@I490@`
- Married **11 Feb 1760, Zeeland (NB)** to Elisabeth Thomassen (`@I491@`), fam `@F171@`.
- Father of Joannes Willems van der Wijst (`@I472@`, m. 1793) and of Godefridus (1769),
  Petrus (1773), Franciscus (1774), Petrus Josephus (1777), all bap. Zeeland (NB).
- Goal: identify **his** parents (the Geurt/Godefridus van der Wijst generation).

**Location note:** "Zeeland" = the village of **Zeeland in Noord-Brabant**
(near Uden / Nistelrode / Gemert), **not** the province. All records are Roman
Catholic DTB from the Brabants Historisch Informatie Centrum (BHIC) via OpenArchieven.

## Evidence chain

1. **Wilhelmus's patronymic is consistently "Geurt(s)"** across every event:
   - Marriage 11 Feb 1760, Zeeland: `Wilhelmus Geurt(s) van der Wijst` × `Elisabet Thomasse`
     — bhi:853ea7fe-4629-11e3-a747-d206bceb4d38 ; bhi:d0da0dd8-ff51-f895-0bd2-5fd97abe93d1
   - Children's baptisms name father `Wilhelmi Geurts / Geurdi / Geurde van der Wijst`
     (1760, 1762, 1773, etc.).
   → A Dutch patronymic "Geurts/Geurt" ⇒ **his father's given name was Geurt**
     (= **Godefridus**, the Latin form used in the same registers).

2. **A Geurt van der Wijst is buried in the same parish, 7 Sep 1764** (Zeeland NB):
   - bhi:1d5d74d6-b434-fe50-3612-2dbe360aa1fe
   - Same surname, same village, dying 4 years after Wilhelmus's 1760 marriage — the
     natural candidate for the father. (Record is a bare name line: no age/spouse/parents.)

3. **Wilhelmus himself is active in Zeeland as a parent** — burial 16 Mar 1766 of an
   infant `N.N. van der Wijst` associated with `Wilhelmi Geurt van der Wijst`
   (bhi:c97a0ec8-71df-de94-623d-441d1d2806a5) — confirms the family is seated in Zeeland (NB).

4. **Naming pattern corroboration:** Wilhelmus's son baptized **Godefridus, 9 Nov 1769**
   (bhi:09cdb1a0-4629-11e3-a747-d206bceb4d38 — father `Wilhelmi van der Wijst`, mother
   `Elisabeth Thomasse`). Naming a son Godefridus fits the RK custom of naming after the
   paternal grandfather ⇒ consistent with the father being **Godefridus/Geurt**.

**Evidence strength: father's *name* = strong (patronymic + burial + naming pattern);
father's *identity as the 1764 burial* = moderate (single indirect record);
mother and grandparents = unresolved.**
