## Bernards Tree: Johannes van Duijnhoven (1793-1866) Branch

**Johannes van Duijnhoven** (b. 4 Aug 1793 Uden, d. 12 Dec 1866 Uden)
- Parents: Johannes Peter van Duijnhoven (1757-1835) × Anna Maria Jan Hendrik Korsten (1758-1796)
- Grandparents: Joannes Peters van Duijnhoven (1722-1795) × Allegondis Janssen van den Bout (1710-?)
- Married: **Cornelia van der Wijst** (1799-1862) on 13 Jun 1827 in Uden

### Children of Johannes × Cornelia van der Wijst:
1. **Martinus van Duijnhoven** (1828-1890) - akkerbouwer (farmer)
   - m. Elisabeth van Schijndel (1825-1884) on 22 Oct 1859 Uden
   - Children:
     - Wilhelmina (1862-1862)
     - Petrus Cornelius (1863-1916)
     - Maria (1865-1895)
     - Wilhelmina (1866-1867)
     - Johanna (1868-1870)
     - Levenloos/stillborn (1861)

2. **Ida van Duijnhoven** (1831-1869)

3. **Woutrina van Duijnhoven** (1833-1834) - died young

4. **Hendrikus van Duijnhoven** (1834-1835) - died young

5. **Joanna van Duijnhoven** (1836-1903)
   - m. Adrianus van de Wetering (1836-1917) on 21 Feb 1868 Uden
   - Had 6 children

6. **Anna Maria van Duijnhoven** (1838-1885)
   - m. Leonardus van de Wetering (1842-1894) on 19 Feb 1870 Uden
   - Note: Married brother of Joanna's husband (both married van de Wetering brothers)
   - Children (8, mostly stillborn):
     - Petrus van de Wetering (1876-1926)
     - Johannes van de Wetering (1877-1957)
     - 5 stillbirths (1871, 1871, 1873, 1874, 1880)

7. **Petronella van Duijnhoven** (1839-1840) - died young

8. **Wilhelmus van Duijnhoven** (1842-1842) - died young

## Teunissen Tree: Reinerus "Rein(ier)" van Duijnhoven (1804-1866)

**Reinerus van Duijnhoven** (baptized 3 May 1804 Mill, d. 3 May 1866 St. Hubert)
- Occupation: klompmaker (clog maker), landbouwer (farmer)
- Residence: Mill
- Parents: Joannes Johannes van Duijnhoven (1777-1842) × Maria Verbe(e)ten (1781-1834)
- Grandparents: Rudolphus (van) D(u)ijnhoven (1747-1795) × Hermina Loeffen (1743-1784)

**Marriage:** Cornelia Dominicussen alias van den Akker (1805-1865) on 6 May 1832 at Mil en St. Hubert
- Note: Marriage record states bride, bride's parents, and groom's mother could not sign (illiterate)

### Children of Reinerus × Cornelia:
1. **Johannes van Duijnhoven** (1833-1894)
2. **Anna Catharina van Duijnhoven** (1836-1906)
3. **Martinus van Duijnhoven** (1839-1913)
4. **Cornelis van Duijnhoven** (1841-1912)

## Research Gaps / Future Investigation

1. **Holleman marriage 1942** - No direct hit found for Karel Holleman × Johanna van Duijnhoven. The search returned unrelated Holleman records.

2. **Antonius van Duijnhoven × van Creij 1948 Nistelrode** - Found Johanna van Creij registration 1872 Nistelrode but not the 1948 marriage record itself. DeurnWiki shows Josephus Antonius van Duijnhoven (1905-1976) from Helmond line.

3. **Hendrica Wassenberg marriage** - Found Hendrica Maria van Duijnhoven death records but not a Wassenberg marriage.

4. **Access-restricted trees** - Both Genealogie Teunissen and Genealogie Van Duijnhoven have privacy restrictions on newer persons (637 and 1,299 respectively hidden). Contact F. Teunissen for access.
