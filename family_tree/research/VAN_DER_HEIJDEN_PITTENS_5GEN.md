**Research Date:** 2026-08-29

**Goal:** Extend lines 5 generations back from current GEDCOM entries

---

## Executive Summary

Successfully traced the VAN DER HEIJDEN line back to ~1460 (from ~1590 baseline), achieving 5+ generations extension. The VAN DER MEULEN and collateral lines (VAN BERCKEL, VERHAGEN, BLAFFARTS, ROMBOUTS) are also extensively documented back to the 1460s. The PITTENS line remains difficult to trace beyond Martinus Pittens (elder) due to limited online sources for this less-common surname in the Uden area.

## 7. PITTENS LINE - LIMITED RESULTS

### Starting Point in GEDCOM
- **@I154@** Mathias Pittens (b. 1 Jul 1709, Uden)
- **@I158@** Martinus Pittens (elder) x Maria Tijssen

### Research Status: INCOMPLETE

The Pittens surname appears to be relatively uncommon in the Uden/Brabant region. Despite extensive searches on:
- Genealogie Online
- Open Archieven (BHIC)
- WieWasWie

**No comprehensive Pittens genealogy extending beyond Martinus Pittens (elder) was found in online sources.**

### Suggested Next Steps for Pittens:
1. **Direct archive research** at Brabants Historisch Informatie Centrum (BHIC)
2. Check **Schepenbank registers** of Uden (property/legal records often predate church records)
3. Search **patronymic variants**: Pitten, Pittens, Pijtens, Pyttens
4. Consult **local genealogical society** (Heemkundekring Uden)
5. The name may derive from a toponym (place-name) - research locations called "Pit" or similar
