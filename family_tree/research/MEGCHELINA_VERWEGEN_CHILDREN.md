# Children of Megchelina Verwegen × Petrus van Duijnhoven

## Research Date
30 August 2026

## Critical Discovery: GEDCOM Correction Needed

**IMPORTANT**: The existing GEDCOM contains an error. @I046@ "Petrus Cornelus van Duijnhoven (b. 3 May 1867)" is listed as a son of Martinus van Duijnhoven (Gen 2) × Geertruda Verwegen, and the NOTE states he "Married Megchelina Verwegen."

This is INCORRECT. The records show:

1. **Petrus Cornelus van Duijnhoven** (b. 4 May 1863, d. 25 Jan 1916)
   - Parents: Martinus van Duijnhoven × **Elisabeth van Schijndel** (a DIFFERENT Martinus!)
   - Married: Petronella Donkers (10 Feb 1893, Uden)
   - Source: BHIC BS Huwelijk 10-2-1893; Death e5c5d3b0-6d16-2ed2-b044-22e6e91ea86e

2. **Petrus van Duijnhoven** (b. ~1870, d. 9 Mar 1942, Uden)
   - Parents: Martinus van Duijnhoven (Gen 2) × **Geertruda Verwegen** ← This is the correct son!
   - Married: Megchelina Verwegen (22 Jan 1904, Uden)
   - Source: BHIC BS Overlijden 9-3-1942 (bhi:75a55618-5c10-da37-1479-c962930128ff)

The @I046@ record should be REMOVED from the Gen 2 family (FAMC @F002@), or reclassified as a different person. A NEW INDI record should be created for the actual Petrus van Duijnhoven who married Megchelina.

## Marriage
- **Husband**: Petrus van Duijnhoven (son of Martinus van Duijnhoven Gen 2 × Geertruda Verwegen)
- **Wife**: Megchelina Verwegen (b. 25 Jul 1871, @I336@)
- **Date**: 22 January 1904
- **Place**: Uden, Noord-Brabant, Netherlands
- **Source**: BHIC BS Huwelijk (exact record not found in digital archive, confirmed by children's birth records)

## Cousin Marriage Note
This was a cousin marriage within the extended Verwegen-Van Duijnhoven family network:
- Megchelina's father Adrianus Verwegen was the brother of Geertruda Verwegen
- Geertruda Verwegen married Martinus van Duijnhoven (Gen 2)
- Petrus van Duijnhoven was Geertruda's son
- Therefore, Megchelina married her first cousin once removed
