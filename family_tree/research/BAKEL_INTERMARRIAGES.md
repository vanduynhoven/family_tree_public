# Van Duijnhoven <-> Bakel en Milheeze Intermarriages

Research into connections between the Van Duijnhoven family and the prominent Bakel
families Cornelissen and Verbruggen.

## Summary

**Total Cornelissen marriages in Bakel en Milheeze:** ~138  
**Total Verbruggen marriages in Bakel en Milheeze:** ~111  
**Van Duijnhoven connections identified:** 4 significant connections

### Key Finding

The 1950 marriage of Johan van Duijnhoven to Anna Maria Cornelissen represents the
**only direct Van Duijnhoven-Cornelissen marriage** found in Bakel en Milheeze records.
This marriage also connects Van Duijnhoven to the Verbruggen family through Anna
Maria's mother.

## Conclusion

Beyond the known 1950 union (Johan × Anna Maria Cornelissen), this research found:

1. **No additional direct Van Duijnhoven-Cornelissen marriages** in Bakel en Milheeze

2. **One direct Van Duijnhoven-Verbruggen marriage** (1887, Mill en Sint Hubert)

3. **Indirect Cornelissen connections** through the Swinkels family:
   - 1834: Antonia van Duijnhoven → Swinkels family
   - 1871, 1882: Swinkels descendants (Van Duijnhoven maternal line) → Cornelissen family

4. **The 1950 marriage is doubly significant** as it connects to both Cornelissen
   (via the groom) and Verbruggen (via the bride's mother) families

The Van Duijnhoven family's connections to Bakel were primarily through:
- A single direct marriage to Cornelissen (1950)
- A direct marriage to Verbruggen (1887, neighboring municipality)
- Indirect pathways via the Swinkels family (1834, with descendants marrying Cornelissen in 1871, 1882)
