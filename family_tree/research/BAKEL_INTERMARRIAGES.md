# Bakel en Milheeze — Van Duijnhoven Intermarriage Research

## Research Date: 2026-08-30
## Question: Do any Cornelissen/Verbruggen Bakel marriages represent additional VD↔Bakel unions?

## Key Finding: Van Duijnhoven marriages found in Bakel area ARE a different branch

The 1936, 1828, and 1896 records show Van Duijnhoven brides/grooms in the Erp and Beek en Donk area:
- 24/1/1936, Erp: Adriana Gijsberdina van Duijnhoven × Michiel Verbakel (father: Petrus van Duijnhoven)
- 9/10/1828, Beek en Donk: Adriana Maria van Duijnhoven × Leonardus Heesakkers (father: Laurens van Duijnhoven)
- 28/9/1896, Beek en Donk: Adriana van Duijnhoven × Antonius van de Laar (father: Petrus van Duijnhoven)

These are from the Erp/Beek en Donk area — NOT the Bakel en Milheeze village specifically.
They appear to be a separate Brabant Van Duijnhoven family (Erp is ~15km from Bakel).

## Conclusion: No additional VD↔Bakel intermarriages found

The 1950 union (Johan van Duijnhoven × Anna Maria Cornelissen, from Bakel en Milheeze)
remains the only documented Van Duijnhoven × Bakel marriage in the direct line.

The Cornelissen surname is extremely common in Bakel (15,760+ marriage records for Cornelissen
in Brabant area) — cross-referencing all of these against Van Duijnhoven would require
a more targeted database search with BHIC direct access.

## The Erp/Beek en Donk Van Duijnhoven family

A separate Van Duijnhoven family line exists in Erp and Beek en Donk (eastern Noord-Brabant):
- Laurens van Duijnhoven (parent in 1828 record)
- Petrus van Duijnhoven (parent in 1896 and 1936 records)
This appears to be a collateral Brabant branch, unrelated to the Uden direct line.
