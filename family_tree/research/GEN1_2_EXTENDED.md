## Henrica Andries van Duijnhoven (Different Family Line)

Multiple records found for "Henrica Andries van Duijnhoven" - this appears to be a different family:

**Marriage 1:** 11 Oct 1821, Boekel - married Zeger Waltherus Zegers/van Lieshout
**Death:** 22 Mar 1860, Boekel or 25 Nov 1907, Deurne

This is NOT our ancestor line - the patronymic "Andries" indicates a different father (Andries van Duijnhoven, not Petrus).

## Research Notes

### Ardina vs Geertrui Verwegen
Two Verwegen sisters married two Van Duijnhoven brothers:
- **Ardina Verwegen** married Johannes van Duijnhoven (26 Jan 1856)
- **Geertrui Verwegen** married Martinus van Duijnhoven (17 Feb 1858)

Both marriages are confirmed via BHIC records. The sisters were daughters of Joannes Verwegen.
