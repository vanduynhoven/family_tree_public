## PART 2: MARIA JAN JACOBS (Wife of Petrus Jansen van Duijnhoven 1679)

### Understanding the Name

"Maria Jan Jacobs" is a **patronymic construction**:
- **Maria** - given name
- **Jan** - father's name (Jan = Joannes)
- **Jacobs** - grandfather's name (Jacob)

This tells us:
- Maria's father was named **Jan** (Joannes)
- Maria's grandfather was named **Jacob**

### Generation 1: Maria Jan Jacobs (~1689-?)

**Known Information:**
- Born: approximately 1689 (based on 1718 marriage)
- Married: 6 February 1718, Uden
- Husband: Petrus Jansen van Duijnhoven (b. 1679)

**Marriage Record Search:**
- The 1718 marriage record was not directly located in the OpenArchieven index
- Early 18th century Uden DTB records have incomplete indexing
- The marriage is documented in family tree records but original source needs verification

### Generation 2: Jan Jacobs (Maria's Father)

**Deduced Information:**
- Name: Jan (Joannes)
- Father: Jacob (hence "Jacobs")
- Active period: ~1680-1720
- Location: Uden and surrounding area

**Search Results:**
- "Jan Jacobs" appears in 9,095 DTB Dopen records in the region
- The patronymic system makes precise identification difficult without additional identifiers
- No definitive match found linking to Maria's specific family

### Generation 3: Jacob (Maria's Grandfather)

**Deduced Information:**
- Name: Jacob
- Active period: ~1650-1690
- Location: Likely Uden area

**Search Results:**
- 409,449 records contain "Jacob" in the search period
- Without a family name/farm name, identification is not possible
- This represents the limit of traceable ancestry through patronymics

### Contextual Evidence - The Van Duijnhoven Line

**Petrus Jansen van Duijnhoven (Maria's husband):**
- "Petrus Jansen" indicates his father was Jan (Jansen = son of Jan)
- This means Petrus's father was also named Joannes/Jan

**Children evidence found:**
The couple had a son named **Joannes Petri van Duijnhoven** who appears as father in Uden records from 1757-1790:
- 24 July 1757 - child baptized in Uden (Source: bhi:2b127da6-e9ae-f671-c8a4-d0da2ee2e5b7)
- 31 August 1790 - father of Gerardus van Duijnhoven (Source: bhi:619337f9-c8d3-baa0-b344-573fa71a6941)

This Joannes Petri van Duijnhoven married **Gertrudis Joannis Franken** and their son:
- **Gerardus van Duijnhoven** - bapt. 31 August 1790, Uden
- This Gerardus married Geertruda van Leuken in 1823 (connecting both family lines)

## RESEARCH NOTES

### Challenges

1. **Pre-surname era records (before ~1811):** Individuals identified only by first name + patronymic
2. **Common names:** "Jan Jacobs" and "Jacob" are extremely common in 17th-18th century Brabant
3. **Incomplete indexing:** Early Uden DTB records may not be fully digitized/indexed
4. **No farm names:** Unlike some Dutch regions, Brabant records rarely include farm names that aid identification

### Recommended Next Steps

1. **WieWasWie.nl** - May have additional indexed records not in OpenArchieven
2. **BHIC reading room** - Physical DTB registers for Uden 1710-1720 may contain the 1718 marriage
3. **Notarial archives** - Property transfers or contracts may identify Maria's family
4. **Family tree cross-references** - Published van Duijnhoven genealogies may have primary source citations

### Connection Point

Both family lines converge at:
- **Gerardus van Duijnhoven** (1790-1866) x **Geertruda van Leuken** (1793-?)
- Married 8 February 1823 in Uden
- This couple had 6 children

Gerardus's great-grandfather was Petrus Jansen van Duijnhoven (husband of Maria Jan Jacobs), making Maria Jan Jacobs the 3x-great-grandmother through the van Duijnhoven male line.
