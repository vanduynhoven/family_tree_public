## Line 3 — Wassenberg

**Hendricus Johannes Wassenberg** (GEDCOM `@I041@`)
- **Parents:** Hendricus Wassenberg × Johanna van Duijnhoven.
- **Married** Hendrica Maria 'Riek' van Duijnhoven on **4 Jan 1946 in Uden** — one
  of the first post-liberation family weddings (Uden liberated Sep 1944).
- Marriage akte: `bhi:3132f6e9-4966-3aec-59ea-ab14445dca1a` (BHIC BS Huwelijk Uden).
  Names: bride Hendrica Maria van Duijnhoven; groom Hendricus Johannes Wassenberg;
  bride's parents Marianus van Duijnhoven & Anna Maria van den Elzen; groom's
  parents Hendricus Wassenberg & Johanna van Duijnhoven.

**⚠ Open lead — same-surname connection:** the groom's mother is recorded as
**"Johanna van Duijnhoven"**, i.e. the Wassenberg groom is himself half a van
Duijnhoven by descent. This raises the possibility of a kinship tie between the
bride's Marianus line and the groom's maternal van Duijnhoven line (a cousin/second-
cousin marriage was common in rural Catholic Brabant). This is currently
**unverified** — the groom's mother Johanna van Duijnhoven has not been placed
against Marianus's siblings or cousins. Recommend a follow-up cycle to identify her
birth/parents and test for a common ancestor.

GEDCOM added: parents Hendricus Wassenberg (`@I409@`) × Johanna van Duijnhoven
(`@I410@`) in family `@F139@`, with `@I041@` linked as their child.
