## API Search Summary

The Open Archieven API searches were conducted but many returned limited results:
- Death records post-1950 have restricted access
- Marriage records 1940s not fully digitized
- Dutch privacy laws restrict access to records <100 years for births, <50 years for marriages, <30 years for deaths

**Recommendation:** Contact BHIC directly or visit archives in person for restricted records.
