# Van Duijnhoven Lineage: Generation -8 to Generation -3

## Summary

This document establishes the **complete direct lineage** from Johannes "Jan Dirkse" van Duynhoven (1539-1590, Gen -8) to Petrus Jansen van Duynhoven (1679, Gen -3). The gap previously identified (1590-1645) has been fully resolved through cross-referencing two major genealogical sources:

1. **Genealogie Van Elk** (genealogieonline.nl) - Primary source for the direct line
2. **Genealogie Teunissen** (genealogieonline.nl) - Provides siblings and extended family

## Key Finding

The original task hypothesized that "Jan Dircx" (~1645) was the father of Petrus Jansen (1679). This was **partially correct but misidentified**:

- The correct father of Petrus Jansen is **Jan Jansen van Duynhoven (1660-1696)** (not "Jan Dircx")
- **Jan Dircx van Duynhoven** is actually the **grandfather** of Petrus Jansen, not his father
- The patronym "Jansen" in Petrus's name indicates his father was named Jan

## Correction to Original Task

The original task stated:
> "Gen -4: Jan Dircx van Duijnhoven (~1645) -- father of Petrus Jansen (1679, Gen -3)"

**Corrected genealogy**:
- **Jan Dircx** (m. 1645) is **Gen -5**, not Gen -4
- **Jan Jansen** (1660-1696) is **Gen -4** and the actual father of Petrus Jansen
- The patronym confusion: "Dircx" means son of Dirck; "Jansen" means son of Jan
- Petrus **Jansen** = son of **Jan** (Jan Jansen), not son of Dircx

## Researcher Notes

1. The Van Elk tree is more minimal, showing only the direct line with one child per generation
2. The Teunissen tree provides rich sibling and extended family data
3. Both trees agree on the core dates and relationships
4. The marriage date of 12 January 1645 (Gemert) for Jan Dircx is a key anchor point
5. The family extended back at least two more generations than originally identified (Gen -10 vs Gen -8)
6. The consanguineous marriage of Johannes to his cousin Jenneken (both van Duijnhoven) is noteworthy
