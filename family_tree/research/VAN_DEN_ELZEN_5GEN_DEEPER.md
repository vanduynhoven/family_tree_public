# Deep Ancestry Research: Van den Elzen, Van der Horst, Dapperen, Lamers

**Research Date:** 29 August 2026  
**Location Focus:** Boekel, Gemert, Noord-Brabant, Netherlands  
**Source:** OpenArchieven.nl (Brabants Historisch Informatie Centrum)

---

## Summary of Findings

This research extends the Van den Elzen and Van der Horst family lines back several generations from the known ancestors of Anna Maria van den Elzen (b. 1871).

### Key Discoveries

1. **Van der Horst Line**: Franciscus Wilhelmi van der Horst's patronymic reveals his father was **Wilhelmus van der Horst**
2. **Van den Elsen/Elzen Connection**: Catharina Huberti van den Elsen's patronymic reveals her father was **Hubertus Thonis van den Elsen** (married 1741 Gemert)
3. **Dapperen Line**: Ida Dapperen's parents confirmed as **Hubertus Dapperen x Cornelia van Leuken** (m. 25 Feb 1800 Boekel)
4. **Lamers Line**: Johanna Tonie Jan Lamers's patronymic indicates father was **Antonius (Tonie)** son of **Jan Lamers**

## Research Notes

### Confirmed Records
1. Ida Dapperen baptism 3 March 1810, Boekel - parents Hubertus Dapperen x Cornelia van Leuken ✓
2. Hubertus Dapperen x Cornelia van Leuken marriage 25 Feb 1800, Boekel ✓
3. Franciscus Wilhelmi van der Horst x Catharina Huberti van den Elsen marriage 21 April 1792, Boekel ✓
4. Hubertus Thonis van den Elsen x Maria Thonissen marriage 17 Jan 1741, Gemert ✓
5. Godefridus Aert Dapperen baptism 13 Dec 1759, Boekel - parents Aert Dapperen x Anna Cornelii Simons ✓

### Needs Further Research
1. **Johannes van den Elzen** (father of the 1838 Johannes) - birth record needed to confirm parents
2. **Wilhelmus van der Horst** - marriage record in Boekel ~1755-1760 needed
3. **Cornelia van Leuken** - baptism record needed to confirm parents
4. **Hubertus Dapperen** - baptism record needed to confirm if Godefridus is his father or uncle

### Naming Patterns Observed
- Strong use of patronymics (Wilhelmi, Huberti, Thonis, Jansse)
- Names often include father's first name + grandfather's first name (e.g., "Aert Jansse")
- Spelling variations common: Elzen/Elsen, Willem/Wilm/Wilhelmus
