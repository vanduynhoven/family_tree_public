# Parents of Mathias van de Weyer & Judocus Biemans (Bakel en Milheeze)

**Research question:** identify the parents of Mathias van de Weyer (father of Joost van de Weijer, @I437@) and Judocus Biemans (father of Josina Biemans, @I438@), both of the Bakel en Milheeze parish, Noord-Brabant.

**Anchor event:** Joost (Judocus) van de Weyer × Syna (Justina/Josina) Biemans, married **5 Feb 1769, Bakel en Milheeze**.
- Schepenbank version: `bhi:46fc19a4-401d-f58d-00da-87937e520174` (bride "Syna Joost Biemans", groom "Joost van de Weyer"; ondertrouw 21-01-1769; boek 11, fol.89).
- Rooms-Katholiek version: `bhi:2a97eecb-8227-8cf9-51de-a4e40bcfbceb` (bride "Justina Biemans", groom "Judocus van de Weyer"; dispensation 3rd/4th degree consanguinity; boek 1, fol.321).
- **Neither 1769 marriage index names the couple's parents** — the parentages below rest on patronymics and baptism records, not on the marriage act.

Source: OpenArchieven / BHIC (Brabants Historisch Informatie Centrum), Gemeentearchief Gemert-Bakel DTB.

## 1. Judocus Biemans — RESOLVED (father found)

**Judocus Biemans (@I438@) = Judocus "Jan" Biemans**, b. ~1710 Bakel en Milheeze.

Evidence chain:
- **Josina/Justina's baptism:** Doop **27-2-1740 Bakel en Milheeze**, child *Justina Biemans*, **father Judocus Joannis Biemans**, mother *Johanna*, witnesses Johannes Joosten & Wilhelma Janssen. `bhi:104a6f72-20bd-a93b-2fc9-2fbb684e7188` (full a2a record confirms father=Vader "Judocus [Joannis] Biemans", mother=Moeder "Johanna", child=Kind "Justina Biemans").
- **Judocus's marriage:** Trouwen **16-2-1738 Bakel en Milheeze**, *Judocus Jan Biemans × Johanna Judoci Janssen*. `bhi:81134232-f50a-4586-9968-c7bac8f62f68` (a2a: groom "Judocus [Jan] Biemans", bride "Johanna [Judoci] Janssen"). Timing (marriage 1738 → first child 1740) is consistent.
- **Sibling corroboration:** Doop **9-7-1742 Bakel**, child *Judocus Biemans*, father *Judocus Jan Biemans*, mother *Johanna* — a second child of the same couple, confirming the family.
- Josina's own children later recorded under her patronym "Justina **Judoci** Biemans" (e.g. 1770-1781 Bakel baptisms), consistent with father Judocus.

**Judocus's father = Joannes (Jan) Biemans** — derived from the patronym "**Joannis / Jan**" carried consistently by Judocus across the 1738 marriage and the 1740 & 1742 baptisms. No baptism for Judocus himself was located (pre-1710 register gaps), so his mother and exact birth are unconfirmed.

Evidence strength: **strong** (marriage act + 2 baptism acts naming Judocus Joannis/Jan Biemans).

## 2. Mathias van de Weyer — link to son CONFIRMED; his own parents NOT found

**Mathias van de Weyer (@I437@)**, b. ~1700 Bakel en Milheeze (estimated). Father of Joost (Judocus) van de Weyer (@I424@).

Evidence chain (patronymic + sibling cluster):
- Joost's children's baptisms 1770-1781 Bakel record him as "*Judocus **Mathys / Tysse** van de Weyer*" (son of Mathias). E.g. Doop 13-7-1778 Bakel: father *Judocus van de Weyer* + *Justina Judoci Biemans*, witness **Mathias van de Weyer**. Doop 1773 & 1781 pair *Judocus (Tys/Mathys) van de Weyer × Justina Judoci Biemans*.
- **Sibling cluster**, all recorded "**Mathei van de Weyer**" (children of one Mathias) in Bakel en Milheeze:
  - Wilhelma Mathei van de Weyer — m. **25-2-1753** Bakel (× Petrus Henrici van Asten).
  - Anna Mathei van de Weyer — m. **29-11-1755** Bakel (× Hermanus Isbrandi Nooyen); `bhi:8b394549-374c-aa8d-707a-d69416008972`.
  - Maria Mathei van de Weyer (witness 1753), Catharina Mathei van de Weyer (1773), Hubertus Mathei van de Weyer (1776/1778/1781), Johanna Mathei van de Weyer.
- This places one **Mathias van de Weyer** as a father in Bakel married ~1725, with children born ~1725-1745 — exactly the right generation for Joost.

**Mathias's own parents = UNKNOWN.** No marriage or baptism record for Mathias van de Weyer himself survives in the searchable BHIC index (the Bakel Rooms-Katholiek registers have gaps before ~1740, and the Schepenbank trouwboek used here begins 1743). His wife's name was also not recovered. Do not invent grandparents.

Evidence strength: **strong** for Mathias→Joost; **not established** for Mathias's parentage.
