# Woutrina van Duijnhoven — identified (last open endogamy lead)

**Question:** Investigate Woutrina van Duijnhoven, mother of Peter van der Voort, who
married Petronella Verwegen (a Verwegen sister) in 1859 — closing the last open
endogamy lead in the Verwegen ↔ Van Duijnhoven intermarriage web.

**Verdict:** RESOLVED. Confirmed. Evidence strength: **STRONG** (2 independent BHIC
civil-registry records agree on the parent names).

## Who she was

**Woutrina van Duijnhoven** — wife of **Joannes van der Voort**, of **Uden**,
Noord-Brabant. Mother of **Peter van der Voort** (b. 13 Sep 1826, Uden), the man who
married **Petronella Verwegen** on 21 May 1859 in Uden.

She is NOT a direct ancestor of Arthur. She is an *in-law of an in-law*: her son Peter
married a Verwegen sister. Her maiden surname (van Duijnhoven) is what makes this an
endogamy data point — it means a third Verwegen sister married into a Van Duijnhoven
family, mirroring the double bond already documented for Geertruda and Ardina Verwegen.

## Evidence

### 1. The 1859 marriage akte (the lead's source)
`bhi:70470602-c8b9-2b5a-e042-f39ff1d68936` — BS Huwelijk, Uden, 21-5-1859:
> Peter van der Voort × Petronella Verwegen; groom's parents **Joannes van der Voort
> × Woutrina van Duijnhoven**; bride's parents Joannes Verwegen × Mechelina van den Berk.

Note: the bride's parents (Joannes Verwegen × Mechelina van den Berk) are **exactly**
the parents of our ancestor Geertruda Verwegen — confirming Petronella is Geertruda's
sister (see GEDCOM @I136@ note).

### 2. Peter van der Voort's own birth record — corroborates the mother
`bhi:27776cb5-6989-b032-793f-5adfe84508a0` — BS Geboorte, Uden, 13-9-1826:
> Child **Peter van der Voort** (Man, b. 13-9-1826); mother **Woutrina van Duijnhoven**;
> father **Joannes van der Voort**.

Full A2A person data from the API `show.json` endpoint confirms the three persons and
the mother/father relation types. This is an independent record (a birth, 33 years
before the marriage) naming the same parent couple → corroboration = STRONG.

## The full set of "Woutrina van Duijnhoven" records at Uden (7 total)

A name search across ALL sources returns exactly 7 hits, all in Uden. They resolve to
**two different women** named Woutrina van Duijnhoven — do not conflate them:

**Woman A — our subject (wife of Joannes van der Voort):**
- `27776cb5…` Geboorte 13-9-1826 — son Peter van der Voort born (the 1859 groom).
- `1d7eeb73…` Overlijden 11-7-1835 — death of daughter **Maria van de Voort**, parents
  Johannes van de Voort × Woutrina van Duijnhoven.
- `70470602…` Huwelijk 21-5-1859 — she is "Moeder van de bruidegom" (the akte above).
- `91f307fd…` Overlijden 19-2-1917 — death of a **granddaughter** also named Woutrina
  van Duijnhoven?? Record lists Peter van der Voort × Petronella Verwegen as parents,
  i.e. this is a daughter of Peter & Petronella named after her grandmother (classic
  Dutch naming pattern). NOT the elder Woutrina. *(Surname "van Duijnhoven" on this
  child is an index artifact/error — parents are van der Voort × Verwegen.)*

**Woman B — a different Woutrina (wife of Johannes van Duijnhoven), unrelated to lead:**
- `487190ff…` Geboorte 9-6-1833 — parents Johannes van Duijnhoven × Woutrina van
  Duijnhoven (a van Duijnhoven × van Duijnhoven couple).
- `fdf6c733…` Overlijden 25-2-1834 — Cornelia van der Wijst, spouse Woutrina van
  Duijnhoven, father Joannes van Duijnhoven.
- `612d4b30…` Overlijden 1-7-1865 "Relatie" — Ardina Bevers / van de Voort household.

## What could NOT be confirmed

- **Woutrina's own birth record** (~1795–1805) and **her parents**: not isolable. A
  "van Duijnhoven" birth search at Uden returns 3,642 hits — the surname is extremely
  common there — and no record uniquely tied to *this* Woutrina surfaced.
- **The Joannes van der Voort × Woutrina van Duijnhoven marriage record**: the BHIC
  marriage index for pre-1826 Uden did not return this couple on combined-name search.
  (The son's 1826 birth implies a marriage c. 1820–1826.)

These gaps do not weaken the endogamy finding, which rests on the two records above.

## GEDCOM impact

No GEDCOM edit made. Woutrina is a collateral in-law-of-in-law, not a direct ancestor,
and her own parentage is unconfirmed. Recording her without confirmed parents/birth
would add a speculative node. The endogamy fact is fully captured in this research note
and already contextualised by the @I136@ / @I148@ Verwegen-sister notes. If desired, a
NOTE could be appended to Petronella Verwegen / the Verwegen family record cross-
referencing this third sister→Van Duijnhoven marriage.
