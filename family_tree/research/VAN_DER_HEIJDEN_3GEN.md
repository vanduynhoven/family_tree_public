## Research Notes & Questions

### IMPORTANT DISAMBIGUATION
There are MULTIPLE individuals named "Joannes Henrici van der Heijden" in the records:
1. **Joannes Henrici van der Heijden (b.1712 Veghel)** - son of Henricus Henrici x Theodora van der Meulen - THIS is likely Johanna's father
2. **Joannes Henrici van der Heijden (b.1757 Erp)** - son of Henricus van der Heijden x Joanna Maes - DIFFERENT family line
3. **Joannes Henrici van der Heijden (b.1760 Erp)** - also son of Henricus x Joanna Maes - DIFFERENT family line

The Joannes who married Anna Maria Pittens (Johanna's mother) appears to be the one born 1712 in Veghel, based on the naming pattern (Henrici = son of Henricus) and timing.
