## 4. Marianus's own Gen 4 children — marriages of the undocumented ones

Marianus (@I005@) × Anna Maria van den Elzen (m. 18 May 1906 Boekel) had 12 children
1907–1923. Already-confirmed marriages (per prior research, not re-done here):
- Johanna Maria (1913) → Petrus van Rijbroek, 1932 Uden
- Antonius/"Toon" (1916) → Wilhelmina Petronella van Creij, 26 Oct 1948 Nistelrode (@F… in GEDCOM)
- Hendrica Maria (1923) → Hendricus Wassenberg, 1946 Uden

**Undocumented children searched (Francina 1907, Martinus 1908, Gerardus 1909,
Hubertus 1911, Jan 1912, Johanna 1914, Gerarda Maria 1919, Marinus 1920):**

- **No BHIC OpenArchieven marriage record** could be matched to any of them by the
  parent pair **Marianus van Duijnhoven × Anna Maria van den Elzen**. Every candidate
  returned (Francina→Slits lines in Gemert/Bakel; Marinus→van Helmond in Deurne,
  →Koolen in Erp, →Prose in Helmond) belongs to *different* van Duijnhoven families
  with different parents.
- **Root cause:** Dutch civil marriage registers are subject to a **75-year privacy
  closure** (deaths ~50yr, marriages ~75yr). Marriages of children born 1907–1920 fall
  largely in **~1930–1955**, which is mostly still closed or not yet digitised in the
  open BHIC index. This is why the three *already-confirmed* Gen 4 marriages were
  established through channels other than the open API, and why the remaining ones do
  not surface here.
- **Conclusion:** No new marriages or descendants confirmable from OpenArchieven at
  this time. **No GEDCOM change.** These lines require FamilySearch, family records,
  or the BHIC reading-room (closed records) to advance.
