## Part 2: Mechelina van den Berk (Verwegen Line)

### Context
- **Mechelina van den Berk** = wife of Johannes Verwegen
- Marriage stated as: 13 Feb 1820 Uden
- Candidate baptism: 11 Dec 1807 Gemert (BHI:39b6387b-aa64-d721-3662-57e5555cc913)

### Baptism Record Found (Gemert 1807):

**Mechelina van den Berk Baptism:**
- Date: **11 December 1807**, Gemert (RK)
- Child: Mechelina van den Berk
- Father: **Henricus Jacob van den Berk**
- Mother: **Joanna Maria Franciscus Slaats**
- Witnesses: 
  - Joannes van den Berk
  - Maria Franciscus Slaats
  - Martinus van den Heuvel
- Source: BHI:39b6387b-aa64-d721-3662-57e5555cc913
- Archive: Gemeentearchief GEMERT-BAKEL, RK doopboek 1806-1810, folio 73

**Note:** The witnesses "Joannes van den Berk" and "Maria Franciscus Slaats" are likely the paternal grandparents.

### Marriage Record Issue - DISCREPANCY FOUND:

**1819 Uden Marriage (Different Mechelina):**
- Date: **31 January 1819**, Uden
- Groom: Joannes Verweegen (b. 18 Apr 1795 Uden)
- Bride: Michaelina van den Berk (birthplace listed as Uden, not Gemert)
- Father of groom: **Arnoldus Verweegen**
- Mother of groom: **Adriana Klaassen**
- Father of bride: **Arnoldus van den Berk**
- Mother of bride: **Petronella van de Voort**
- Source: BHI:0f3848b2-7473-84ee-895e-fa2c98028e37

⚠️ **IMPORTANT:** This 1819 marriage shows a **DIFFERENT** Mechelina van den Berk:
- The 1819 bride's parents are Arnoldus van den Berk x Petronella van de Voort (Uden)
- The Gemert baptism shows parents Henricus Jacob van den Berk x Joanna Maria Franciscus Slaats

### Conclusions for Mechelina:

**IF the Verwegen line Mechelina is from the 1819 marriage:**
- Father: **Arnoldus van den Berk**
- Mother: **Petronella van de Voort**
- (This is NOT the Gemert Mechelina)

**IF the correct Mechelina is from Gemert (b. 1807):**
- Father: **Henricus Jacob van den Berk**
- Mother: **Joanna Maria Franciscus Slaats**
- Grandparents (paternal): Joannes van den Berk x Maria Franciscus Slaats
- A different marriage record is needed (not found yet)

**Recommendation:** 
Verify which Mechelina is correct by:
1. Finding the death record of Mechelina van den Berk (wife of Johannes Verwegen) which should state birthplace
2. Finding children's birth records which state mother's birthplace
3. The 1858 marriage of Geertruij Verwegen x Martinus van Duijnhoven shows parents "Johannes Verwegen" x "Mechelina van den Berk" - check if mother's details are given

**Evidence strength: WEAK to MODERATE** (conflicting candidates)
