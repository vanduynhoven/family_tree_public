# Is Cornelis Verwegen (grandfather of Hendricus Wassenberg Sr.) from the main Verwegen family?

**Research date:** 30 August 2026
**Source:** OpenArchieven.nl / BHIC (Brabants Historisch Informatie Centrum) civil (BS) and church (DTB) records
**Question:** Cornelis Verwegen is the father of Joanna Maria Verwegen (who married Joannes Wassenberg, 1868) and thus a great-grandfather of Hendricus Johannes Wassenberg (`@I041@`). Is he related to the main Verwegen family — Johannes Verwegen × Mechelina van den Berk, parents of Geertruda who married Martinus van Duijnhoven?

---

## VERDICT

**YES — Cornelis Verwegen belongs to the main Verwegen family.** He is a **son of Geurt (Godefridus Petri) Verwegen × Helena (Leen Jan) Gielens** (`@I123@` × `@I124@`), the couple recorded as the parents of **Johannes Verwegen** (`@I121@`, the main-family patriarch, husband of Mechelina van den Berk).

On the evidence, **Cornelis Verwegen (b. 1775) is a brother of Johannes Verwegen (b. ~1795)**, making him the **uncle of Geertruda Verwegen** and a granduncle-by-blood into the Wassenberg branch. Evidence strength: **strong** for Cornelis's own parentage (multiple independent records); **moderate** for the brother link, because the main-family Johannes's own parentage still carries a documented source discrepancy (see Caveat).

This research also uncovered **two data errors already present in the GEDCOM** that should be corrected (see "Corrections required").

---

## Two errors in the current premise / GEDCOM

### Error 1 — Joanna Maria Verwegen's MOTHER is Joanna van Berlo, NOT Hester van Wijhoven
The task premise (and the current GEDCOM `@F170@`) records Joanna Maria Verwegen's parents as *Cornelis Verwegen × Hester van Wijhoven*. This is a misreading of the 1868 marriage act. The full act shows:

> **Huwelijk 21-2-1868 Uden** — Bruidegom **Joannes Wassenberg** (b. 6 Dec 1830 Uden), parents **Albertus Wassenberg × Hester van Wijhoven**. Bruid **Joanna Maria Verwegen** (b. 11 Nov 1833 Uden).
> Source: bhi:093f9a8d-0e04-97db-788b-fa69f50f7d23

**Hester van Wijhoven is the GROOM's mother** (wife of Albertus Wassenberg), not the bride's. Joanna Maria Verwegen's true parents are confirmed by two independent records:
- **Birth act:** Geboorte 11-11-1833 Uden, akte 151 — **Vader Cornelis Verwegen, Moeder Joanna van Berlo**, kind Joanna Maria Verwegen. Source: bhi:87dad4ee-9afb-9204-f594-2b3ea4c91cac
- **Death act:** Overlijden 11-5-1917 Uden — Overledene Joanna Maria Verwegen, **Vader Cornelis Verwegen, Moeder Joanna van Berlo**, Relatie Joannes Wassenberg. Source: bhi:7fad5c47-d8aa-ce14-2107-a4f8a12392ad

So the GEDCOM has the two mothers **swapped**: `@F169@` currently says "Albertus Wassenberg × Joanna van Berlo" and `@F170@` says "Cornelis Verwegen × Hester van Wijhoven". They should be **Albertus Wassenberg × Hester van Wijhoven** and **Cornelis Verwegen × Joanna van Berlo**.

### Error 2 — Cornelis Verwegen's parentage was unknown; it is now established
`@I488@` Cornelis Verwegen had no parents in the tree. He is now identified:

- **Cornelis Verwegen, b. 18 September 1775 Uden**, son of **Gerardus (Geurt/Godefridus Petri) Verwegen × Helena Gielens**.
  - **2nd marriage:** Huwelijk 2-3-1827 Uden, akte 8 — Bruidegom **Cornelis Verwegen (geb. 18-9-1775 Uden), weduwnaar van Jennemie de Groot; Vader van de bruidegom Gerardus Verwegen, Moeder Helena Gielens**; Bruid **Joanna van Berlo (geb. 20-6-1797 Boekel)**, dr. van Lambertus van Berlo × Maria van der Leemputten. Source: bhi:47786d8b-9e37-1770-1f80-2f1682331d2e
  - **1st marriage:** DTB Trouwen 25-2-1810 Uden — **"Cornelis Geurt Verwegen" × Jennemie Gijsberts de Groot** (patronymic "Geurt" = son of Geurt). Source: bhi:7480cb0e-6f5d-a268-9b2c-7641c47cda1a
  - **Child of 1st marriage:** Doop 4-12-1810 Uden, Gertrudis Verwegen, father **"Cornelis Godefridi (vulgo Geurt) Verwegen"** ("Godefridi vulgo Geurt" = son of Godefridus, called Geurt). Source: bhi:b3af786a-ec96-ebcb-7cd3-8b6e41a6eac7

The father named — **Gerardus / Geurt / Godefridus Petri Verwegen × Helena Gielens** — is exactly `@I123@` Geurt Peter Verwegen × `@I124@` Helena Gielens already in the tree (m. 31 Jan 1773 Boekel), the recorded parents of Johannes Verwegen (`@I121@`).

## Family structure (proven)

```
Geurt (Godefridus Petri) Verwegen  ×  Helena (Leen Jan) Gielens        (@I123@ × @I124@, m.1773 Boekel)
        |
        ├── Cornelis Verwegen (b.18 Sep 1775 Uden)                     ← Joanna Maria's father  [@I488@]
        │       ├── m1 (1810) Jennemie Gijsberts de Groot
        │       └── m2 (1827) Joanna van Berlo (b.1797 Boekel)
        │               └── Joanna Maria Verwegen (b.11 Nov 1833)      [@I485@]
        │                       × Joannes Wassenberg (m.1868)          [@I484@]
        │                               └── Hendricus Wassenberg Sr. (b.1873)  [@I409@]
        │
        └── Johannes Verwegen (b.~1795 Uden)                            ← main-family patriarch  [@I121@]
                × Mechelina van den Berk (m.1819 Uden)                  [@I122@]
                        └── Geertruda Verwegen (b.7 Sep 1833)           [@I004@]
                                × Martinus van Duijnhoven (m.1858)      [@I003@]
```

**Consequence:** Joanna Maria Verwegen (Wassenberg branch) and Geertruda Verwegen (van Duijnhoven branch) are **first cousins** — both granddaughters of Geurt Peter Verwegen × Helena Gielens. Both were, coincidentally, born in 1833 (Geertruda 7 Sep, Joanna Maria 11 Nov). This is a genuine blood tie between the Wassenberg line and the core Verwegen/van Duijnhoven line.

## CAVEAT (why the brother link is "moderate", not "strong")

The existing research files (`VERWEGEN_3GEN.md`, `VERWEGEN_5GEN_DEEPER.md`) and the GEDCOM note on `@I121@` flag an **unresolved discrepancy** in the main-family Johannes's OWN parentage:
- One "Johannes Verwegen, son of Geurt Verwegen × Helena Gielens" married Anna Maria van der Putten (13 Feb 1820) and **died 29 June 1826** — which cannot be Geertruda's father (his children run to 1833).
- The 1819 Uden marriage akte used to seat `@F026@` (bhi:0f3848b2, akte 3) reportedly names the groom's parents as **"Arnoldus Verweegen × Adriana Klaassen"**, not Geurt × Helena.

So while **Cornelis's** descent from Geurt Peter Verwegen × Helena Gielens is firmly documented, the claim that he and the main-family **Johannes** are brothers depends on the main-family Johannes truly being a son of that same couple — which is currently asserted in the tree but not fully reconciled with the 1819 akte. The Geurt × Helena connection is where any remaining doubt sits, and it affects the whole main line equally, not just this cousin link.

## Recommended GEDCOM actions
1. **Fix the swapped mothers:** `@F169@` → Albertus Wassenberg × **Hester van Wijhoven**; `@F170@` → Cornelis Verwegen × **Joanna van Berlo**. Update `@I489@` Hester van Wijhoven to be Joannes Wassenberg's mother; add/relink Joanna van Berlo (b.1797 Boekel) as `@I485@`'s mother.
2. **Seat Cornelis Verwegen (`@I488@`)** as a child of `@F027@` (Geurt Peter Verwegen × Helena Gielens), i.e. a sibling of Johannes `@I121@`. Add BIRT 18 Sep 1775 Uden; note m1 Jennemie de Groot (1810), m2 Joanna van Berlo (1827).
3. Optionally add Joanna van Berlo's parents Lambertus van Berlo × Maria van der Leemputten.
4. Before hard-linking the *brother* relationship, resolve the `@I121@` 1819-akte discrepancy (VERWEGEN_CORRECTION.md) so the shared-parent claim is on firm ground.
