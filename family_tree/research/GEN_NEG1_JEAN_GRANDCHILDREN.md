# Grandchildren of Jean (Johannes Petrus) van Duijnhoven (1757-1835)

Research documenting the children of Gerardus (1790-1866) and Johannes (1793-1866),
both sons of Jean van Duijnhoven and Anna Maria Jan Hendrik Korsten.

- **Primary**: Brabants Historisch Informatie Centrum (BHIC) via Open Archieven API
