# Antonius van Duijnhoven (b. 1865) - Death Records Research

**Research Date:** 29 August 2026  
**Source:** Open Archieven (openarchieven.nl)

## Summary

This research focused on finding death records for **Antonius van Duijnhoven** (b. 11 Nov 1865, son of Martinus 1829) and his two wives:
1. **Lamberdina van Dijk** (first wife, married 1894, died ~1901-1902)
2. **Antonia Maria Vermulst** (second wife, married 1903)
