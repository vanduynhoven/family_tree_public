## The "Fairman" Name Etymology

"Fairman" is an unusual given name. Research shows:

**As a Surname:**
- English origin from Old English "faeger" (fair/beautiful) + "mann" (man)
- Or from Old Norse "Farmathr" (seafarer/traveling merchant)
- Found in USA primarily in Pennsylvania (1840), spreading to other states by 1920

**As a Given Name:**
- Historically used when:
  1. It's the mother's maiden name given as a first name
  2. It's a family surname honoring maternal or paternal lineage
  3. Named after a specific ancestor named Fairman

**Hypothesis:** Fairman Campbell may have been named after:
- His mother Genevieve's maiden name "Fairman"
- His father's middle name
- A maternal grandfather named Fairman

This suggests searching for **Fairman** as a surname in connection with the Campbell family in Minnesota or neighboring states.
