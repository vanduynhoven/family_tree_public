# Joannes Cornelissen (@I428@, ~1750 Bakel) — Parents Research

**Goal:** Find the parents of Joannes Cornelissen (@I428@, b. ~1750 Bakel),
who married Willemijn van de Meulenhof (@I429@); together the parents of
Jan Cornelissen.

**Status: NOT FOUND** — records exist as unindexed FamilySearch scans, but are
not reachable via the public OpenArchieven index or a signed-out FamilySearch session.

---

## What WAS confirmed

The couple Joannes Cornelissen × Willemijn van de Meulenhof is corroborated by
the civil marriage act of their son **Theodorus Cornelissen**:

- **Huwelijk 7 May 1815, Bakel en Milheeze**, akte nr. 4
  - Bruidegom: Theodorus Cornelissen, geboren te Bakel
  - **Vader van de bruidegom: Joannes Cornelissen**
  - **Moeder van de bruidegom: Willemijn van de Meulenhof**
  - Bruid: Joanna Maria van Leunen (dr. van Willem van Leunen & Ida Sterken)
  - Source: BHIC, BS Huwelijk, archief 50, inv. 374, Huwelijksregister Bakel en
    Milheeze 1815
  - URL: https://www.openarchieven.nl/bhi:5154a458-01dd-da70-6ccb-9746ebbd49ed

This names Joannes+Willemijn as PARENTS but gives **no data on Joannes's own
parents** (grandparents of Theodorus/Jan).

## Root cause

The relevant records — Joannes Cornelissen's baptism (~1750) and his RK marriage
to Willemijn van de Meulenhof (~1775–1785) — would be in the **Bakel en Milheeze
Rooms-Katholiek doop-/trouwboek**:
- archief **8009**, inv. **1** (RK doop- en trouwboek 1686–1795)
- archief **8009**, inv. **25** (RK trouwboek 1795–1854)

These books are **scanned but only partially person-indexed** on OpenArchieven
for the Cornelissen family in this period, so name search returns nothing. The
1815 act page itself notes the huwelijksbijlagen for Bakel en Milheeze 1814–1816
are "beschikbaar via FamilySearch" — i.e. the images live on FamilySearch and
must be browsed, not searched.
