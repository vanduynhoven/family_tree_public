# Van Leuken Family Connection Research

*Research Date: 30 Aug 2026*

---

## Discovery Summary

**CONFIRMED: Van Leuken appears on BOTH sides of Anna Maria van den Elzen's ancestry!**

Anna Maria van den Elzen (@I006@), wife of Marianus van Duijnhoven (Gen 3), has Van Leuken ancestors on BOTH her maternal lines:

1. **Paternal grandmother's line (van den Elsen):** Joanna Maria Peter van Leuken = wife of Dirck Cornelis van den Elsen
2. **Maternal grandmother's line (Dapperen):** Cornelia van Leuken = wife of Hubertus Dapperen

**These two women are SISTERS** - both daughters of **Peter van Leuken** and **Jenneke Jan Bankers** of Boekel.

## Pedigree Collapse

This confirms a **pedigree collapse** in the Van Duijnhoven family tree:

Anna Maria van den Elzen's great-great-great-grandparents on her **father's side** (Peter van Leuken x Jenneke Jan Bankers) are the same as her great-great-grandparents on her **mother's side**.

**Coefficient of inbreeding:** The common ancestors are at generation 5 (paternal) and 4 (maternal) from Anna Maria van den Elzen. This is a relatively distant relationship and would have been typical in rural Brabant communities.
