# Van Boxtel — Extended Ancestry Pre-1767

**Research question:** Identify the parents and grandparents of **Henricus Peters
(Petri) van Boxtel** (d. 21 Feb 1789, Uden), father of Elisabeth Henricus van Boxtel
(~1767–1856), and assess the alleged 1649 *van Boxtel × van Collenberch* connection.

**Date:** 30 August 2026
**Sources:** OpenArchieven API (BHIC DTB records) — re-run and re-verified this cycle.
**Status:** Gen −2 fully confirmed; Gen −3 (Henricus's father Petrus) **NOT yet
proven**; the 1649 van Collenberch lead assessed as **likely a false lead** (wrong parish).

## 2. Gen −3 — Petrus van Boxtel (Henricus's father): STILL UNPROVEN

**No baptism of Henricus Petri van Boxtel is indexed in the Uden DTB** in the plausible
window (searched *Henricus van Boxtel*, relationtype = Gedoopte/Kind, eventplace = Uden,
1715–1755 → **0 hits**; and relationtype = Kind, father = Petrus → **0 hits**). Likewise
**no marriage of a Petrus van Boxtel × N.N.** in Uden produces Henricus's parents
(the only pre-1780 Petrus-van-Boxtel Uden marriage hit is a 1773 *widower* remarriage,
"Petrus van Boxtel, Adriana N.N." as *Eerdere man* — `bhi:214a34ad-cf36-224c-eae8-88658099b689`).

Consequences:
- Henricus (b. ~1720–1745) was **either baptized before the surviving Uden register
  begins, or baptized in another parish** and moved to Uden as an adult.
- The prior note in `VAN_BOXTEL_5GEN_DEEPER.md` placing Gen −3 Petrus and Gen −4
  Adrianus in **Oirschot** rests on Oirschot van Boxtel baptisms/marriages that share
  the surname but have **no proven link** to the Uden Henricus. Treat those as
  candidate leads, not confirmed ancestry.

**Oldest van Boxtel couple actually attested IN Uden** (candidate great-/grandparent
generation, link unproven):
- **Petrus (Aren/Arts) van Boxtel × Elisabetha Gorts**, children baptized Uden
  6 Sep 1679 (Joannes) and 10 Jul 1688 (Anna). "Aren Arnoldus" / "Arts" = son of Arnold.
  Identifiers `bhi:77c17f89-55bf-375d-8941-cbbe73ebf28e`, `bhi:68c1013a-85eb-3e5f-26a0-6fedebf51773`.
  Chronologically this Petrus is too early to be Henricus's *direct* father, but could be
  a grandfather; not demonstrated.

**Evidence strength on Gen −3 identity: WEAK.** Do not enter a specific Petrus into the
GEDCOM as Henricus's father until a baptism or marriage record ties them.

## 3. The 1649 "Henricus Peters van Boxtel × Anna Peters van Collenberch" lead — LIKELY FALSE

Assessment this cycle: **the van Collenberch family is an Oirschot cluster, ~40 km from
Uden, and does not connect to the Uden van Boxtel line.**

- A search for *van Collenberch* 1640–1670 returns **639 records, overwhelmingly in
  Oirschot** (notarial acts + DTB), e.g. the van Collenberch/van den Collenberch families
  of Adriaen, Aelbert Martens, Guielmus Petrus, etc. — all Oirschot.
- The related marriage cited in prior notes, *Joannes Petrus van Boxtel × Maria Petrus
  van Collenberg, 20 Aug 1641, Oirschot*, is likewise **Oirschot**, and names *Joannes*
  (not Henricus) as groom.
- A combined *Collenberg + Boxtel* search returns **0** records outside that Oirschot
  context.

**Conclusion:** the 1649 van Boxtel × van Collenberch marriage, if it exists, belongs to
an **Oirschot van Boxtel branch**, not to our Uden line. It is ~120 years before our
Henricus and geographically disjoint. **Recommend dropping it as a direct-ancestry lead**
unless an Oirschot→Uden migration of a specific individual is independently documented.

**Evidence strength: MODERATE** (single-source geographic clustering; enough to
down-weight the lead, not to positively exclude an undiscovered link).

## 4. Corroboration of the daughter Elisabeth's identity (bonus)

Records naming *"Elisabeth Henrici van Boxtel"* as mother, married to a *van Duijnhoven*,
appear in Uden 1797 and 1799 — consistent with our Elisabeth (daughter of **Henrici** =
Henricus) marrying Petrus Joannis van Duijnhoven:
- 17 Dec 1797 Uden — child of Elisabeth Henrici van Boxtel × Joannes van Duijnhoven
  (`bhi:cad419ab-1fc3-ec69-16a5-30e381f80cf0`, already in GEDCOM @I…@).
- 27 Aug 1799 Uden — child, parents Petrus Joannis van Duijnhoven × Elisabeth Henrici
  van Boxtel (`bhi:48f86655-d2c0-585b-282c-7379cfa41dc3`).

This re-confirms the Gen −1 → Gen −2 link (Elisabeth = daughter of Henricus Petri).
