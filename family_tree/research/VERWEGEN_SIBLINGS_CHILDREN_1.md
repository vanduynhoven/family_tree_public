## Family 2: Antonius Verwegen x Joanna van Berlo

### ⚠️ MARRIAGE RECORD NOT FOUND

**Claimed:** Marriage 28 May 1853, Uden

**Research Result:** No marriage record found for "Antonius Verwegen x Joanna van Berlo" in 1853 in Uden or surrounding areas.

The 28/5/1853 record that appeared in searches shows **Antonius Verwegen as "Vader van de bruidegom"** (father of the groom), not as groom himself.

## Critical Notes

### Joanna van Berlo
The name "Joanna van Berlo" appears in Uden records but married to different people:
- Joanna Catharina van Berlo married Joannes Mulder (2-5-1818, 's-Hertogenbosch)
- Various other Joanna van Berlo records relate to the Mulder family

**Recommendation:** The claim that Antonius Verwegen married Joanna van Berlo on 28 May 1853 needs source verification. This marriage does not appear in:
- BHIC digitized records
- OpenArchieven database
- WieWasWie database

### The Correct Antonius Verwegen (Brother of Geertruda)
Based on parent matching, **Antonius Verwegen who married Petronella van Duijnhoven in 1860** is confirmed as a sibling of Adriana and Geertruda Verwegen, as they all have parents:
- Father: Johannes (Joannes) Verwegen
- Mother: Mechelina van den Berk
