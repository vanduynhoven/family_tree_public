### Name Analysis
The name "Allegondis Janssen van den Bout (van Haandel)" contains important clues:

1. **Allegondis/Aldegondis** - Common Catholic first name in Brabant, honoring Saint Aldegonde
2. **Janssen** - Patronymic meaning "daughter of Jan/Johannes" - indicates her father's first name was **Jan (Johannes)**
3. **van den Bout** - Topographic surname meaning "from the bout" (a short dike section projecting into a river, per CBG Familienamenbank)
4. **van Haandel** - Alternative surname, possibly from a hamlet/location "Haandel" near Erp/Uden

The dual surname (van den Bout / van Haandel) suggests either:
- An alias used interchangeably by the family
- A farm/house name combined with a family name
- A maternal surname carried alongside paternal

### Surname Origin Analysis

**Van den Bout:**
Per CBG Nederlandse Familienamenbank:
> "Den Bout = 'kort in de rivier uitstekend dijkgedeelte'" (a short dike section projecting into a river)

The surname originates from Hardinxveld area (Zuid-Holland), with documented bearers from 1500s:
- Joris Brandsz → Joachim Jorisz → Rochus Joachimsz van den Bout (documented 1602, Hardinxveld)

However, the Brabant "van den Bout" families may have separate origins or be a topographic name applied independently to people living near similar geographic features.

**Van Haandel:**
This surname appears concentrated in the Erp/Uden/Veghel area of Noord-Brabant, suggesting a local origin, possibly from a hamlet or farm named "Haandel."
