## 4. RESEARCH GAPS — what is still missing

### Cross-branch linkage (the biggest open question)
1. **The Wisconsin ↔ Minnesota link.** Both trace to Uden, Noord-Brabant, but the founding WI immigrant (father/grandfather of anchor **Albert Van Dyn Hoven**) has not been individually identified. Need his Little Chute birth/baptism (St. John Nepomucene parish) → then his **Uden baptism** in BHIC/Open Archieven, cross-checked against the Petrus→Martinus→Marianus line.
2. **The New York branch's deeper Dutch roots.** Locate **John van Duynhoven Sr.'s** (× Marie Tol) own birth/baptism to confirm whether the line is purely Noord-Holland or has any Brabant tie (currently judged a separate family).

### Dutch-side records
3. **De-duplicate the 5,563 Brabant event-rows** into distinct individuals — the `DUTCH_FAMILY_DATABASE.md` index is per-event, so a true historical headcount is not yet computed.
4. **The 1762–1782 Uden baptism gap** blocks confirming Generation −2/−3 (Petrus Joannis's exact baptism and parents). Check physical BHIC scans and neighbouring parishes (Boekel, Gemert, Volkel, Erp, Zeeland).
5. **Unresolved spouse-line parents:** Francijna van der Horst's parents; Mechelina van den Berk's parents; parents of Johannes van den Elzen and Johannes Verwegen; Elisabeth van Boxtel's baptism/parents.

### US-side records
6. **Ship manifest** for the Nov/Dec 1950 Minnesota emigration (NARA T715 / Ancestry NY arrivals) — QE ~30 Nov 1950 is a working hypothesis; the 16 Nov 1950 list is ruled out.
7. **USCIS C-Files** (G-1041 → G-1041A) for Johan & Anna — resolves the ship, arrival date, and the Anna certificate-number discrepancy (7468860 vs 7468862).
8. **Minnesota Birth Index + St. Brendan's baptismal records** (Diocese of New Ulm) for the four MN-born children.
9. **Peter Van Duynhoven (Welaka FL, d. 2026)** — full obituary is 403-blocked; confirm whether he belongs to the NY branch (brother vs nephew — age 83 → b.~1943 conflicts with the "Peter" who predeceased Joseph in 2019).
10. **Living descendants** of all three branches (LinkedIn/Facebook/Ancestry login-gated); the ESPN athlete **Evan Van Dyn Hoven** confirms a living 5th generation in WI.

### Branches with zero records found
11. **Canadian branch** — none found; worth a targeted search (Ontario/Quebec Dutch-Catholic settlement, common post-WWII destination).
12. **Australian / international** — only **Larry Van Duynhoven** (film industry) surfaced; his branch is unconfirmed.
