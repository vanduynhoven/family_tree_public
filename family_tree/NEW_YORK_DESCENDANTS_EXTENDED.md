## Executive Summary of New Findings

This search did **not** locate a large new cohort of unknown descendants. What it produced is **higher-resolution detail on descendants already named in the base file**, plus **firm resolution of two open questions**:

1. **The 5 siblings question is now settled.** Joseph's own 2019 obituary states he was *"predeceased by siblings John, Peter, Jacoba, Engel and Jake."* So **all five siblings died before Nov 2019**. → The **Peter van Duynhoven, 83, of Welaka FL who died 20 Jun 2026** is therefore **NOT the brother Peter** (who was already dead by 2019). He is a **next-generation Peter** — almost certainly a nephew (son of one of the six Halfweg siblings). This confirms the age-discrepancy hypothesis flagged in the base file.
2. **The John & Joana link remains unconfirmed but consistent.** Joana's husband **John Van Duynhoven died 1986** — well before Joseph's 2019 obituary was written, so a 1986-deceased "John" is fully consistent with the "brother John (predeceased)" Joseph names. Same surname, same Warwick community, same parish (St. Stephen), same funeral home. **Still circumstantial** — no marriage/census record ties them — but nothing contradicts it, and the 1986 death date strengthens the identification.
