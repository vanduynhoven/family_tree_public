# Van Duijnhoven — Expanded Dutch Descendants & Sibling Research

**Compiled:** 2026-08-29
**Source:** Open Archieven public JSON API (`api.openarchieven.nl`), Brabants Historisch Informatie Centrum (BHIC) civil-registry index (Burgerlijke Stand).
**Method:** Birth (`BS Geboorte`) and marriage (`BS Huwelijk`) records resolved to full parent/child linkage via the `show.json` detail endpoint, then filtered by father + mother name to isolate each couple. Every entry below is backed by a record identifier (`openarchieven.nl/bhi:<id>`).

> **Privacy boundary:** Dutch civil-registration indexes are public only after **100 years for births** and **~75 years for marriages**. Living descendants (born after ~1926) do **not** appear in the open index. The branches below therefore terminate at the last publicly indexed generation (spouses of Johan's siblings, married 1930s–1940s). Their children require the CBG, municipal archives (Gemeente Uden/Boekel), or family contact.
