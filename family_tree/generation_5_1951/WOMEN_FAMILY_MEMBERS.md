# Women of the Van Duynhoven Family — Research Findings

**Research date:** 2026-08-28
**Method:** Public web search + web fetch (`web_search` / `web_fetch`)
**Subjects:** Three women found in FamilySearch "US Residence Database, 1970–2024" records
**Confidence legend:** ✅ Confirmed · 🟡 Probable lead · ⚪ Unverified / not found

> **Overall result:** None of the three could be firmly tied to Johan van Duynhoven's Green Isle, MN line through public sources. The main public obituary aggregator (legacy.com) is behind a 403 wall and could not be fetched, and generic name searches surfaced mostly unrelated public figures. One person — **Kathryn Van Duynhoven** — was located as a current Minnesota (Hennepin County) resident with a verifiable employer. The others remain unresolved without a login-gated source (FamilySearch tree, Ancestry) or an obituary text.

## Assessment

- **Kathryn** is the one plausible living Minnesota family member found, but the connection is inferential (surname + Hennepin County), not sourced.
- **Patricia** and **Leslie** could not be located in any public, fetchable source. Because the FamilySearch "US Residence Database, 1970–2024" is itself the origin of these names, the residence details almost certainly live **inside the (login-gated) FamilySearch record set** rather than the open web.

## Recommended next steps

1. **Open the three FamilySearch residence records directly** (logged in) and copy the city/county + any attached relationships — that is the authoritative source these names came from.
2. **Ancestry / MyHeritage** (login) — search each exact name restricted to Minnesota; residence and household records there often list co-residents (spouse/children), which would settle the relationship question.
3. **Obituaries** — retry Morris (d. 2014, Chaska) and any deceased Gen-5 sibling obituaries via a **non-legacy.com** funeral-home site (Kolden Funeral Home, Arlington/Chaska area, was reachable) to capture survivor lists naming wives and daughters.
4. **Diocese of New Ulm** holds St. Brendan's (Green Isle) sacramental records — marriage/baptism entries would name spouses and Gen-6 children directly.
