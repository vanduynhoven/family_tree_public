# Van Duynhoven — Spouse Family Lines Database

All surnames that married **into** the Van Duijnhoven / Van Duynhoven line, traced
across five generations. Sourced from the Open Archieven / BHIC (Brabants Historisch
Informatie Centrum) civil-registry index via its public JSON API. Each fact below is
backed by a specific record; the record UUID resolves at
`https://www.openarchieven.nl/bhi:<uuid>`.

Dutch name spelling **van Duijnhoven** (ij) is used throughout the civil registry;
the US branch uses **van Duynhoven** (y). Search both.

Generations (relative to Arthur):
- **G1** grandparents: Johan van Duijnhoven × Anna Maria Cornelissen
- **G2** great-grandparents: Marianus van Duijnhoven × Anna Maria van den Elzen
- **G3** g-g-grandparents: Martinus van Duijnhoven × Geertruij Verwegen
- **G4** g-g-g-grandparents: Petrus van Duijnhoven × Joanna van der Heijden
- **G5** deepest confirmed: Petrus (Joannis) van Duijnhoven × Elisabeth van Boxtel

## Interconnections between the families

- **Van der Heijden ↔ Van Boxtel converge at G4/G5.** The Petrus who married Joanna
  van der Heijden (1821) is himself the son of a Petrus × Elisabeth van Boxtel union.
  So the Van der Heijden and Van Boxtel lines meet one generation apart on the direct
  Van Duijnhoven male line — Van Boxtel is the grandmother's surname of the 1821 groom.
- **All three "core" marriage records name both sets of parents**, giving a clean,
  cross-verified four-generation spine:
  Petrus × van der Heijden (1821) → Martinus × Verwegen (1858) → Marianus × van den
  Elzen (1906) → Johan × Cornelissen (1950).
- **Geographic clustering:** Uden (van Duijnhoven, Verwegen, van der Heijden, van
  Boxtel, Pittens, Timmers) vs Boekel (van den Elzen, van der Horst) vs Bakel en
  Milheeze (Cornelissen, Verbruggen, Verhoeven, van Grootel). The 1906 Boekel
  marriage is where the Uden and Boekel clusters join; the 1950 Bakel marriage is
  where the Boekel-descended groom's line joins the Bakel cluster.
- **Recurring given names** (naming-pattern signal, useful for disambiguating
  collateral lines): *Anna Maria* recurs in van den Elzen, van der Horst, Cornelissen,
  Verbruggen, and Pittens lines; *Adriana/Adrianus* recurs across Verwegen; the
  Van Duijnhoven line reused *Petrus* father→son at G4/G5.

## Method note

Open Archieven search API: `place=` is not a hard filter (it OR-expands), but
`eventplace=` + `sourcetype=BS%20Huwelijk` narrows correctly. Full parent/witness
relation sets come only from the detail endpoint
`records/show.json?archive=bhi&identifier=<uuid>&lang=nl` (A2A `a2a_Person` +
`a2a_RelationEP`), not from the search index (which returns one person per hit).
