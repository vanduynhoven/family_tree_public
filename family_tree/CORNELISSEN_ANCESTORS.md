# Cornelissen Ancestor Line

BHIC (Brabants Historisch Informatie Centrum) public API. Every civil-registry event for
this family occurred in the municipality of **Bakel en Milheeze** (Noord-Brabant), which
covers the villages of Bakel and Milheeze.

## Research gaps & next steps

1. **Missing siblings of Anna Maria Cornelissen.** The Open Archieven indexed birth search
   for Bakel en Milheeze surfaced only two children of Wilhelmus × Anna Maria Verbruggen
   (Johanna 1913, Anna Maria 1916). A couple married in 1905 almost certainly had more
   children (typical span c. 1906–1922). The index appears incomplete/deduplicated for this
   window. **Next:** browse the Bakel en Milheeze BS Geboorte registers 1906–1922 directly
   (scans at BHIC / familysearch), or query the parents as a couple in the BHIC person
   database rather than the aggregated API.

2. **Exact birth dates/years for Wilhelmus Cornelissen and Anna Maria Verbruggen.** Both were
   born in Bakel en Milheeze before 1905 but their birth records were not pinpointed here.
   Working back from a marriage age (~20–25 in 1905) suggests Wilhelmus b. ~1876–1882 and
   Anna Maria Verbruggen b. ~1878–1884. **Next:** search BS Geboorte Bakel en Milheeze
   ~1875–1885 filtering on the confirmed grandparent names.

3. **Grandparents' vital records (Generation 3).** Only Petronella van Grootel's death
   (28 Oct 1938) is firmly located. Peter Cornelissen, Johanna Verhoeven, and Martinus
   Verbruggen still need their birth/marriage/death records isolated — several same-name
   individuals in the register require disambiguation via the confirmed spousal links.

4. **FamilySearch IDs** are known only for Generations 1–2 (QL7P-S2G3 / QL7P-S2GH /
   QL7P-S2GC). Generation-3 individuals have no captured FS IDs yet; the FamilySearch
   collaborative tree may already link them from the three known profiles.

5. **Name-spelling variants to search:** Cornelissen / Cornelisse; Verbruggen; van Grootel /
   van Grootel / Grootel; Verhoeven / Verhoven. The BHIC (archive code `bhi`) and
   Gemeentearchief Gemert-Bakel (`gmb`) hold overlapping indexes of the same civil registers.
